# A wide-mesh watch test sometimes sees no notification wake

## What was measured

The case was reproduced and instrumented, then the instrumentation was removed. Logs are `tickets/.logs/wide-watch-diag*.log` and `tickets/.logs/wide-watch-candidate*.log`. They are git-ignored and pruned automatically.

Command, from `packages/db-p2p`:

```
OPTIMYSTIC_INTEGRATION=1 node --import ./register.mjs node_modules/mocha/bin/mocha.js test/substrate-real-libp2p.integration.spec.ts --grep "mesh wider than one storage group" --reporter dot
```

Each run of the case on its own takes 6 to 20 s, so loop it rather than running the whole file.

Two facts combine to make the test flaky. Neither one is a fault in the push path.

**1. The watcher can store the collection's data blocks.** The test picks a watcher outside the *log tail's* storage group. That says nothing about the tree's leaf block, which is placed by its own id. In a mesh of six with `clusterSize` 3, the watcher was in the leaf's cohort in about half the runs. There, every commit lands the leaf on the watcher during the commit's sweep. Its watch service's local feed (`onLocalChange` in `packages/db-p2p/src/reactivity/collection-watch.ts`) then wakes the watch 50 to 130 ms after the commit, before `tree.replace` returns. It also raises `lastSeenRevision`, so the notification that arrives next wakes nothing (`wakeIfNewer`).

**2. The first commit after the registration reaches the announcing member is often not announced.** In 8 of 16 runs of the case, attempt 0's notification never reached the watcher. The next commit's notification did, 3 to 13 s later. Three ways it was lost were seen:

- (a) The announcing member dropped its own notification as `untrusted` (4 runs). That member verifies the notification against the root group's membership certificate, and the group publishes that certificate only on the gossip-cadence pump after its engine forms (`publishMembership` in `packages/db-p2p/src/cohort-topic/host.ts`, every 5 s).
- (b) The announcing member's forwarder had no forwarding state for the root, because `hasDemand` found no subscriber (1 run). This happened although the test had waited for the registration to reach that member. The test waits on `writerHost.registry.findServing(topicId, 0)`. The forwarder looks the engine up by coordinate instead: `host.registry.findByCoord(reactivityRootCoord(rootTail))`, which must be root-placed (`directSubscribers` in `packages/db-p2p/src/libp2p-node-base.ts`). The two lookups can disagree. This is a test synchronisation fault.
- (c) No root-group member retained a full commit certificate for the revision, so nobody announced it (1 run). The cause was not isolated, and it is filed separately as backlog `bug-a-commit-can-go-unannounced-with-no-group-member-holding-its-certificate`.
- In the other 2 runs the stage was not recorded.

**How the two combine.** The attempt loop counts an attempt as a wake with no tail read in between and a frame delivered at that moment. When the watcher stores the leaf, each attempt's wait ends at once on the local wake with no frame yet, and the loop moves straight on. All four attempts then finish within about half a second, inside the window where (a) or (b) drop the notification, and the test fails. When the watcher does not store the leaf, a lost notification makes its attempt wait the full 10 s, which outlasts the window. That is why the failure is intermittent.

**The attempt after a lost notification is also disqualified, correctly.** The next notification arrives with a revision gap. The watcher's subscription manager tries a backfill, which finds nothing, so it wakes and re-reads the tail (`wakeThenReAnchor` in `buildManager`). That read is a tail read between the commit and the wake, so the attempt does not count. In every one of the 8 runs above, attempt 1 was disqualified this way and attempt 2 counted.

## The change

All of it is in the test, plus one docs paragraph. No product code changes.

- **Wait for the frame, not only the wake.** An attempt waits for `wakes > wakesBeforeCommit && delivered > deliveredBeforeCommit`, then counts if `reads.started === readsBeforeCommit`. This is exactly what the sibling describe ("every machine in every cohort") already does. A lost notification then costs its attempt the 10 s timeout, which outlasts the window, instead of ending it at once. Measured with this change alone: 16 of 16 runs passed, in 6 s when attempt 0 counted and in 15 to 17 s when it was lost.
- **Wait on the product's lookup.** Replace the replication wait's `writerHost.registry.findServing(topicId, 0)` with the lookup the forwarder host uses: `findByCoord(reactivityRootCoord(reactivityTailBytes(tail!.tailId)))`, requiring `engine.rootPlaced`. That closes arm (b). Check that `findByCoord` takes the coordinate in the form `reactivityRootCoord` returns, the way `libp2p-node-base.ts` calls it.
- **Raise the attempt budget from 4 to 6.** A lost notification spends two attempts: its own, by timeout, and the next one, disqualified by the gap-recovery read. Four attempts leave room for only one such loss, and arms (a) and (c) remain. Six cost at most another 20 s inside the describe's 480 s timeout.
- **Rewrite the comments to match.** The describe's header and the comments around the attempt loop should say:
  - The watcher is outside the log tail's storage group, but it may store the collection's other blocks. Its own storage can therefore wake it before the notification lands, and a delivered frame is what shows the notify socket carried the commit, as in the sibling describe.
  - The first commit after a registration can go unannounced, for the reasons in the docs paragraph below. Its attempt times out, and the next one is disqualified by the gap-recovery read.

  The header's sentence "The case passes only on a wake that a delivered notification accounts for" is no longer true and must be reworded.
- **Docs.** Extend the "A commit right after a registration can reach nobody" paragraph in `docs/reactivity.md` with the second cause, which is not documented anywhere yet. The announcing member verifies its own notification against the root group's membership certificate. A newly formed root group publishes that certificate on its next gossip-cadence pump (`publishMembership` in `packages/db-p2p/src/cohort-topic/host.ts`), so a commit before then is dropped as untrusted. As with the documented cause, the watcher is woken by the next notification's gap recovery or by the next tick. Record it as a tripwire, using the paragraph's own form: if the window ever matters, publish the certificate when a root-placed engine forms rather than on the next pump. Say what was measured: the next commit 3 to 13 s later was announced, and no tighter bound was taken. Keep citations in house style (`yarn lint:docs`).

Do not make the test pick a watcher that stores none of the collection's blocks. On six machines no such node exists in a fair share of runs, because the tail and leaf cohorts can between them cover the whole mesh. The test is about the notify socket reaching a node outside the tail's group, and a delivered frame shows that whichever path woke the watch first.
