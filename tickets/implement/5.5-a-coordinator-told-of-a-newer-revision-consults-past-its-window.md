# A coordinator told of a newer revision consults its cohort past its window

## What is wrong

`CoordinatorRepo.get` decides per present block whether to consult the cohort with `shouldReadRepair`, which in the default `lazy` mode is a pure time test against `lastSeenCommitMs`: consult only when the block was not marked freshness-checked inside the last `readRepairWindowMs`. The stamp means "this copy matched the cohort at some past moment". It knows nothing about what the *asker* has since learned.

The prerequisite ticket makes the asker say it: `BlockGets.floors` now reaches the coordinator with, per block, the lowest revision the asker can accept. Today the coordinator ignores it. Two consumers pay for that:

- A client refreshing after a refused write asks a lagging coordinator for the log tail with the confirmed revision as floor; the coordinator answers from its window and the client has to spend a second round on another coordinator, which may also be inside its window.
- A collection that walked a log entry saying revision 7 changed block X re-reads X with floor 7 from a coordinator holding 6, stamped 2 s ago (the trace in `refreshed-collection-caches-a-block-older-than-its-log-entry`). It gets 6, retries elsewhere, and the lagging replica is fixed only when its window expires.

## What to build

### The floor is a stale trigger

In `get`, for a present block, compute `isStale = !isMissing && (this.shouldReadRepair(blockId) || this.floorDemandsConsult(blockId, floor, localRev))`, where the new predicate is true exactly when a floor was sent for the block, the block's local `state.latest.rev` is below it, and the floor is above the floor this node last consulted under for the block (next section). The rest of the existing path runs unchanged: `fetchBlockFromCluster` corroborates, `restoreCorroborated` promotes or acquires, the refreshed local answer replaces the entry, `recordAheadClaim` and `flagUnconfirmedCurrency` run. The coordinator never refuses: a copy still below the floor after the pass is served exactly as today, labelled with its `materialized` revision, and the asker's own check and retry handle it. A floor changes *when* this node asks its cohort, never what it says about the answer.

Log the trigger: extend `cluster-tx:read-repair-triggered` with `floor` and a `reason` of `'window'` or `'floor'` (both, when both hold, report `'floor'`; the window would have consulted anyway). `docs/debugging.md` § "was the read-repair window armed?" gains the new reason.

`readRepairMode: 'off'` stays off: it is an operator's decision to never consult for present blocks, and the asker still gets the transactor's second-chance round. `'paranoid'` already consults on every read. Say so at the predicate.

### The floor consulted under is remembered with the stamp

Without memory, a floor no machine can meet (a log entry whose blocks never landed, `bug-a-refused-write-can-leave-its-log-entry-behind`) would make every read of that block consult the cohort, since the asker keeps sending the floor for as long as its own answer stays below it. Widen the stamp: `lastSeenCommitMs: LruMap<string, number>` becomes `LruMap<string, FreshnessStamp>` with `FreshnessStamp = { at: number; floorConsulted?: number }`, and `markBlocksSeen(blockIds, floor?)` records the floor the pass ran under. The predicate above reads `floorConsulted` and consults only for a floor strictly above it. A stamp written without a floor (a commit, or a pass that was not asked one) clears the memory, which is right: a commit moves `latest`, and a floor above the new `latest` is new information.

Record the floor at exactly the exits that already arm the window, and nowhere else. Every one of those exits arms under the stated rule "re-asking sooner than one window could not teach this node anything", and that is the same rule that says re-asking under the same floor could not either: the solo-self exit, the `cohort-too-small` decline, the `local-current` exit, and the post-restore exit. The exits that deliberately do not arm (empty cohort, a transient decline, a thrown consult) do not record the floor either, so the next floored read consults again; that is the same per-read cost those exits already impose once a window has expired, and it is bounded by the asker sending the floor only for blocks it actually re-reads. Thread the floor into `fetchBlockFromCluster` as a fourth parameter for this. `ageMs`, `shouldReadRepair` and `soloAbsenceNamedThisWindow` read `.at`; `setLastSeenForTest(blockId, ts)` keeps its signature and writes `{ at: ts }`.

This widens the stamp that `backlog/debt-freshness-state-scattered-across-coordinator-repo` names as the reason to consolidate first. That ticket is not promoted and its own tradeoffs say a maintainer may leave the file alone; blocking this fix on it was weighed and rejected. Keep the new field on the one existing map rather than adding a fifth `LruMap`, and append a short measurement note to that ticket saying what this ticket added and where (the record type, the one predicate, the parameter on `fetchBlockFromCluster`), so the consolidation inherits it as one named fact.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **Floor met after the pass** (converged onto the corroborated revision): `latest` is now at or above the floor, the predicate is false on the next read whatever the stamp says. Pinned by the first test below.
- **Floor unmet after the pass** (cohort corroborates at or below what this node holds, or acquisition failed): the arming exit records the floor; the next read inside the window with the same floor does not consult; a higher floor does. Pinned by the first test below.
- **Transient decline** (a silent peer): no stamp, no floor recorded; the next floored read consults again. Bounded per the argument above; record a `NOTE:` at the predicate naming the bound and the remedy if it ever shows in a profile (record the floor at the transient exit too, with the window as the retry cadence).
- **Block missing locally**: consults on every read already; the floor is irrelevant and must not change the absence path. Inspection.
- **Solo cohort under an unmeetable floor**: the solo exit arms with the floor recorded, so a one-machine deployment holding an abandoned entry's floor consults once per window per block, not once per read. Pin with a case in `coordinator-repo-solo-read-repair-window.spec.ts` or the first test below.
- **A pinned read below the floor**: the asker never sends it (`BlockFloors.applicableTo`). Inspection.
- **Two concurrent floored reads of one block** both pass the predicate before either stamps: two consults, as two window-expired reads race today. Inspection; not new.
- **LRU eviction of the stamp** loses the recorded floor; the next floored read consults once and re-records it. Bounded; matches the eviction `NOTE:` on `unsettledAheadClaims`.
- **A commit through this coordinator between two floored reads** rewrites the stamp without a floor; the next read consults only if the floor is still above the new `latest`. Inspection.
- **`skipClusterFetch` sync reads** never carry floors (the sync protocol does not use `BlockGets`). Inspection.
- **Freshness and doubt stay separate**: the floor never touches `unsettledAheadClaims` or `flagUnconfirmedCurrency`; a floored read served below an unsettled claim still carries `unconfirmedAheadRev`. Inspection against `coordinator-repo-small-cohort-arming.spec.ts`, which must keep passing.
- **An asker on an older build** sends no floors; the predicate is false; nothing changes. Verified by every existing coordinator spec.

## Tests

- `packages/db-p2p/test/coordinator-repo-read-repair.spec.ts`, one describe: a block present at local revision 6, `setLastSeenForTest` stamped fresh, cohort holding 7. A read carrying floor 7 consults and returns 7 (the acceptance test the plan named). Then, with the cohort *also* at 6, a read carrying floor 7 consults once, and a second identical read inside the window does not consult, while a read carrying floor 8 does. Assert consults by counting `clusterLatestCallback` invocations, as the file already does.
- A mesh case, modelled on `packages/db-p2p/test/superseded-own-write-is-saved.spec.ts` with its `missTheNextDataCommit` lag injector and the write harness in `concurrent-diary-append-acknowledgement.spec.ts`: two members; member B misses a commit that member A holds; a handle coordinating its reads through B writes, is refused by A with `staleAt` at A's revision, and its retry lands. Assert the write's durability is reported, no `SyncRevisionStalledError` escapes, and B's storage holds A's revision of the log tail afterwards (the storage-side half: the lagging replica was fixed at the moment of the read, not at window expiry). This is the downstream shape (`sereus`, one case in ten) end to end.
- Nothing else. The plumbing of the fourth parameter and the stamp record is checked by the compiler and by the existing suite.
