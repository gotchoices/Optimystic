## TODO

- **Call-site discriminator.** Give `advanceContext` a `site: 'refresh' | 'attach'` parameter and
  print `site=` on its lines. `updateInternal` passes `'refresh'` (this instance's copy vs the
  stored log — indicts a forked replica); `attachToLog` passes `'attach'` (the tail block's claim
  vs a walk of its own chain — indicts storage). The class doc at :199-210 already explains the
  two meanings; the log line must carry it.
- **Earliest divergent revision.** Instead of comparing only at `current.rev`, scan the two
  committed lists for the LOWEST revision present in both where the action ids differ. When one
  exists, print `forkRev=` with `held=`/`read=` being the action ids AT forkRev, plus
  `heldRev=`/`readRev=` for the two contexts' own `rev` values. This widens detection (today a
  fork below the current rev is silent). Both lists are short (they grow one entry per commit
  between context reads and truncate at checkpoints) and the whole comparison stays gated on
  `log.enabled`, so cost is nil on a normal run — keep the gate.
- **Action ids on `context-not-lowered`.** Add `heldAction=`/`readAction=` — `actionIdAt(current,
  current.rev)` and `actionIdAt(next, next.rev)`, with a `none` placeholder for undefined
  (`actionIdAt` legitimately resolves undefined; see its doc). This is what distinguishes "a fork
  just got sealed" from "this node is simply behind". Keep the `actionIdAt` lookups inside a
  `log.enabled` guard but do NOT let the guard skip the early `return` that refuses to lower.

  `attachToLog` and passed both into it and into the constructor. If the prereq ticket generated
  the tag inside the constructor instead, hoist it.
