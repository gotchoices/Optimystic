Third of three tickets for stage 9 of [docs/offline-sync.md §Implementation stages](../../docs/offline-sync.md#implementation-stages). After the first two, a queued transaction's outlook rests on facts recorded while its statements ran: which staged elements carry a fresh value, and which decisions each statement made against other rows. Those facts exist only in memory. A restart puts the journaled transactions back and judges them again ([§Restore after a restart](../../docs/offline-sync.md#restore-after-a-restart)), and without the facts every insert under `StampId()` comes back **exposed** and every `unchecked` comes back `'not-computed'`: an application that showed "syncing" before the relaunch shows "may not go through" after it, for the same rows. The doc's sentence "Nothing of it is journaled" was written for stage 7, when the judgement needed nothing beyond the segments; this ticket journals the facts and corrects it.

## Design

**One optional field on the plugin's entry record.** `EntryRecordV1` gains `outlook?: JournaledFacts`, JSON-safe:

```typescript
type JournaledFacts = {
  readonly fresh: readonly (readonly [CollectionId, readonly string[]])[];   // EntryFacts.fresh as pairs
  readonly unchecked: readonly { table: QualifiedName; kind; scope }[];        // rows dropped, see below
  readonly complete: boolean;
};
```

The record version stays 1: a reader that finds no `outlook` field (a record written by the previous build) restores the entry without facts, exactly as it does today, and nothing else about the record changes. `encodeFacts` and `decodeFacts` live in write-description.ts beside `foldWriteFacts`, so the two shapes stay in one file.

**`rows` are not journaled.** A row-scoped read's bound values are `SqlValue`s, and a bigint or a blob among them is not JSON-representable under the journal's `JSON.stringify`. Rather than invent an encoding for them, a restored read is widened to its table (matched by table, as a `group` read is) and keeps its reported scope. That is the conservative direction, costs one extra dependency at most, and only until the earlier entry settles. Record the choice as a `NOTE:` at `encodeFacts` with the revisit condition: if restored outlooks ever list dependencies an application cannot act on, journal the encoded main-tree keys instead (the controller can encode them at enqueue, since it has every published table's codec).

**How the facts reach the journal.** `OfflineJournal` already takes callbacks for what db-core does not know (`tablesOf`, `outcomeOf`); add `factsOf: (transactionId) => EntryFacts | undefined`, which the controller answers from `entryFacts` — set before `queue.enqueue` by the first ticket's rule, so it is there when `IOfflineJournal.queued` runs inside the enqueue.

**How they come back.** `OfflineRestore.restoreOne` decodes `entry.outlook` and hands it to the controller (`host.factsRestored(transactionId, facts)`, a new member of the restore's host interface) **before** `queue.restore`, whose `queued` status is what judges the entry. A deferred record keeps nothing in the controller; the next pass hands the facts again. `unregisterRestored` and the terminal-status cleanup drop them as they drop a judgement.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **A record without the field** (written before this ticket): restored without facts; judged by tags alone, `unchecked: 'not-computed'`. Verified by the existing restore specs continuing to pass over fixtures that lack it, plus one assertion in the new test that an entry record stripped of `outlook` restores exposed.
- **Facts present, the entry's drive record says it is in doubt**: facts apply the same; the outlook describes the entry, not its attempt. By inspection.
- **A restored entry ahead of entries committed before `hydrate`** (`offline:outlook-regressed`): unchanged; the restored entry's own outlook is now as good as the original. By inspection.
- **A journal that refuses the entry record**: the enqueue rejects and the controller deletes the pending facts (first ticket's rule). By inspection.
- **A row-scoped read restored as table-wide**: the `dependsOn` may be wider than before the restart; never narrower. Verified by the new test's second case (a key-matched read restored lists the table's other writer too).
- **Two labels over one store**: facts are inside the entry record, so they follow the label. By inspection.

## Tests

- `test/offline-journal.spec.ts`: the entry record of an offline commit under `StampId()` carries `outlook.fresh` naming the main-tree key and `outlook.complete: true`; a commit with an FK decision carries it in `outlook.unchecked`.
- `test/offline-restore.spec.ts`: a crash after the local acknowledgement with a conversation under a natural key and a message under `StampId()` queued; after restart the restored `queued` statuses carry the conversation exposed, the message assured with `dependsOn` `via: 'unchecked'` and `unchecked` listing the foreign key; and the same store with the `outlook` field removed from the records restores the message exposed with `'not-computed'`.
