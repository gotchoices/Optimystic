# A decided promise map is never extended

Absorbs backlog `bug-a-late-promise-invalidates-the-commit-signatures-already-collected` (deleted; its analysis is restated here). First half of the work the maintainer accepted on 2026-11-17 for GitHub #64; the second half, `a-consensus-round-stops-waiting-once-its-outcome-is-decided`, depends on this one.

## Background

A cohort member's commit vote is a signature over `computeClusterCommitHash(messageHash, message, promises, membershipDigest)`, where `promises` is the record's whole promise map. Every member verifies every commit signature against the record it holds (`ClusterMember.validateSignatures`), and a `BlockCommitProof` projected from a member's record carries that same map. So once anyone has signed a commit, the promise map is fixed: adding a promise to it invalidates every commit signature on the record.

Today two places can add a promise to a map that is already decided — one whose approved promises already reach super-majority (`ceil(peerCount × superMajorityThreshold)`):

- **A late member.** Member D was silent for the promise round (its delivery failed) and is reachable for the commit round. D's phase loop sees no promise of its own, runs `OurPromiseNeeded`, adds its promise, then — approvals still at super-majority — runs `OurCommitNeeded` and signs over the map *with* its promise. The coordinator merges D's commit into a record without D's promise (`mergeCommits` takes `commits` only). The consensus broadcast then fails every member's signature check (`Invalid commit signature from D`, penalising an honest D with `InvalidSignature`), nobody applies, and the retry timer re-sends the same broken record. Reachable at the default threshold from four members up (super-majority 3 of 4), which is exactly where the follow-up ticket makes it routine: a round that stops waiting for a straggler leaves that straggler to answer later.
- **A member's held state.** A member that answered the promise round (its promise sits in its `activeTransactions` record) but whose answer the coordinator did not merge, then receives a decided record without its promise: `mergeRecords` unions the two maps, and the member signs over the union. The same breakage, reached through the merge rather than the phase loop.

Both are fixed by one rule, stated for the member and mirrored at the coordinator. The signed-hash layout does not change: changing what `computeClusterCommitHash` covers would break verification of every stored proof and of mixed-version cohorts.

## The rule

**A record is decided when its approved promises reach super-majority. A decided record's promise map is never extended, and a commit signature is placed only in a record whose promise map is exactly the one it was signed over.**

### Member side (`ClusterMember`)

- **`getTransactionPhase`:** after the `Rejected` and `ConflictSuperseded` checks (unchanged, still first), a decided record whose commits lack this member's goes to `OurCommitNeeded` whether or not this member has a promise on it. Only an undecided record without this member's promise goes to `OurPromiseNeeded` (or the conflict vote) as today. The commit vote was already blind to this member's own promise ("the rest of the cohort reaching super-majority is the commit rule", Theorem 1 Case 2), so the member signs exactly what it signed before, minus the promise that poisoned the map. Note the expiry branch: today a late member on an expired decided record adds an expiry reject and then still signs the commit; it now just signs the commit, the same outcome.
- **`mergeRecords`:** choose the promise map by which side is decided.
  - Held record decided: keep the held map; promises the incoming record adds are not taken. Incoming commit signatures are taken only when the incoming record's promise map equals the held one (`canonicalJson`, the comparison `keepCommitsSignedOverMergedPromises` already uses); otherwise they are dropped and logged (`cluster-member:commit-over-other-promise-map`, naming the peers dropped). First-seen wins because the held map is the one this member may already have signed over.
  - Held record undecided, incoming decided: adopt the incoming map, dropping the held record's extra promises (this member's own late one included). An undecided record carries no commit signature that verifies, so nothing of the held side is lost.
  - Neither decided: union, as today.
  - Equivocation detection (`detectEquivocation`) still runs over the peers both maps share, and apply outcomes merge as today.

The member decides "decided" with the threshold `assertSuperMajorityCoupling` already forces equal to the coordinator's, so both sides agree on when the map freezes.

### Coordinator side (`ClusterCoordinator`)

- **One merge rule for commit signatures.** Generalise `keepCommitsSignedOverMergedPromises` into the only way a commit signature enters `record.commits`: take the answering member's *own* entry (keyed by the peer asked, as `collectApplyOutcomes` does), and only when the answer's promise map equals `record.promises` exactly. Replace `mergeCommits` with it at every site — the promise round's keep, `presignLocalCommit`, the commit round in `commitTransaction`, and `runRetry`. A dropped signature is logged (`cluster-tx:commit-over-other-promise-map`). This is the guard for a member on an older build, which still adds its late promise: its commit is dropped instead of poisoning the broadcast, and the member stays owed the record like any member that did not commit.
- **`presignLocalCommit` stops merging promises.** Its comment explains it merged this node's promise for a member whose prevote failed; under the member rule that member no longer adds one to a decided record (and the record is decided whenever the presign runs, since the promise round succeeded). Merge its commit through the rule above.
- **`collectPromises`:** the keep no longer needs its "this node's promise is already in the map" condition (it existed so the presign would add nothing after the keep); run it unconditionally, and update the method's comment.
- The promise round itself is unchanged here (`Promise.all` over every member); the follow-up ticket changes when it resolves.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **Two-member cohort (the ordinary write path).** The remote member receives an undecided record (one approval of two), adds its promise, which decides it, then signs. Unchanged. `packages/quereus-plugin-optimystic/test/two-node-write-request-count.spec.ts` must still count 3 requests. Verified by that test.
- **Cohort of three.** Every member must promise; a decided record always carries every member's promise, so the member rule never fires. Verified by inspection.
- **Late member that conflict- or held-voted earlier.** Its held record is either absent (a conflict vote clears it) or undecided; a decided incoming record is adopted and the member signs the commit without a conflict vote. Quorum intersection, not the reservation, is what keeps the rival out (Theorem 9). Verified by inspection; say so in the `getTransactionPhase` comment.
- **A record already at super-majority on first delivery** (the case the existing NOTE in `getTransactionPhase` describes). Now skips the promise entirely; the NOTE's reasoning (quorum intersection) still holds. Update the NOTE.
- **Two coordinators for one `messageHash`** (an identical message routed twice). Each could decide a different map; members keep the first decided map they see and drop commits signed over the other. Verified by inspection; record as a `NOTE:` at the merge, since today's behaviour in that case is no better.
- **Mixed versions.** Older member, newer coordinator: the coordinator's equal-map rule drops the older late member's commit; the older member's own stored proof may carry a mismatched map (its own loss). Newer member, older coordinator: the older coordinator merges every commit, and a newer late member's commit is over the decided map, so the backlog defect is closed even before coordinators upgrade. Verified by inspection; state both in `docs/internals.md`.
- **Proofs.** `buildBlockCommitProof` projects from the member's merged record; with the held map kept consistent, every commit on it verifies against the proof's promise map. Verified by the mesh test below reaching consensus at the broadcast (members build the proof on apply).
- **Disputes, slot holds, stale-loss counting.** A member that skips its promise skips `noteStaleLoss` and pend validation for that record; neither matters for a decided record (it is not a loss, and its content was checked by the super-majority that decided it — the documented content-check residual). Verified by inspection.
