## Partial commits

`commitEntry` (ticket `offline-sync-coordinator-entry-commit`) throws `CoordinatorPartialCommitError` once some participants are durably committed and the rest were refused for good or by a hard failure; by then it has consumed the committed participants' head segments and left the others sealed at their heads with their attempts retained. The queue reacts:

- **Status** `partially-committed`, added to `EntryStatus`: `{ kind: 'partially-committed'; entryId; committedCollections; pendingCollections; cause: Error }`, where `committedCollections` is `error.committedCollections` accumulated across submissions, `pendingCollections` the collections in which the entry is still a sealed segment, and `cause` the error's `reason` (or the error itself when it has none).
- **The entry stays at the head and in doubt** (its remaining attempts may have stored a tail), with its `committedCollections` and the partial's `durability` recorded on it. `discard` refuses it as it refuses any sent entry.
- **The next submission drives only the remaining collections**: those in which `entryId` is still the head segment (`sealedEntries`). One remaining collection goes through `commitHead`, several through `commitEntry`, under the same action id, with `resume` holding the retained attempts of exactly those collections.
- **Success of the remainder** settles the entry `committed`, with a `TransactionDurability` merged from the partial's committed half and the remainder's answer (`recordCollectionDurability` per collection, `transactionDurability` over the map) — every collection the entry wrote, each with its class.
- **A permanent refusal of the remainder** (`guard-refused`, `rejected`, `too-large`) fails the entry with `committedCollections` set on the `failed` status, drops its remaining segments and cascades exactly as any failure does; `changedCollections` names only the collections whose segments were dropped. Expiry never applies (the entry is in doubt). A second partial on the remainder accumulates `committedCollections` and stays `partially-committed`.
- **`queued` and `QueuedEntry`** carry `committedCollections` once non-empty, so `entries()` shows a partly landed entry between submissions.

## The mesh scenario

`packages/db-p2p/test/offline-queue-mesh.spec.ts`, over `createMesh(2, …)` and `buildNetworkTransactor` from `packages/db-p2p/src/testing/mesh-harness.ts`, with an `OfflineQueue` on node A's transactor over two trees (a "table" and its "index", two collection ids) and no reachability port (always reachable):

1. One entry over both trees drains and lands on node B: a handle over B's transactor reads both rows, and both logs hold one entry under the entry's action id — the batched `pendMany`/`commitMany` path the `TestTransactor` never takes.
2. A rival on B commits the same key to the "table" tree before A drains: the entry fails with `constraint`, and B holds nothing of it under the action id in either collection (a refusal at pend of one collection of a multi-collection entry leaves nothing durable, now on real cohorts).

Two cases, no more; everything else is pinned on `TestTransactor` already.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **Partial, then the remainder commits**: a double that commits tree A and refuses tree B's commit permanently on the first submission (the `CoordinatorPartialCommitError` shape the slice ticket's test produced), then behaves: `partially-committed` with A committed and B pending, then `committed` with a durability naming both; each log holds the entry once. **Test** in `offline-queue.spec.ts`.
- **Partial, then the remainder refused for good**: same start, then a rival takes B's key: `failed` with `reason: 'constraint'`, `committedCollections: [A]`, `changedCollections: [B]`; A's row stays (it is committed), B's is gone locally; a later entry that updated B's row fails with `causedBy`. **Test.**
- **`discard` of a partially-committed entry** throws `EntryNotDiscardableError`. One assertion inside the first test.
- **Two partials in a row** on an entry over three collections accumulate `committedCollections`. By inspection; the accumulation is one `Set` union.
- **Durability merge when the partial's half has an unknown class**: `overall` absent, as `TransactionDurability` already specifies. By inspection.
- **A refusal reported for the remainder by a reader's refresh** between submissions fails the entry with `committedCollections` set, through the queue core's pending-refusal path. By inspection: `fail` reads the entry's `committedCollections` whatever the trigger.
- **The mesh spec's cost**: two nodes, two cases, well under a minute; it runs in the plain `yarn test` of db-p2p, not the integration tier, since the in-process mesh needs no sockets.
