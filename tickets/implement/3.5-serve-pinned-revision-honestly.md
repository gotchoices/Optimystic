## Original problem statement (for context)

`StorageRepo.get` honours `context.rev` (`blockStorage.getBlock(context?.rev)`) and reported the
materialized revision as a bare number, while `serveBlockArchive` built the archive from
`entry.state.latest` — the serving node's *newest* revision — so a rev-1 request was answered with
rev 1's bytes under rev 2's number and action id. The asker's `BlockStorage.saveRestored` writes
`saveMaterializedBlock(blockId, action.actionId, block)` keyed by action id, so its own good rev 2
was overwritten with rev 1's content, `meta.ranges` stayed `[[2,3]]`, and every later read of rev 1
repeated the fetch and the overwrite. Root cause: `GetBlockResult` had no field carrying the
`(rev, actionId)` of the content actually held, so the one site that needed it reached for
`state.latest`. Making that pair one first-class field is what makes the mislabel unrepresentable.

### Expected behaviour

- A peer asked for revision N serves revision N labelled N, or serves nothing. It never serves
  content from one revision under another revision's number or action id.
- A peer asked for no revision serves its latest, byte-identical to before.
- `SyncService`, the mesh harness, and both `libp2p-node-base.ts` latest-revision consults (which
  pass `rev: undefined`) behave identically.
- A restore that cannot be satisfied fails loudly at `ensureRevision` rather than writing a wrong
  answer into local storage.
