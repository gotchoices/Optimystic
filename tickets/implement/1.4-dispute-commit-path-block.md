# Block a split pend until a ruling

## Why

Today a pend whose promise round ends with a super-majority of approvals commits even when other members rejected it on validity; the record is merely flagged `disputed` (`executeTransaction`'s dispute-marking branch), and a pend with too few approvals fails outright with `ValidatorRejectionError` whichever side is right. The design of record (`docs/right-is-right.md` §The block, on the coordinator / §The block, on the members) replaces both with: any validity split blocks, both sides drive an escalation, and the ruling decides. This ticket is the consensus-layer half; `dispute-writer-sees-disputed-pend` carries the outcomes to the writer.

Everything here is behind `disputeEnabled` (`DisputeConfig`, default `false`). With it off, behaviour is byte-for-byte today's.

## Specification

**The port.** `ClusterCoordinator` and `ClusterMember` take an optional dispute port, e.g. `{ drive(record, side): void; awaitRuling(disputeId, deadline): Promise<Ruling | undefined>; verifyRuling(ruling, record): Promise<boolean>; disputeIdFor(record): Promise<string> }`, backed by `DisputeService`. Absent port ⇒ disputes off, regardless of config. One port object, so the two classes cannot disagree on whether disputes are on.

**Coordinator — `executeTransaction`**, after `collectPromises` and **before** the `rejectionCount > maxAllowedRejections` throw:

1. *Split* = at least one `approve` and at least one marked validity reject (`validityEvidence`, from `dispute-validity-reject-marker`). Unmarked rejects never split. No approvals at all ⇒ today's path (unanimous rejection fails fast).
2. *Notice*: deliver the merged record to every cohort member except self (reuse the promise-round delivery with a distinct log phase; failures are logged, not fatal).
3. *Drive*: `port.drive(record, 'approvers')`, not awaited — the dispute runs to its own deadline even after this transaction gives up.
4. *Wait*: `port.awaitRuling(disputeId, cutoff)` where `cutoff = record.message.expiration − disputeCommitMarginMs` (new consensus config, default 5 000 — enough for a commit round including one dial; document the choice). If `cutoff` is already past, do not wait.
5. *Outcome*:
   - `approvers-win` and `approvalCount >= superMajority`: set `record.ruling`, persist, `commitTransaction(record)`.
   - `approvers-win` below super-majority: `broadcastAbandonment` carrying the ruling; throw `DisputeSettledError` (retryable: the re-drive meets the cohort without the ejected rejecters).
   - `rejecters-win`: `broadcastAbandonment` carrying the ruling; throw `TransactionRuledInvalidError` (permanent).
   - no ruling by the cutoff: no broadcast (members drop the record at its expiration, which is moments away); throw `DisputeUnresolvedError` carrying the dispute id and `retryAfter` = the dispute's deadline.
   The three error classes live beside `ValidatorRejectionError` and carry the dispute id.
6. The existing `disputed` / `disputeEvidence` marking stays for the disputes-off path only (with disputes on, a split never reaches it).

**Member — `getTransactionPhase`** (disputes on):

- A record whose promises include a marked validity reject is **not** committable unless `record.ruling` is present, is for this record (dispute id matches), says `approvers-win`, and `port.verifyRuling` accepts it. Not committable means: the member does not sign a commit; the record stays in the member's reservation table until its expiration (it is not `Rejected` — it may yet be resumed).
- A record carrying a verified `rejecters-win` ruling is `Rejected` (leaves the reservation table), the same treatment as the proof-carrying abandonment of a validator rejection.
- An unverifiable ruling is ignored (treated as absent) and logged with the verifier's reason.

**Member — notice handling.** A member that receives a split record and is the **dissent coordinator** — the marked rejecter whose ring coordinate (the key network's hash of its peer id, the same coordinate FRET places it at) is nearest the hash of `record.message.coordinatingBlockIds[0]` — calls `port.drive(record, 'rejecters')`. Every member computes the same choice from the record alone; put the selection in one pure function both sides could call, and test it.

**Record field.** `ClusterRecord.ruling?` (db-core). It is outside `message`, so it is not part of the message hash; it is also not folded into the commit hash. NOTE (accepted tradeoff, at the field): a commit proof built from a resumed record does not attest that a ruling existed — every signer verified the ruling before signing and the ruling is retained by the dispute service; revisit if an offline verifier ever needs to tell a disputed commit from an undisputed one.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **The coordinator that skips the block.** A coordinator (hostile, or with disputes off) that runs the commit round on a split record without a ruling gets no commit signature from any disputes-on member — test this; it is the safety contract.
- **Omitted dissent.** A coordinator that leaves a marked reject out of the merged record cannot be stopped here; that is `dispute-post-commit-challenge`. By design.
- **Coordinator's own member rejects.** The pre-vote (`prevoteLocalPromise`) can itself be a marked reject; the split rule is the same (the coordinator is still the approvers' driver only if there is an approval; otherwise unanimous-or-no-approval path).
- **Reservations while blocked.** Approvers hold the record; a rival pend gets `conflict` (by inspection — `resolveRace` is unchanged, and a blocked record still has its approvals). No storage pending record exists for a blocked pend (the pend is never applied). Slot holds are unaffected.
- **Expiry during resumption.** The margin is what keeps the commit round inside the record's lifetime; a member that has already expired the record answers the commit as today. If the margin proves too small in practice, raise the default rather than extending the record.
- **Commit-phase consensus op.** Validation runs only on the pend's promise round; the later commit operation is not re-disputed (by inspection).
- **Mixed configuration.** A disputes-off coordinator with disputes-on members stalls a split record until expiry. Network policy says the flag is uniform; log `cluster-tx:split-without-dispute-port` at the coordinator when a split occurs with the port absent but a marked reject present, so the mismatch is visible.
- **Persistence.** `persistCoordinatorState` should record the `disputed-waiting` stage so `recoverTransactions` does not resume a blocked record's commit round after a restart without a ruling; after restart such a record is abandoned (it will expire).
- **Cohort of one / solo path** never runs consensus, so never splits (by inspection: `CoordinatorRepo`'s solo branch).

## Tests

Coordinator/member specs with an injected fake port (the port is the seam; the dispute service itself is tested in `dispute-escalation-loop`):
- Split + `approvers-win` before the cutoff → commits; the commit record carries the ruling.
- Split + `rejecters-win` → `TransactionRuledInvalidError`; no member applied the pend.
- Split + no ruling → `DisputeUnresolvedError` at the cutoff; nothing applied.
- A member refuses to sign commit for a record with a marked reject and no ruling (coordinator bypassing the block).
- Dissent-coordinator selection is deterministic and picks the nearest marked rejecter.
