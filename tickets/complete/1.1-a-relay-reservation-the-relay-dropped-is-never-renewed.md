# A relay reservation the relay dropped is never renewed — complete

Source: GitHub sereus#64. The relay reservation supervisor judged "held" only from the node's own advertised addresses, so a relay that forgot the reservation while the connection stayed open left the node unreachable (every relayed dial answered `NO_RESERVATION`) until the connection happened to drop.

## What landed

- `forceReservationRenewal` in `packages/db-p2p/src/network/relay-reservation.ts`: reads the held reservation through the store's `getReservation` (now part of `RelayReservationStoreLike` and required by `isReservationStore`), sets its `expire` to `0n` so libp2p's refresh-window check sends a fresh RESERVE over the open connection, and calls `addRelay(relay, 'discovered')`. Never throws. The `NOTE:` at `findCircuitRelayTransport` records the libp2p 4.2.13 facts this rests on.
- The supervisor (`RelayReservationLoop`) renews a held reservation every `renewMs` (default `DEFAULT_RELAY_RENEW_MS`, 5 min, jittered ±`RELAY_RENEW_JITTER`), serialized with drives, raced against `driveTimeoutMs` through the shared `raceDeadline`. A refused renewal (address withdrawn) drives again at once.
- `NodeOptions.relayReservation` passes supervisor timings through `createLibp2pNodeBase`.
- Docs (readme, `docs/optimystic.md`, `docs/architecture.md`, the `relay-reservation` row of `docs/debugging.md`) describe the renewal; the backlog ticket `debt-relay-supervisor-can-keep-the-configured-listen-address` carries the arm that a switch to `'configured'` reservations must renew as `'configured'` too.
- Tests: the seam spec pins that a plain `addRelay` never reaches a relay that forgot a held reservation and `forceReservationRenewal` does without withdrawing the address; the supervisor spec reproduces the outage end to end (first relayed dial `NO_RESERVATION`, reachable again within `renewMs` plus slack, address advertised at every sample).

## Review findings

Read the `ticket(implement): a-relay-reservation-the-relay-dropped-is-never-renewed` diff first, then checked it against `@libp2p/circuit-relay-v2` 4.2.13's `src/transport/reservation-store.ts` in `packages/db-p2p/node_modules`.

**Correctness (checked, no defect found).**
- libp2p facts in the `NOTE:` confirmed against the store source: the refresh-window early return, the in-place refresh for a connected reservation, slot id reuse for a live `discovered` entry, the old refresh timer cleared on replace, the `catch` removing the entry. `getReservation` returns the live protobuf `Reservation`, so the `expire` write lands on the object the window check reads; `expire` has no other reader on the client side.
- Loop interleavings: a `wake` during a renewal is ignored (`inFlight`), as during a drive; a wake in the microtask gap after `renewOnce` either completes synchronously (held → `onHeld`, after which the resumed tick's second `onHeld` only re-schedules, `schedule` clears the timer first) or starts a drive that sets `inFlight` synchronously, which the resumed tick's `if (this.stopped || this.inFlight) return` honours. No timer is pending while a renewal runs (both `tick` entry paths clear or consume it). `stop()` mid-renewal discards the outcome and arms nothing.
- Renewal clock: cleared at every drive start, armed in `onHeld` when unset, re-armed after every attempt; a stale clock left by an unobserved held→held transition can only renew early, which is harmless.
- `renewMs` clamp ≥ 1 and the jitter factor ≥ 0.9 keep the schedule positive.

**Error handling / resource cleanup.** `raceDeadline` clears its timer in `finally` and leaves the abandoned `addRelay` handled; `requestReservation`'s refactor onto it is behaviour-preserving. A renewal past its deadline leaves libp2p's refresh running with the address advertised — conditional, reasoned not tested; parked as a `NOTE:` tripwire in `RelayReservationLoop.renew` (the implementer had it only in the handoff).

**Type safety / API.** `RelayReservationSupervisorOptions` is not re-exported from the package index, so a host names it as `NodeOptions['relayReservation']`; acceptable, every field optional. No `any`.

**Docs.** Fixed inline: the `RelayReservationSupervisor.driving` doc said "a drive is in flight", but it is also true during a renewal; and the `stop()` doc now names a renewal among what it discards. Every doc the change touches was read and reflects the behaviour; `yarn lint:docs` clean.

**Tests.** The seam case pins the libp2p fact the fix depends on (fails loudly if an upgrade changes the refresh rule) and the supervisor case is the regression; both kept. The added `getReservation` assertion in the first seam case is partly redundant with `isReservationStore` but gives a pointed message; kept. No tests added or cut. The `console.log` timing line in the new supervisor case matches the two existing cases in that file.

**Not covered, unchanged from the handoff.** The overlap of a forced renewal with libp2p's own timer-driven refresh is not exercised (by reading the store, the second `addRelay` joins the queued job — benign). Why a relay forgets a reservation is out of scope.
