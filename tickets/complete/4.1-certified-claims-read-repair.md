# Complete: certified claims in read-repair + provable-only penalties

Second of three tickets from `accept-certified-claims-in-repair`. `certified-claim-selection`
landed the selection rules and `cluster/certified-claims.ts`; `certified-claims-reconcile-and-persist`
(4.2, in `implement/`) follows.

## What shipped

All in `packages/db-p2p/src/repo/coordinator-repo.ts` unless noted.

**Proof verification in `queryClusterForLatest`.** After the claim set is built, every claim
carrying a `proof` runs through `certifyClaim` in parallel, with thresholds
`{ superMajorityThreshold: this.superMajorityThreshold, simpleMajorityThreshold: 0.5 }`. The 0.5
is a deliberate hardcode: cluster members enforce `count > total / 2` (`ClusterMember.hasMajority`),
not the config's 0.51 default, and `ProofThresholds` documents that a verifier must mirror what
members enforce. A verifying proof sets `claim.certified = true`, which `selectQuorumRev` weighs.
A failing proof leaves the claim in the set UNCERTIFIED — it still corroborates by distinct-peer
count exactly as a proof-less claim does — and logs `cluster-fetch:proof-uncertified`.

**Verification-time penalties.** A proof failure classified attributable by
`isAttributableProofFailure` (replay / `claim-not-in-message`, self-contradicting artifact, digest
mismatch, …) reports the serving peer for `InvalidRestoration` via the never-throwing
`penalizeProofService`. Non-attributable failures (malformed proof or signature, unknown signer,
legacy record, oversized cohort) are never penalized: the identity behind the artifact was not
proven, so penalizing would let an attacker frame a peer.

**Equivocation naming.** On a selection decline, `certifiedEquivocation(claims)` is consulted and
`cluster-fetch:certified-equivocation` is logged (two verified proofs for different actions at the
same revision) before the unchanged `no-quorum` / `reportRepairDeadlock` path.
`cluster-fetch:certified-selected` names the passes where the certified rule won.

**Penalty rework (`penalizeContradictingRevClaims`).** The `rev > selected.rev` branch is gone: a
peer can honestly be ahead of the sampled quorum (in-flight commit, honest holders dropped by the
1s per-peer consult deadline), and the `InvalidRestoration` weight of 30 against a deprioritize
threshold of 20 meant one false hit deprioritized an honest peer. The same-rev/different-actionId
branch stays. This folded and deleted `tickets/backlog/debt-read-repair-penalty-provable-only.md`.

**Wiring.** `CoordinatorRepoComponents` gained optional `proofAnchoring?: ProofAnchoring`, threaded
through the `coordinatorRepo` factory and constructor into `certifyClaim`. Production wiring leaves
it absent by design (4.2 documents that at the composition root); `certifyClaim` logs unanchored
acceptance internally regardless.

`PenaltyReason.InvalidRestoration`'s doc in `reputation/types.ts` and the surrounding comments in
`coordinator-repo.ts` (the sole-holder message, the `uncorroboratedRev` availability-lever NOTE,
`reportRepairDeadlock`) were refreshed for the new reality.

## Review findings

### Fixed in this pass

- **A certified selection carried the contradiction penalty, so one forged proof could deprioritize
  the whole honest cohort.** `penalizeContradictingRevClaims` reported every claim at the selected
  revision under a different action, whichever way the selection was won. A passing proof shows the
  cohort it names signed the commit, never that those signers are the block's responsible cohort —
  caller obligation #1 in `cluster/commit-proof.ts`, and the anchoring layer that would close it is
  observational and unwired. So a peer holding N self-minted keys could mint a proof for
  `(block, rev, fabricated-action)`, win the selection at equal rev (the accepted cost of the
  certified path), and additionally have every honest holder of the real action at that revision
  reported for `InvalidRestoration` — weight 30 against a deprioritize threshold of 20 — on every
  consult. That is exactly the class of unprovable penalty this ticket existed to remove, re-entering
  through the new path. **Fix:** `penalizeContradictingRevClaims` now returns early when
  `selected.certified`; the method doc states why and names the revisit condition
  (`feat-cluster-membership-threshold-cert-anchoring` gating certification to the derived cohort
  makes the contradiction provable again). **Regression test:** "claims contradicting a CERTIFIED
  selection are never penalized" in `coordinator-repo-read-repair-trust.spec.ts` — verified to fail
  with the guard removed and pass with it. The corroborated-selection penalty test was renamed to
  say which selection it pins.

### Recorded as tripwires (no ticket)

- **Verification cost per consult.** One `certifyClaim` pass per proof-carrying answer, each bounded
  by `MAX_PROOF_SIGNERS` (256) signature checks. In `lazy` mode the read-repair window rate-limits
  consults; in `paranoid` mode every read of every block pays cohort-width verifications. Fine at
  deployment cohort sizes (~10). Parked as a `NOTE:` at the verification site in
  `queryClusterForLatest`, with the remedy if it ever shows in a profile (cache verdicts per
  `(blockId, rev, actionId, proof hash)` — never skip verification).

### Checked and found sound

- **Keeping an attributably-bad claim in the claim set.** A peer whose proof provably lies still
  votes and still contributes to `uncorroboratedRev`. Considered and left as is: that peer could
  equally have attached no proof at all, so dropping the vote removes no capability an attacker has
  — the comment at the site now says so explicitly.
- **The hardcoded `simpleMajorityThreshold: 0.5`.** Correct against `ProofThresholds`' documented
  contract and `ClusterMember.hasMajority`; using the config's 0.51 would reject proofs real cohorts
  produce.
- **`certifiedEquivocation` is asked only on a decline**, as its doc requires (a corroborated pair
  strictly above the top certified rev wins while an equivocation exists below it).
- **Totality of the verification pass.** `Promise.all` over `certifyClaim` cannot reject:
  `certifyClaim` is total on hostile input (guarded cap read, verifier's own catch-all
  `malformed-proof`, swallowed anchoring callbacks) and `penalizeProofService` never throws. A
  malformed proof from one peer cannot fail the whole read.
- **`cluster-fetch:certified-selected`** — the implementer flagged it as possibly beyond the
  ticket's letter. Kept: it fires only when the certified rule won, and it is the only way a log
  reader can tell a selection resting on one claimant's signature set from one resting on votes.

## Honest gaps carried forward

- **The lone certified holder does not yet heal end to end.** `queryClusterForLatest` now selects
  the certified pair, but acquisition goes through `acquireBlockFromCohort` → `reconcileBlock`,
  which still requires a content quorum of two carriers (`selectQuorumBlock`, floor 2 at any cohort
  size above two). A single certified holder in a 10-peer cohort therefore drives the selection and
  the promotion attempt but cannot supply the bytes until 4.2 certifies the reconcile path. The
  headline test asserts what this ticket owns — that the certified claim reached the restore path
  with `context.committed` set — not that bytes landed. This is the intended split, not a defect.
- **`QuorumRev.certified` is not propagated out of `queryClusterForLatest`**; the returned
  `corroborated` is a plain `(rev, actionId)`. 4.2's reconcile path re-verifies for itself, so no
  information is lost, but the two paths verify the same proofs twice per repair.
- **Anchoring is unwired**, so an attacker minting a whole cohort's keys still forges a proof that
  verifies. Accepted posture for the chain; closed by
  `feat-cluster-membership-threshold-cert-anchoring`.
- **Repeated penalization.** A peer persistently serving an attributably-bad proof is penalized on
  every consult (bounded by the read-repair window in lazy mode, every read in paranoid). Same
  posture as the pre-existing contradiction penalty; unchanged here.
