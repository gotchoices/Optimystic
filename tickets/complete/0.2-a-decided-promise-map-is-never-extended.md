# A decided promise map is never extended

First half of the GitHub #64 work; `a-consensus-round-stops-waiting-once-its-outcome-is-decided` builds on it. Absorbed backlog `bug-a-late-promise-invalidates-the-commit-signatures-already-collected`.

## What landed

A consensus record is *decided* when its approved promises reach `ceil(peerCount × superMajorityThreshold)`. A decided record's promise map is never extended, and a commit signature sits only in a record whose promise map is exactly the one it was signed over. The signed-hash layout (`computeClusterCommitHash`) is unchanged.

- **Member** (`ClusterMember` in `packages/db-p2p/src/cluster/cluster-repo.ts`): `getTransactionPhase` sends a decided record without this member's commit straight to `OurCommitNeeded`, whether or not the member promised; the own-promise branch runs only on an undecided record. `mergeSignatures` (called by `mergeRecords`) keeps a decided held map, adopts a decided incoming map over an undecided held one, otherwise unites the two, and keeps each side's commits only when that side's map is the chosen one (`cluster-member:commit-over-other-promise-map`). The member now uses db-core's `canonicalJson` instead of a private copy.
- **Coordinator** (`ClusterCoordinator` in `packages/db-p2p/src/repo/cluster-coordinator.ts`): one rule, `keepCommitsSignedOverRecordPromises`, takes each answering member's own commit only when its answer's promise map equals the record's. The promise round, the pre-sign, the commit round and the commit retry all use it (`cluster-tx:commit-over-other-promise-map`). A member whose commit was dropped stays owed the record by the commit retry.
- **Review addition**: a member that signs a decided pend without promising now consumes that action's own slot hold (`consumeOwnSlotHoldsOfDecided`). Before, it never did, because it skips pend validation.

## Review findings

I read the implement diff (`ticket(implement): a-decided-promise-map-is-never-extended`) before the handoff, then the surrounding member phase loop, timer paths, and coordinator retry and carry paths.

**Checked and correct (no change):**
- Member phase machine: traced 2-, 3-, 4- and 5-member cohorts through the promise round, commit round, retry and broadcast. A member never adds a promise to a decided record, the fixpoint loop still terminates, and the `Promising`/`Consensus`/`Propagating` fall-through is unchanged for decided records the member has signed.
- `mergeSignatures` in each branch:
  - A response lost after a member promised: held `{A,D}` against the coordinator's decided `{A,B,C}` adopts the incoming map and signs over it. This is the reproduced defect.
  - A member's reject or `held` vote, whether merged or lost.
  - The immediate per-peer retry of the promise round: neither record is decided, so the maps are united.
  - The held-decided branch is dormant, because a member clears a record once it signs. If it is ever reached it heals itself: the member signs over its held map, the coordinator drops that commit, the member has already cleared its state, and the next retry delivery is signed over the coordinator's map.
- Coordinator keep rule, now unconditional in the promise round: no remote member can sign a commit there when this node's pre-vote failed. One approval cannot decide a 2-member cohort, and larger cohorts need more than one, so dropping the old self-promise guard is safe.
- Expiry: a member holding its own promise still throws `Transaction expired`. One whose own promise was not merged now signs instead of throwing. That matches the blind commit vote, and the existing NOTE in `processUpdate` already pointed this way. Accepted, as the handoff said.
- Timer paths: `handleExpiration` acts only on a held record without this member's promise, which no write path produces. `processCleanupQueue` and `provenAbandoned` are unaffected.
- Carried (owed) records ride on other requests only once a majority exists, and the broadcast merges no commits, so neither bypasses the keep rule.
- The change to `runRetry` and `commitTransaction` that keeps owing members that answered without a kept commit is bounded by the retry budget. Agreed it is the right behaviour.
- Error handling and logging: drops are logged on both sides with the dropped peers, and nothing is swallowed.

**Found and fixed (minor):**
- A late member that signs a decided pend without promising skipped `judgeSlotHolds`. The action's own slot hold was therefore never consumed, and kept answering rivals `held` for the rest of its window (up to `slotHoldWindowMs`, 3 s by default) after the holder had already won.
  - Fix: `consumeOwnSlotHoldsOfDecided`, called from the phase loop's `OurCommitNeeded` arm when the member has no promise on the record. The consume step is shared with `judgeSlotHolds` through `consumeSlotHold`.
  - Pinned by the new case "is consumed when this member signs the holder's pend decided without its promise" in `packages/db-p2p/test/cluster-slot-hold.spec.ts`. Without the fix it fails: no `slot-hold-consumed` line.
  - Updated the slot-hold bullet in `docs/internals.md` and the `getTransactionPhase` comment.
