Stage 5 of [docs/offline-sync.md §Implementation stages](../../docs/offline-sync.md#implementation-stages), part three of three: restoring journaled offline transactions after a restart. The design as built is [docs/offline-sync.md §Restore after a restart](../../docs/offline-sync.md#restore-after-a-restart). Implemented in `ticket(implement): offline-sync-journal-restore`; this review pass fixed two defects in it, recorded one tripwire, and found nothing to file.

## What was built

`OfflineRestore` (`packages/quereus-plugin-optimystic/src/optimystic-adapter/offline-restore.ts`), owned by `OfflineMode`, runs one pass at a time: it lists the label's entry, drive and outcome records once, loads the outcomes into the retained map, reserves every listed sequence number on the queue (`OfflineQueue.reserveSeqThrough`), and walks the entry records in queue order — a record with an outcome record is settled and deleted, one the queue holds is skipped, one of an unknown version throws `OfflineJournalError` with every record from it on untouched, and otherwise each table is initialized through `OptimysticModule.collectionsOfTable` and the entry put back through `OfflineQueue.restore`, whose `stage` callback answers `already-committed` when the collection holds the recorded attempt (`Collection.holdsCommitted`) and otherwise inserts the segment by sequence (`Collection.restoreSealed`). A missing table, a `BlockUnavailableError` along the cause chain, or a collection the table no longer backs defers the record and every record after it (`behind-deferred`). Deferred records are retried by `plugin.offline.restore()` (run by `plugin.hydrate`), by the vtab after a full initialization or a live refresh that reached the cohort (`OfflineMode.tableReadable`, once `TransactionBridge.whenTransactionIdle` resolves), and by the queue's reachability gate, which starts a pass and answers `false` while anything is deferred. The controller lists deferred records as `queued` with `waitingFor: 'restore'`, counts them as queued for routing and the DDL gate, refuses to degrade reads of their tables, waits for them in `whenDrained`, and discards one not in doubt through the journal's `settled`.

The implement handoff's departures from the plan all stand: the gate starts rather than awaits a pass (awaiting would deadlock with the drain iteration asking it); deferred sequence numbers are reserved on the queue; `behind-deferred` as a fourth reason; a discarded deferred record gets an outcome record; `whenDrained` waits out a running pass; `whenTransactionIdle` on the bridge; the mesh harness's `durabilityWindowFor` and the per-call coordinator resolution after `Mesh.restart`.

## Review findings

**Checked.** The implement diff first, with fresh eyes, then the handoff. `OfflineRestore` end to end — `run`'s pending-pass sharing, the pass's publish-on-completion and restore-on-throw of the deferred map, the walk's four dispositions, `attempt`'s `attempting` window, `restoreOne`'s rollback-to-deferral on an unavailable replay, `stage`'s two already-committed tests, `positionOf`, `loadOutcomes`, `settleDiscarded`, `dispose` — against `OfflineQueue.restore`'s contract (the drain hold, registration by `seq`, rollback of a throwing `stage`, `UnknownEntryError` for the entry being restored) and against `Collection.restoreSealed` and `holdsCommitted` (`actionIdAt` reaches every entry since the log's start while no checkpoint is written, which the existing NOTE at `holdsCommitted` records). The bridge's idle waiters settle on both transaction-ending paths (`endTransaction`, and `rollbackTransaction`'s `finally`). `OptimysticModule.collectionsOfTable` does what `resolveConnectedTable`'s live path does — lookup or instantiate, `initialize`, `ensureConnectionRegistered` for a fresh instance — and never the provisional pass, so nothing that path does is skipped. Both vtab triggers (after `publishOfflinePolicy` in `doInitialize`; `reachedCohort`), the gate, `whenDrained`'s loop (no gap between the queue's answer and the restorer's check), `dispose`'s ordering, `entries`, `status`, `discard`, `assertNoQueuedEntries`, `degradesLiveReads`, `OfflineJournal.scan`/`adopt`/`deleteRecords`/the decoders, the harness changes, and every doc the change touches (`docs/offline-sync.md`, `docs/internals.md`, `docs/debugging.md`, the README). Error paths by reading: a pass that throws out of a table's initialization leaves unreached records as the previous pass left them; a transaction that begins during a gate-triggered pass makes `restoreSealed` throw a plain error, the queue rolls the registration back, the pass is logged `offline:restore-pass-failed` and the next gate re-check or read retries it. Kept as a retry rather than a deferral, as the handoff asked: the cause is this process's own and clears within one re-check interval, and a deferral would list the record `waitingFor: 'restore'` for a reason no table trigger answers.

**Found and fixed (minor).**

- **`discard` of the record a pass was putting back was accepted** (defect, confirmed by reading `OfflineRestore.discard` beside `attempt` and the queue's `discard`; test added). The record stays in `deferred` for the whole `attempting` window and the queue refuses it as unknown until admitted, so the controller fell through to the restorer, which removed it, chained an outcome write and record deletion behind the pass, and emitted `failed/discarded` — while the queue went on to admit and drive the same entry, whose next drive-record write would find no sequence number. `EntryNotDiscardableError` (`packages/db-core/src/offline/status.ts`) gains `because: 'restoring'`, which the restorer throws for the attempting record; the controller, README and `docs/offline-sync.md` say so. Pinned by the new case "discard of a deferred record while a pass is putting that record back is refused, and the pass queues it", which parks the pass on its first block read of the table (`ParkedReads` in the spec over the `withReads` wrapper now in `test/isolation.ts`).
- **A record discarded mid-walk could come back as deferred.** After the walk stopped, a record listed behind the stopping one was already in the pass's `next` map; a `discard` landing during a later settled record's `deleteRecords` await removed it from the old map only, and publishing `next` relisted it while its settlement ran. The pass now drops every `discarding` id from `next` before publishing — exact, since a discard's settlement is chained behind the running pass. No test: the window is one await inside the walk and pinning it needs a journal double; the fix is one line at the publish site.
- **DRY:** `unavailableIn` was duplicated between `offline-restore.ts` and `test/isolation.ts`; the source exports it and the helper re-exports it. The transactor read-wrapping `Proxy` is extracted as `withReads` so `Isolation` and the new parked-read double share it.

**Tripwires recorded.** `OfflineJournal.settled`: a crash between its entry delete and its drive delete leaves a drive record no pass lists or deletes (`NOTE:` there, with the safe deletion rule — at or below the highest listed entry sequence only). Left standing from the implementation: one pass per queue re-check while anything is deferred (`OfflineMode.reachability`), and the accepted tradeoff for an in-doubt record whose table was dropped (`OfflineRestore.discard`).

**Considered, not changed.** A pass initializing a table that a `behind-deferred` record also names triggers `tableReadable`, which schedules one extra pass (at most one is ever pending); cheap, and distinguishing it from a concurrent read's trigger would cost more than it saves. An outcome record of an unknown version stops the pass before any entry is walked, since outcomes are decoded first; consistent with the loud-failure rule and a downgrade is unsupported. `collection-mismatch` also covers an index another machine dropped while this one was down; the record is listed and discardable. The harness's `currentCoordinatorOf` claims `pendMany`/`commitMany` unconditionally, which every mesh coordinator (`CoordinatorRepo`) has. The implementer's twelve cases each pin a contract from the design and none restates the implementation or verifies a mock, so all are kept.
