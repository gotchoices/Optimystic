# A consensus round stops waiting once its outcome is decided

GitHub #64 (VoteTorrent). `ClusterCoordinator` used to await every member in each of its three rounds (promise, commit, consensus broadcast), so one connected-but-silent member cost every write its RPC deadline per round (reporter: median control write 0.47 s healthy against 75 s with one silent phone).

## What landed

- `packages/db-p2p/src/repo/round-decision.ts`: `awaitDecision` (resolves once a rule says the answers in hand settle the round — reached, or out of reach — plus a grace for the unanswered), and the three rules `promiseRoundDecided` (super-majority of approvals in the merged map), `commitRoundDecided` (simple majority of commits the record would keep, by the equal-map rule `commitsSignedOverRecordPromises`), `broadcastDecided` (strict majority of holders by `isDurableMajority`, which `coordinator-repo.ts` now imports from here, so the broadcast and the durability gate count with one function).
- **Grace** (`DecisionGrace`, `STRAGGLER_GRACE_FLOOR_MS`): after the decision a round waits for the unanswered members up to as long again as the decision took, at least 50 ms. This deviates from the plan's pure "stop at the decision"; see the review findings for why it was accepted.
- **Stragglers** in `cluster-coordinator.ts`: a member still unanswered after the grace is marked in flight (`ClusterTransactionState.inFlight`), owed the record (`stragglers`, then the retry's pending set via `owedAfterRounds`), never penalised, and its promise never merged. Its answer (`onStragglerAnswer`) is buffered until the next round boundary while the rounds run (`lateAnswers`, `foldBufferedAnswers`), folded at once after the retry is armed (`foldStragglerAnswer` → `settleOwedDelivery`). The commit round never targets a member a promise request still rides to (`targetsNow`).
- `runRetry` re-arms without spending an attempt while every pending member is in flight; `releaseTransaction` is idempotent (`released`).
- Docs: `docs/internals.md` (§Both consensus rounds…, "A behind member actively reconciles"), `packages/db-p2p/docs/cluster.md` (Phase 1, Phase 2 with the cohort-size table, Consensus Achievement, Commit Retry Loop, Latency), `docs/correctness.md` (§2 durability residual, Theorem 1 note on late votes). `PromiseShortfallError.silentPeers` doc says it can name merely-unanswered members.

Tests: `test/cluster-coordinator.spec.ts` › *ClusterCoordinator stops waiting once a round is decided* (an undecidable round keeps waiting; a straggler outliving the retry budget is still delivered), and `test/a-silent-member-does-not-hold-up-a-write.spec.ts` (four-member mesh, one member silent: pend and commit acknowledged inside one RPC deadline as `majority` with the silent member `unconfirmed`, not penalised, and holding the commit once it answers).

## Review findings

Read the implement commit (`ticket(implement): a-consensus-round-stops-waiting-once-its-outcome-is-decided`) first, plus the early draft of `round-decision.ts` that commit `Tess bump` swept up; the final version is the implement commit's.

**Deviation from the plan (the grace): accepted.** The strict rule makes the last member of every healthy four- or five-member cohort a straggler a few milliseconds behind the others, which turns every such write's durability class from `full` to `majority`, puts that member in the under-replication ledger, and draws a redundant drain push per block. The grace removes that while keeping a silent member's cost at about twice the decision time. The tuning direction is recorded in the `NOTE:` on `DecisionGrace`; no change.

**Correctness — checked, nothing found.**
- Decided-map safety: a straggler's promise is never merged (`onStragglerAnswer`, `foldBufferedAnswers`); its commit is kept only through `keepCommitsSignedOverRecordPromises`, so a late promise-round answer (signed over the prevote-only snapshot) is dropped, as it must be.
- The commit round's out-of-reach arithmetic excludes in-flight members it does not target; a shortfall there owes them through `owedAfterRounds`, and the retry's broadcast reaches them. Correct.
- Every round end arms the retry with the stragglers (`deliverConsensus`, the commit round's no-majority branch, the pend deferral), so `executeClusterTransaction`'s `finally` never releases a transaction that still owes a straggler. Failure exits (rejection, conflict, held, shortfall) throw and release; a straggler's answer then finds `released` and is dropped. Correct.
- `releaseTransaction`'s new idempotence covers the one new double-exit (a straggler finishing the retry before the `finally`).
- `awaitDecision`: a request that rejects becomes a failed delivery; the grace timer is cancelled when the last answer lands first; a round with nobody to ask resolves at once.
- Classification on votes in hand (merely-unanswered members in `silentPeers`; a late minority reject no longer marks the record `disputed`) is documented in correctness.md Theorem 1 and the `silentPeers` doc; dispute origination is dormant. Accepted as the implementer described.

**Efficiency — one finding, fixed.** While every pending member was in flight, `runRetry` re-armed at the same interval, so a transaction with a silent straggler woke every 250 ms for the straggler's whole request — up to 20 s with the default one immediate retry on the promise round, about 80 wakes per write, each re-persisting coordinator state. Now the re-armed retry carries `waitingOnInFlight` and its timer is a fallback at the longest retry interval (`retryMaxIntervalMs`, 8 s); the answer that ends an in-flight request (`onStragglerAnswer`, `returnCarried`) re-arms it at its own interval through `resumeRetryAfterInFlight`, so a member still owed after that answer is sent the record as promptly as before. The existing straggler test pins the prompt resume (mutation-checked: removing the resume from `onStragglerAnswer` fails it). `packages/db-p2p/docs/cluster.md` §Commit Retry Loop updated.

**Tripwires recorded.**
- `NOTE:` at `broadcastAbandonment`: a promise-round straggler is sent the abandonment while its own promise request is still on its way; taken in the other order the stale request plants a reservation until the member's staleness sweep (same shape as the escorted-pend `NOTE:` at `takeOwedRecords`). Bounded by the sweep; revisit if such reservations show up as `held`/`conflict` votes.
- Existing implementer tripwires stand: escorted pend vs. its own promise request (`takeOwedRecords`), behind coordinator waiting for outstanding remotes (not a regression), deferred pend retry while carried, `runRetry`'s own `Promise.all` first loop (background only).

**Hygiene / size.** `cluster-coordinator.ts` is 2261 lines (`wc -l`, after this review). The straggler path is owed-delivery bookkeeping; the arm in backlog `debt-cluster-coordinator-owed-delivery-outgrew-its-file` was extended with this review's additions and the new count. `round-decision.ts` is pure, small and well-scoped.
