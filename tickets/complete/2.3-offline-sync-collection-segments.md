The design is [§The drain does not hold the collection latch across the network](../../docs/offline-sync.md#the-drain-does-not-hold-the-collection-latch-across-the-network); the rules as built are the sixth bullet of [docs/internals.md §Staged actions are segments](../../docs/internals.md#staged-actions-are-segments). ## What landed

**A shared attempt module**, `packages/db-core/src/collection/write-attempts.ts`: `AttemptCycle` (one write's retry accounting: the consecutive-failure budget, the stall check, the three refusals stopped by name, the durability folded across commits, and the back-off-refresh-repeat loop that keeps refreshing while finishing a half-landed write is refused for a cause that can clear), `stageAttempt` (a per-attempt tracker over a copy of the target's transforms sharing its pins, the log append under a fixed `WriteIdentity`, the digests, the size check) and `retainedAttempt`. `Collection.syncAttempts` and `Collection.commitHead` each decide only what an attempt is built from and what to do with a commit.

**`Collection.commitHead(entryId, options)`** with `HeadCommitOptions` (the queue's action id and timestamp beside `SyncOptions`), and `SealedSegmentsError` extended with a `reason` (`whole-collection`, `not-head`, `refused`, `in-flight`) and the entry named. The cycle opens with a refresh under the supplied action id, so a re-driven entry's earlier commit is finished or consumed rather than sent twice; each attempt runs on a head tracker built once per requested revision from the head segment's actions alone over the committed state (from the invented baseline while nothing is committed), re-judged for moved bases before each attempt; the one `await` on `source.transact` runs with the collection latch released and `headInFlight` set for exactly that window; on success the head is dropped, the attempt's transforms folded and the revision adopted before the remaining segments replay.

**The three hooks the released latch needs.** A refresh that walks into the in-flight entry returns before changing anything (`collection:refresh-deferred-to-head-commit`); `consumeOwnEntry` has a head arm that drops the head segment after checking its action count; `dropSealed` of the in-flight head throws `in-flight`.

**Docs**: `docs/internals.md` (the segments subsection and three Key Invariants bullets), `docs/offline-sync.md` (status, stage 2, the drain section rewritten for what was built), two citations retargeted in `docs/correctness.md` and `docs/transactions.md`, and the `collection` namespace row of `docs/debugging.md` (review).

## Review findings

**Read first, with fresh eyes**: the implement diff over `collection.ts`, `write-attempts.ts`, `struct.ts`, the test double, both specs and the four docs; then the handoff.

**Checked by reading and running the code.**

- The latch handoff: `sendUnlatched` releases, sends, retakes in `finally` and only then clears `headInFlight`, so every error out of the send leaves the instance latched and unmarked; the outer `release` closes over the rebound handle. A second `commitHead` queues on the latch and meets `in-flight`; a later entry meets `not-head`.
- The deferral in `updateInternal` is gated on `headInFlight`, which the head commit's own refreshes run with cleared, so they take the own-entry path. The ordering `committableHead` before `recordRefusal` cannot skip retaining a torn attempt: a rival refusing the head in a reader's replay means the rival took the revision the attempt requested, so the attempt's tail did not land; and if the tail did land, a reader's walk contains the own entry and defers.
- The fold is of the attempt's transforms (log tail included), before the replay; the two-consecutive-head-commit cases pin it. The head tracker is reused per revision and rebuilt on a moved base or a new revision, so the byte-identical retry rule holds the way the sync path's does.
- `AttemptCycle` reproduces the sync path's accounting unchanged: the stall check, the backoff and the slot-hold exemption, the three stop-by-name refusals, the last-chance round. The opening refresh sleeps zero because no attempt has failed; with `maxAttempts: 1` it is also the last-chance round, which is consistent with `recordRefusal` ending the write on the first refusal.
- Every existing own-entry, retry and coordinator suite still passes over the extracted module, which is the evidence the extraction changed no behaviour.
- Source hygiene: the new module is 457 lines of mostly contract comments that say why, not what; `collection.ts` is a net fourteen lines shorter and the remaining size debt is appended to `debt-collection-write-retry-logic-outgrew-its-file` by the implementer. No comment narrates statements.
