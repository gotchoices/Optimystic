Part 2 of stage 6 of [docs/offline-sync.md §Implementation stages](../../docs/offline-sync.md#implementation-stages): the hard form of the reachability gate, under the maintainer decision of 2026-10-25 recorded in [§When to submit: the reachability gate](../../docs/offline-sync.md#when-to-submit-the-reachability-gate). Built in `ticket(implement): offline-sync-solo-refusal`.

## What landed

- `SyncOptions.refuseSoloCommit` (default off). It travels as `refuseSolo: true` on `PendRequest`, `CommitRequest` and `RepoCommitRequest`. When unset the field is left off, never sent as `false` (`refuseSoloField` in `packages/db-core/src/network/refuse-solo.ts`), so signature preimages are unchanged.
- Every send of an attempt cycle carries it:
  - `Collection.send`, and the re-send `completeOwnEntry` makes to finish a torn attempt (through `InFlightCycle`, recorded beside the in-flight mark);
  - `openHeadCommit`'s `OpenHeadCommitOptions`;
  - the coordinator's `CommitCycle.inFlight` → `WriteContext.refuseSolo` → `pendRequestFor` / `commitRequestFor`;
  - `NetworkTransactor`'s per-coordinator rebuilds (`pendRequestForBatch`, `repoCommitRequest`, `commitOf`).

  `TransactorSource.transact`'s optional trailing arguments became one `TransactOptions` object.
- `CoordinatorRepo` (db-p2p) applies one rule, `soloRefusalFor`: refuse with `unrouted`, `absent-members` or `awaiting-bootstrap`, otherwise the node is genuinely alone and commits.
  - `pendSolo` refuses before anything is reserved, answering with a `PromiseShortfallError` failure carrying `soloRefused`.
  - `commitSolo` refuses before a proof is minted or anything is applied, with a conflict-shaped `SOLO_COMMIT_REFUSED_REASON`.
  - The refusals are logged as `pend:solo-refused` and `commit:solo-refused`.
- `OfflineQueue` sets the option on every submission unless built with `allowSoloCommit: true`.
- Deviation: `TransactionCoordinator.execute` takes no `SyncOptions` and does not carry the flag. Offline mode never uses it.

## Review findings

**Read first:** the implement diff, then the handoff.

**Coverage of the flag:** checked every path that builds a pend or commit request. None drops it:
- `TransactorSource.transact`;
- `commitInOneRound` → `sendCommit` → `repoCommitRequest`;
- `commitMany`;
- `commitTailThenSweep`, for both the tail and the sweep, via `commitOf`;
- the whole-request send in `pendMany`;
- the coordinator's per-collection fallback after a declined batch (`pendCollection` → `pendRequestFor`, which spreads the flag whatever `sole` is);
- the `WriteContext` built by `commitEntry`.

db-p2p's `refusesSolo` refuses a batch if any of its requests carries the flag. That is correct, because a batch is one action.

**Correctness checked:**
- Shortfall arithmetic: `needed = max(2, ceil(threshold × (1 + absent)))` is always above `approvals: 1`, so `PromiseShortfallError` stays a real shortfall. `classifyCommitFailure` sorts it `cohort-unreachable`, and the mesh case shows `waitingFor: 'cohort'`.
- Commit-side refusal: `TransactorSource.transact` discharges the pend and returns the conflict. `syncAttempts` and the coordinator re-drive it, and the retry's pend meets the pend-side refusal.
  - On the two-step path, a refused sweep block after a landed tail goes through `completeOwnEntry`. Its re-send carries the flag again, so the write ends as a non-final `TornActionError` and the entry stays in doubt.
  - That is the right direction: the entry never settles on a lone commit. The case can only arise when a partner drops between the tail's commit and the sweep.
- `pendSolo` now reads `absentCohortMembers` before the apply rather than after. The only extra cost is one lookup when the apply is refused.
- Re-marking with a different `InFlightCycle` replaces the old one. That is harmless, because every caller re-marks with what it marked. Two writers on one instance remain the hazard the existing note at `beginInFlightAction` already records.
- After a commit-side refusal the partner keeps the pend's reservation, because the writer's cancel reaches only the lone coordinator. This is the existing partition behaviour the handoff already stated, so nothing was filed.
