# A wide-mesh watch test sometimes sees no notification wake — complete

Test and docs only; no product code changed.

## What landed

From `ticket(implement): a-wide-mesh-watch-test-sometimes-sees-no-notification-wake`:

- The wide-mesh case's attempt loop waits for the watch's wake and a delivered notify frame together, then counts the attempt only when no tail read started in between. A lost first notification costs its attempt the 10 s timeout instead of ending it at once on the watcher's own storage wake. Attempts went from 4 to 6, leaving room for two lost notifications (each costs two attempts: its own, and the next one, disqualified by the gap-recovery tail read).
- The wait for the registration to replicate to the announcing member uses the forwarder's own lookup (`findByCoord` at the tail's root coordinate, root-placed engine only — the same as `directSubscribers` in `packages/db-p2p/src/libp2p-node-base.ts`) instead of `findServing(topicId, 0)`.
- `docs/reactivity.md` gained a paragraph: a newly formed root group publishes its membership certificate only on its next gossip-cadence pump, so the announcing member drops its own first notification as `untrusted` until then; measured 8 of 16 first commits unannounced, the next commit 3–13 s later announced.
