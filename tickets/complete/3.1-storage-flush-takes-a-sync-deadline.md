# Storage flush takes a sync deadline — complete

Second of five tickets from the plan `durability-window-scales-with-cohort-size` (GitHub #37). `flush()` on every store takes `FlushOptions.withinMs`, every store has `whenDurable()`, the backend under `WriteGroupDriver` is a `WriteGroupSink` whose `commit` is told `syncWithinMs` and hands back a `durable` promise, and `WriteGroup` holds a write in its overlay until that promise resolves. No production caller passes a window and no production backend defers, so every production path behaves as before. The work landed as `tess: timed out on storage-flush-takes-a-sync-deadline` (the contract, the wrappers, the `WriteGroup` rewrite and the test double) and `ticket(implement): storage-flush-takes-a-sync-deadline` (one fix, the tests, the docs); the implementers for the file store and the other backends were swept into the sibling review commit `ticket(review): file-storage-commits-groups-through-a-log` and are recorded in `tickets/.pre-existing-known.md`.

## Review findings

**Scope.** The whole feature was read, not only the second commit: `WriteGroup`, `WriteGroupDriver`, the `WriteGroupSink` and `BatchBackend` contracts, the flush contract on `RawStoreDriver`, the four wrappers, the four trivial drivers, `FileBatchBackend`, `FileKVStore`, `SYNCED_BATCH`, `MemoryBatchBackend`, the conformance registration, the spec, and Invariant 6 of `packages/db-p2p/docs/storage.md`. The `NOTE:` additions in `file-write-log.ts`, `store-lock.ts` and the two fs specs that appear in the same diff range belong to the two sibling review commits, not to this ticket, and were left alone. The two downstream tickets (`nodes-apply-the-durability-window`, `file-store-syncs-by-the-earliest-deadline`) were read to check that what they will build on is what the contract promises; the second confirms that a real sync covers exactly the records appended before it, which is the premise of the first defect below.

**Found and fixed, major (both latent: no backend defers yet, both wrong the moment one does).**

- *A strict flush could share a sync that began before its batch committed.* `WriteGroup` kept one in-flight `sink.sync()` and every strict flush with nothing to seal joined it. A batch committed after that sync began is not covered by it, so the joining flush waited on a `durable` the shared sync would never resolve, until the batch's own deadline. That breaks rule 4 (a `withinMs` 0 flush hastens every earlier deferred write). `syncThrough(seq)` now records the latest committed batch when a sync begins and shares it only when that covers the requester's batch, running a second sync after it otherwise. Pinned by *a strict flush does not share a sync that began before its batch committed*, which hangs against the pre-review code.
- *A relaxed flush that sealed nothing ignored its deadline.* Rule 2 binds every write a flush covers to that flush's `withinMs`, but a relaxed flush arriving with nothing open (the common shape under two concurrent groups: the first seals both groups' writes, the second finds nothing to seal) resolved at once and left the already-committed batches on the later deadline they were given. Under `nodes-apply-the-durability-window`, windows differ by cohort size, so this is reachable. The sink cannot be told a sooner deadline after a commit, so `hastenBy` now hastens the batches in the background when the flush's deadline is sooner than theirs, behind the commit in flight if there is one; the flush itself still resolves as a relaxed one. The early sync costs at most the difference between the two deadlines and never happens under one window per node. Pinned by *a relaxed flush that seals nothing hastens the committed batches when its deadline is sooner than theirs, and leaves them when it is not*, which hangs against the pre-review code.

**Found and fixed, minor.**

- `runCommit` now rejects its flush when the group latched while the commit was in flight (a deferred sync of an earlier batch failed), so no flush resolves after a latch; before, a strict flush in flight at the latch could resolve while an earlier write it covers was lost.
- `flush()` rejected for a non-finite or negative `withinMs` by throwing synchronously out of a promise-returning method; it now returns a rejection like every other failure path.
- The background-fault log is one path, `latchUnawaited`, logged once per group on `write-group:error` whether the fault came from a deferred `durable` or from the hasten behind a relaxed flush.
- `MemoryBatchBackend` drained its unsynced list until empty, so a batch committed while a parked sync ran was marked durable by that sync. A real sync covers what was committed when it began (the file-store ticket says so of its sync step), and the double now snapshots the list at the start of each drain, which is what lets the first test above fail against the old code. A drain also refuses once the backend has latched, as its own doc already claimed.
- The sink's commit options were an inline `{ readonly syncWithinMs: number }` repeated in three files; named `SinkCommitOptions` in `write-group.ts` and used by `MemoryBatchBackend` and `FileBatchBackend`.
- Docs: rule 2 in `storage.md` said a flushed write survives a process crash unconditionally where the contract on `RawStoreDriver.flush` says "where `durability` names one"; the two now agree, and the *Writes that wait in memory* paragraph describes the relaxed-empty-flush hasten and the unawaited-fault log.

**Checked and found correct, by reading and by the existing tests:** the pooled deadline of flushes sharing a seal and the forced `syncWithinMs` 0 under `maxUnsyncedBytes`; overlay entries held until `durable` and the listing merges keyed by record; `whenDurable()` as the latest committed batch's `durable`, resolved at once with nothing pending and rejected after a fault; `close()` and the idle timer flushing strictly; `latch` unwrapping a sink's own `StoreFailedError` to its cause; every wrapper forwarding both members; the four trivial drivers ignoring the window; `FileBatchBackend` and `FileKVStore` returning `SYNCED_BATCH` with a no-op `sync`. The one-microtask window the handoff noted, where a hasten can call `sink.sync()` on a backend with nothing to sync, is unreachable from an awaiting caller because the resolved `durable`'s continuation is queued before the commit's own waiters, and was left as noted.

**Tests.** The implementer's seven cases were kept: each pins a distinct rule or a path the conformance suite cannot reach (the handoff's own account of what the shared-seal case does not assert is accurate and acceptable, since the two settle points are pinned by the first case). Two cases were added, one per defect above, both verified to fail against the pre-review `write-group.ts`.

**Tripwires parked.** `NOTE:` at `deferred` in `packages/db-p2p/src/deferred.ts`: every promise `flush()` returns is pre-observed, so a fire-and-forget flush fails silently; every caller awaits today.

**Tickets filed:** none. **Accepted-tradeoff notes met:** none at the sites changed.

**Not changed, by design:** `maxUnsyncedBytes` accepts `Infinity` (no bound); the idle flush stays strict; `IKVStore.flush` keeps its option-less optional signature, since hosts implement it outside this repository and `FileKVStore` accepts the options as an extension.
