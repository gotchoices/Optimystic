# Cohort gossip sends a frame only when something changed

GitHub [#49](https://github.com/gotchoices/Optimystic/issues/49), from Sereus (Taleus). First of three tickets from the plan `feat-idle-cohort-gossip-costs-a-phone-little`; siblings are `push-state-gossip-sends-only-when-the-state-changed` and `cohort-gossip-is-verified-once-off-the-synchronous-path`.

## What changed

Before: `gossipRound` throttled only a round with no resident topics and no deltas. Every engine serving a watched table holds a registration, so it always had a topic summary and sent a frame on every 5 s tick — 18 engines × 6 rounds = the 108 frames per 30 s reported.

Now a round sends its frame only when `isGossipRoundDue` says so — any one of:

- it carries deltas (`pending` not empty: admission, renewal touch, re-attach, eviction, child link/unlink including the child-set re-advertisement);
- its `gossipMaterialKey` differs from the last frame this engine sent (a first frame always differs);
- `willingnessHeartbeatMs` (default 30 s) has passed since that frame.

The material key is a JSON string over `cohortEpoch`, `treeTier`, `willingnessBits`, `loadBuckets`, `windowSeconds`, `rootPlaced`, and topic summaries reduced to `topicId`/`tier`/`directParticipants`/`promoted`/`childCohortCount`, sorted by `topicId`. Excluded: `timestamp`, `signature`, `arrivalsPerMin`, `queriesPerMin`, the delta arrays (deltas force due-ness on their own; including them would make the round after a delta round differ), and the per-engine constants `v`/`fromMember`/`coord`.

Host wiring (`createCoordEngine` in `host.ts`):

- `lastGossipAt: number` became `lastSent: SentGossip` (`{ key, at }`). Both the round and the out-of-band willingness advert set it, synchronously, before signing — same moment the old `lastGossipAt` was set — so a round racing a slow sign compares against it. A frame lost to `closed` during signing is still recorded (accepted: the engine is gone).
- `gossipRound` checks `pending.isEmpty()` and drains **only when something is queued**; a queued delta makes the round due by itself, so the drain only ever feeds a frame that goes out. The frame is built once, then the key is computed and due-ness decided. This deviates in shape from the ticket's "decide due-ness before `pending.drain()`" but keeps its invariant (a non-sending round never drops a delta) without building the frame twice. Reviewer: judge whether the comment at the call makes that clear enough.
- `buildCohortGossip` lost its `heartbeat` input. Its only remaining rule: an idle engine (no topics, no deltas) that is willing for no tier returns `undefined`; otherwise it builds a frame. The heartbeat throttle now lives entirely in `isGossipRoundDue`. So unwilling→willing on an idle engine is announced next round (if siblings did not already hear that willingness); willing→unwilling on an idle engine still sends nothing, as before.
- `traffic.publish` still runs every round, sent or not (one-line comment at the call).
- `NOTE:` tripwire at the renewal `touch:` hook: defer lastPing-only touches up to ttl/6 if renewal-driven frames on engines holding many records show up in profiles, weighed against backup-replica age at failover.

Not done (per the ticket): deferring renewal touches. Touches stay immediate deltas.

## Tests

Added in `packages/db-p2p/test/cohort-topic/gossip-cadence.spec.ts`:

- `round due-ness` describe (pure helpers): deltas → due and first frame → due; `promoted` flip and `willingnessBits` change → due inside the heartbeat, while `arrivalsPerMin` + `timestamp` + `signature` changes alone → not due; unchanged at exactly `heartbeatMs` → due, one ms short → not due; reordered topic summaries → same key.
- `an engine holding a registration sends on a delta or once the heartbeat elapses, and nothing in between` (host level, timer parked): admission round sends with the record; round inside the heartbeat → `undefined`; round after a re-attach touch sends it; unchanged one ms short of the heartbeat → `undefined`; at the heartbeat → a refresh frame with no delta and the topic summary. Fails on the old code (the 7_000 round would have sent).

Adjusted:

- `a gossip round drains a touched record into a signed, coord-scoped frame`: dropped its tail that asserted a resident topic keeps every round non-idle (the old behaviour; now covered by the new host test).
- The two `buildCohortGossip` tests: `heartbeat` input removed; the "idle, no heartbeat → no frame" assertion is gone with the parameter. Frame-content assertions unchanged.
- Timer lifecycle test: now runs with `willingnessHeartbeatMs: 1` so every tick is due. Without it the post-`stop()` "no further dials" assertion would hold vacuously (ticks would have nothing to send).
- A comment in `re-advertisement is bounded to one frame per heartbeat…` corrected (its 31_001 frame also falls on the heartbeat).

## Known gaps / things to look at

- The "~6× fewer verifies" effect is the ticket's arithmetic for the reported shape (about one renewal touch per engine per ping interval), not something measured here. No on-device or two-party profile was taken.
- Load buckets that flap on a busy phone make rounds material more often; worst case is the old one frame per round (accepted by the ticket).
- An idle engine that turns unwilling still announces nothing, so siblings keep its last willing frame — unchanged behaviour, kept on purpose.
- The substrate simulator (`packages/substrate-simulator`) models gossip on its own fixed round and was not changed; if it is meant to mirror host cadence for traffic-staleness questions, it no longer does.
- Docs: besides the sections the ticket named, `docs/cohort-topic.md` §Willingness ("refreshed every gossip round"), §Cold-start instantiation item 3 ("a non-idle engine gossips every round anyway") and `docs/matchmaking.md`'s `queriesPerMin` lag line were also corrected.

## Review findings

Read the implement diff (`ticket(implement): cohort-gossip-sends-a-frame-only-when-something-changed`) before the handoff, then the readers of the gossip view (`createCohortView` in `packages/db-core/src/cohort-topic/gossip/view.ts`, `GossipWillingnessCheck` in `packages/db-core/src/cohort-topic/willingness.ts`, the traffic `snapshot` in `packages/db-core/src/cohort-topic/traffic.ts`) to see what a sibling actually consumes from a frame.

- **Correctness of the round (checked, no defect).** `pending.isEmpty()` and `drain()` read the same three maps, so `hasDeltas` true always yields a non-idle frame and `buildCohortGossip` cannot return `undefined` after a drain; nothing awaits between the check and the drain. A non-sending round leaves the queue intact. A failed `signAndBroadcast` still loses the drained deltas, exactly as before the change. The drain-only-when-queued shape is clear from the comment at the call; kept as is.
- **Liveness (checked, no defect).** Nothing reads a sibling contribution's age: the view keeps the newest contribution with no expiry, the willingness check counts a heard member as heard regardless of age, and the traffic snapshot has no age gate. A membership change moves `cohortEpoch`, which is in the material key, so a rotated cohort hears every engine's frame on the next round rather than after the heartbeat. The new doc paragraph saying so is accurate.
- **Material key covers fields nobody reads (tripwire).** Siblings read only `willingnessBits` and the summaries' `arrivalsPerMin`/`queriesPerMin` from each other; `directParticipants` and `childCohortCount` come from the local store and child registry. `directParticipants` is nevertheless in the key, so an admission or eviction merged into every member makes every member send next round (cohort-size amplification of admission frames, still well under the old every-round rate). Parked as a `NOTE:` at `gossipMaterialKey` with the revisit condition; not changed, since the plan chose the key and dropping it would make the wire summaries siblings hold quietly stale.
- **Stale doc quotes (fixed).** `selfWillingnessBits`'s doc comment and the substrate simulator's `backoff.ts` header quoted the old "refreshed every gossip round" wording of docs/cohort-topic.md §Willingness; updated to the new wording.
- **Simulator cadence (tripwire).** The handoff's gap stands: `packages/substrate-simulator` still models a frame every round. Parked as a `NOTE:` on the `traffic` field in `packages/substrate-simulator/src/topic-tree.ts`; it matters only if the simulator is used to judge traffic staleness.
- **Docs (checked).** Read every section the change touched in docs/cohort-topic.md, docs/internals.md §Registration-record lifecycle and docs/matchmaking.md, and grepped docs and sources for "every round" / "one gossip round" claims; the remaining ones (willingness flip seen within one round, provider arrival observed within one round) are still true because a willingness change is material and an admission is a delta.
- **Tests.** Cut two pure-helper cases (`a round with deltas is due, and so is a first frame`; `an unchanged frame is due once the heartbeat has elapsed, not a millisecond sooner`) that restated `isGossipRoundDue`'s boolean OR and were pinned end to end, boundary included, by the host-level test `an engine holding a registration sends on a delta or once the heartbeat elapses, and nothing in between`. Kept the two material-key cases, which pin which fields are material — the named part of the spec. No new test: no defect was found that one would reproduce.
