Second of four tickets for stage 3 of [docs/offline-sync.md §Implementation stages](../../docs/offline-sync.md#implementation-stages). Implemented by `ticket(implement): offline-sync-coordinator-entry-commit`, reviewed here. The queue (`offline-sync-queue-core`) is the only intended caller.

## What landed

- **`TransactionCoordinator.commitEntry(entry: EntryCommit, options?: SyncOptions): Promise<TransactionDurability>`** in `packages/db-core/src/transaction/coordinator.ts`. The per-entry slice commit: one `HeadCommitHandle` per collection the entry wrote, opened in sorted collection-id order and closed in reverse; an opening refresh round through the handles; per attempt `stage()` on every remaining participant, `entry.recordAttempt` with every participant's `InFlightAttempt` (awaited under every latch), `release()` every handle, the coordinator's own gather/pend/commit/cancel phases over the staged attempts, `retake()` every handle in sorted order inside a `finally`, then the outcome under the retaken latches: `fold` on every committed participant in one await-free pass then `replay` on each; `refused()` on every refused one, every one visited before the first recorded refusal is thrown. A partial landing on returned refusals records the committed participants as saved, closes their handles and throws `CoordinatorStaleLossError` so the shared retry loop re-drives the rest; a partial landing on a hard failure throws `CoordinatorPartialCommitError`. Once anything is saved, every later failure goes through `reportSaved`, as `commit`'s does. Requires `pendValidation: 'none'` (throws before touching anything otherwise). Always answers a `TransactionDurability`, since a slice writes at least one collection; a participant saved by the status-read fallback is listed with an `undefined` class, as `commit` lists one (changed in review, below).
- **`packages/db-core/src/transaction/entry-commit.ts`** (new): `WriteContext` and `writeContextOf(transaction)`; the public `EntryCommit` type (exported from the transaction index); `RefreshParticipant`; and `EntryParticipants`, the lock-step over the handles (open sorted with duplicate, empty and unregistered ids refused as defects before anything is opened and partial opens closed on a throw; `refreshers()`, `stage()`, `retainedAttempts()`, `release()`, `retake()` visiting every handle, `landed()` fold-all-then-replay-each-then-close, `refused()` visiting every handle, `close()` in reverse).
- **The phase refactor.** `applyActionsToCollection`, `coordinateTransaction`, `pendPhase`, `pendTogether`, `pendCollection` and `pendRequestFor` take a `WriteContext` (action id, timestamp, reads, clamped priority) in place of a `Transaction`; `commitOnceLatched` and `execute` derive one per attempt with `writeContextOf`. The phases from the gather on (`coordinateAttempts`, new; `pendPhase`, `commitPhase`, `commitTogether`, `commitEach`, `commitCollection`, `commitRequestFor`) take a per-collection `CollectionAttempt` (transforms, tail id, revision, base revisions, block digests) in place of three parallel maps plus a declared-digests map. `declareTouchedBlocks` builds those attempts from the live trackers; `coordinateTransaction` is declare-then-`coordinateAttempts`, and `commitEntry` calls `coordinateAttempts` directly with attempts read off the `StagedAttempt`s, which `stageAttempt` has already described and size-checked against the head tracker.
- **The retry loop shared.** `commitAttempts` runs over an `AttemptDriver` (attempt, refresh round, nothing-left, opens-with-refresh) with its budget in a `RetryBudget` class; `refreshRounds` is the inner "back off, refresh, keep refreshing while finishing is refused" loop; `refreshBetweenAttempts` takes a list of `RefreshParticipant`s (`registeredRefreshers()` for `commit`, `EntryParticipants.refreshers()` for the slice). `reportSaved` takes an action id and no longer releases the stamp; `commit` releases it on every successful return and when `reportSaved` hands back a partial error. `cleanFailureError` is the one place a nothing-committed failure becomes its error.
- **Docs**: `docs/internals.md` §Staged actions are segments carries the slice's bullet; `docs/transactions.md` names `commitEntry` beside `commit` under the coordinator-centric principle and in the key-files list; `docs/offline-sync.md` §One entry, one network transaction names it. The backlog ticket `debt-multi-collection-retry-cannot-see-the-taken-revision` carries the slice as an arm, and `debt-collection-write-retry-logic-outgrew-its-file` the re-measured line counts.

## Deviations from the implement ticket, each deliberate and confirmed in review

- **The slice does not call `declareTouchedBlocks`.** It reads the LIVE tracker, which under segments holds every sealed segment's effects plus the open segment's, so its digests would declare content the head attempt does not send. The slice carries `StagedAttempt.blockDigests` and `.baseRevs` into the phases unchanged. The reviewer confirmed there is no third place the live tracker is read on the slice's path: `pendRequestFor` reads `attempt.baseRevs`, `commitRequestFor` reads `attempt.blockDigests`, and `applyActionsToCollection` is not on the path.
- **The committed set is recorded in `cycle.saved` BEFORE the local treatment on both partial branches** (`commitEntryOnce`), so a replay that throws inside `landed()` is still reported over the right committed set.
- **The retry loop was generalised rather than copied**, so `commit`'s behaviour depends on the same `RetryBudget` arithmetic as before. The reviewer compared the loss count, the backoff index, the last-chance test, the deadline check and the abort check against the inline loop they replaced: equivalent.
- **A missing collection at the declare step is a thrown defect** rather than a per-collection pend failure; unreachable, since `commitOnceLatched` takes its participants from the registry and `execute` refuses an unregistered collection earlier. The dead-path phase case in `coordinator.spec.ts` was removed.

## Review findings

**What was checked.** The implement diff in full, read before the handoff summary: `coordinator.ts`, `entry-commit.ts`, the index export, both changed specs, the new spec, the three docs and the two backlog arms. The lock-step was checked against the handle's contract in `packages/db-core/src/collection/head-commit.ts` and the handle closure in `Collection.headCommitHandle` (which steps need the latch, when `headInFlight` is set, what `close` tolerates): every step of `commitEntryOnce` runs with the latch state the handle asserts, `retake` is in a `finally`, and every exit of `commitEntry` passes through `participants.close()`. The deadlock argument was checked at all three acquisition sites (`commitOnce`, `execute`, `compareCollectionIds`), which spell the same string order. The success-path fold was checked against a reader's refresh refusing the head while the latches were released: a rival committed at or above the requested revision refuses the attempt, so a committed attempt and a refused head cannot coincide, the same reasoning `driveHeadCommit` relies on. Error paths traced by reading: the write-ahead hook throwing (nothing sent, nothing changed), `stage()` throwing a recorded refusal (earlier participants' staged attempts discarded, nothing sent), a throw out of the phases (retaken, closed, nothing retained, parity with `commitOnceLatched`'s catch), a replay throwing inside `landed()` (committed participants dropped and closed, reported as partial because the saved set was recorded first), `refused()` throwing a recorded refusal on the partial branch (propagates as that refusal and is wrapped as a partial commit by `reportSaved`). The stamp handling in `commit` was checked equivalent to the old `reportSaved` release. The interaction with a whole-collection `commit` over sealed segments is already refused by `getPendingActions` (documented), so the slice's absence of a stamp is safe. Ran: `yarn workspace @optimystic/db-core typecheck` (clean), `eslint` over the two changed source files (clean; specs are outside the lint config's scope), `yarn lint:docs` (48 documents, all resolve), the full db-core suite (1966 passing, 18 s), then `yarn workspace @optimystic/db-core build` and `yarn workspace @optimystic/quereus-plugin-optimystic typecheck` against the rebuilt output (clean). db-p2p and plugin suites were not re-run: nothing in either names the changed signatures, and the plugin typecheck covers the public surface.

**Minor, fixed in this pass.**

- `commitEntry` answered `undefined` when every participant was saved by the status-read fallback of `Collection.completeOwnEntry`. That contradicted the documented meaning of `undefined` from a coordinator commit ("nothing was written") and made the return type optional for a path that always writes at least one collection. It now always answers a `TransactionDurability`, typed `Promise<TransactionDurability>`, with such a participant listed with an `undefined` class and `overall` absent, exactly as `commit` lists one; the three-line `holdersUnknown` helper is gone and a cycle that somehow ended with nothing written throws as a defect. The queue ticket `offline-sync-queue-core` already expects that shape (it wraps `commitHead`'s `undefined` with `transactionDurability` itself), so nothing downstream changes. `docs/internals.md` updated.
- The abort signal was not checked before the opening refresh round, so an already-aborted call still paid a network refresh before `assertOpen` refused the attempt. `commitAttempts` now calls `budget.assertOpen()` ahead of the opening round.
- The acquisition-order `NOTE:` in `execute` named two sites to change together; it now names the third, `compareCollectionIds` in `entry-commit.ts`.
- One assertion in the hard-failure case of `coordinator-entry-commit.spec.ts` pinned the commit phase's transient retry count (exactly three throws), which is the commit phase's policy, not this ticket's contract; loosened to "at least two", which still says B's commit was retried before being given up on.

**Major findings.** None. No class-level defect was found; the two gaps the implementer named (no tail floor reaches the slice's refresh rounds, and `RetryBudget` being a second copy of the retry accounting) are already arms on `debt-multi-collection-retry-cannot-see-the-taken-revision` and `debt-collection-write-retry-logic-outgrew-its-file`, each re-measured by the implement commit. No ticket filed.

**Tripwires.** None new. The existing ones that apply were confirmed in place: the `NOTE:` on `refreshBetweenAttempts` (no tail floor, naming the backlog ticket), the `NOTE:` at the handle's `replay` (a drain is quadratic in the queue length), and the `NOTE:` on `registeredRefreshers` (a transaction's round refreshes every registered collection; the slice refreshes its participants only).

**Considered and declined.** `landed()` closing the committed handles itself on the success path, leaving the outer `finally`'s `close()` a no-op: kept, because it keeps "the participant set is what is left to commit" true at every point, and the handle's `close` is idempotent by contract. Unifying `RetryBudget` with `AttemptCycle`: already tracked on the file-size debt ticket, and the shapes differ (no stall check, no `staleAt`).

**Tests.** The five cases in `packages/db-core/test/coordinator-entry-commit.spec.ts` each pin a contract over real `Tree`s and a real `TestTransactor`, with doubles that decide only when an answer returns or how much of it lands: pend refusal leaves nothing durable and the recorded refusal is thrown; both participants commit as one action with the entry's timestamp; the latches are released during the round trip; a partial landing on a hard failure and its re-drive; a recorded attempt resumes a torn participant with and without `resume`. None restates the implementation or verifies a mock, and no new test was needed for the fixes above (the return-type change is a type, the abort check is a one-line guard the existing abort coverage of `commit` already exercises through the shared loop).

**Not covered, by inspection.** `reportSaved` after a refresh saved a participant (reused unchanged); a head refused while the round trip is in flight (the one-collection case in `collection-segments.spec.ts` pins the re-check order, and `refused()` runs through the same handle); the `pendValidation` guard (a one-line throw).
