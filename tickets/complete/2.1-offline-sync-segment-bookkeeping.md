## What landed

**The segment list.** `SegmentList` in `packages/db-core/src/collection/segments.ts` holds the sealed segments (one `SealedSegment` per queue entry, oldest first, each with its entry id, its actions and an optional recorded refusal), the open segment, and a `version` counter bumped on every change to the sealed list. The sealed list is private; every change goes through `seal` (answers `false` and changes nothing for an empty open segment, throws for a duplicate id), `drop` (anywhere in the list, throws for an unknown id) and `refuse`, and it is read through `find`, `head`, `entryIds`, `hasSealed`, `replayable` and `replayableCount`. The collection's old single pending list is gone; while nothing is sealed the open segment is that list and every path behaves as before.

**The refusal class.** `ActionRefusedError` in `packages/db-core/src/collection/struct.ts` carries the collection id and the message. `TreeGuardRefusedError` extends it, so every tree guard refusal is one; `classifyCommitFailure` and the Quereus bridge keep matching the tree class.

**The collection's new members.** `sealPending(entryId): boolean` (synchronous, latch-free, caller brackets it), `sealedEntries()`, `sealedRefusal(entryId)`, `dropSealed(entryId)` (takes the latch, drops, then replays), and the constructor-time `onSealedRefused` callback in `CollectionInitOptions`, threaded through both open paths. `Tree.open` and `Tree.createOrOpen` take a trailing optional `options: TreeCollectionOptions` carrying that callback.

**Segment-aware replay.** `replayActions` resets the tracker to the invented baseline (the header, empty log block and root `createOrOpen`'s create branch staged, copied after `Log.open`) while the collection holds no committed revision, and to empty otherwise. It then stages each non-refused sealed segment through one `internalTransact` call over all of its actions, so a refusal discards the whole segment's writes. A throw of `ActionRefusedError` from a sealed segment is recorded on the segment, logged as `collection:sealed-segment-refused` and reported once through the callback; a throw out of the callback is logged as `collection:refusal-callback-threw`. The open segment stages one action at a time with a refusal propagating, as before.

**Counts and refusals.** `mustReplay`, `restageIfBasesMoved` and the invalidation conjunct in `updateInternal` count every replayable action. `sync`, `updateAndSync`, `actAndSync` and `getPendingActions` throw `SealedSegmentsError` (collection id and sealed entry ids) while any segment is sealed, before anything is minted or staged; the coordinator's existing catch restores every participant. `clearPendingActions`, `filterAgainstEntry`, `consumeOwnEntry` and `unstage` touch the open segment only.

**The success-branch reorder in `syncAttempts`.** The committed transforms are folded into the cache and the revision adopted before the replay. The order is load-bearing for an invented collection's first commit: a replay first would reinstate the baseline's header over the committed one and `hasUnsyncedChanges` would never turn false.

**Docs.** `docs/internals.md` has the subsection *Staged actions are segments*. `docs/offline-sync.md` §The segment model lists the settled members and the `SealedSegmentsError` rule; §Replay with a per-segment refusal names `ActionRefusedError`. The stage status line is left for the third ticket.

## Tests

`packages/db-core/test/collection-segments.spec.ts`, all over `TestTransactor` and `Tree` with a real rival handle where a refusal has to be provoked: the `SegmentList` version rule; `sealPending` over an empty open segment; a refresh refusing a sealed segment (recorded, reported once, listed, the read sees the rival, logged); no re-report on a later refresh; atomic staging of a three-action segment; an open-segment refusal propagating; a throwing callback; `dropSealed` of a non-head segment cascading a dependent segment's refusal; an invented collection replaying from its baseline and then settling on its first commit; and the whole-collection commits refusing with every participant restored.

## Review findings

Read the implement diff (`ticket(implement): offline-sync-segment-bookkeeping`) before the handoff.

**Checked, no defect:**
- **Atomicity of a sealed segment's replay.** `internalTransact` runs every handler against one `Atomic` and commits it last, so a throw mid-segment leaves nothing in the live tracker and no pins in the shared store (the atomic's pins are its own). The replay's catch is narrowed to `ActionRefusedError`; any other error propagates, half-rebuilt tracker and all, which the existing backlog `debt-a-failed-refresh-can-leave-a-collection-half-restaged` already covers.
- **The invented baseline.** Captured after `Log.open` so the header carries the tail id; read only while no context is adopted, so an invented collection that later finds the collection committed remotely correctly drops its invented header (the empty reset) when the walk adopts a revision. `Collection.open` and the existing-collection branch of `createOrOpen` leave it undefined. `unstage` with an unmoved revision reinstates the pre-call transforms and never consults it.
- **The coordinator path.** `getPendingActions` throws inside `commitOnceLatched`'s try, after the pre-commit snapshots and after `restageIfBasesMoved`; the catch restores every participant from those snapshots, which the test confirms with a clean sibling. A replay run by `restageIfBasesMoved` before the throw is an ordinary re-stage, not a change the restore has to undo.
- **Refused segments and later refreshes.** `mustReplay`'s conflict arm replays even when the replayable count is zero, which is right: the refused segments contributed nothing to the tracker, and the reset drops only what the walk superseded.
- **Public types.** `TreeGuardRefusedError`'s constructor and its `collectionId` field are unchanged for callers; every subclass still passes `(collectionId, key, message)`. The plugin's bridge and `classifyCommitFailure` match the tree class and are untouched. db-core, the plugin and db-p2p all typecheck.
- **Resource and latch handling.** `dropSealed` releases in `finally`; the callback runs synchronously under the latch and its contract (no latched call from inside it) is written at the option, at the constructor and in internals.

**Fixed inline (minor):**
- `SegmentList.sealed` was a public mutable field, so a caller could change the sealed list without bumping `version`, the one thing the next ticket's restore branches on. Made it private and added `hasSealed()`; `Collection.assertNoSealedSegments` uses it.
- `TreeCollectionOptions` carried two type parameters it never used (the callback's type does not depend on the action type). Now a non-generic alias.
- `hasUnsyncedChanges` now states at its site the gap the handoff raised: a sealed segment whose actions were all refused, or wrote nothing (a blind delete of an unseen key), leaves no tracker transform, so the predicate reads `false` while `sealedEntries()` is non-empty. The queue drives off `sealedEntries()`.
- Stale documentation: the own-entry bullet in `docs/internals.md` still said consuming drops actions "off the head of `pending`", a field that no longer exists; `packages/db-core/docs/collections.md` still showed the class sketch, the update sketch and the sync sketch over the old single list, with the sync sketch's success branch in the pre-reorder order that is now wrong. All updated, with a paragraph pointing at the internals subsection.

**Tripwires recorded (not tickets):**
- `NOTE:` at the `TreeGuardRefusedError` arm of `classifyCommitFailure` in `packages/db-core/src/transaction/commit-failure-class.ts`: it matches the tree's class rather than the new base `ActionRefusedError`, because the `constraint` / `concurrent-modification` split is tree vocabulary and the tree is the only collection type that refuses. If another type ever throws an `ActionRefusedError`, widen that arm or its refusal sorts as `unknown` and the drain retries a final failure.
- The handoff's `NOTE:` at `CollectionSnapshot.pending` stands: a restore across a change to the sealed list reinstates `transforms` verbatim, and the moved-boundary arm with an empty captured open segment resets the tracker empty, dropping sealed effects. No caller seals yet, and `offline-sync-open-segment-snapshot` (already in `implement/`) owns the version-aware restore, so no ticket was filed.

**Tests:** none cut. The `SegmentList` version test pins the contract the next ticket's restore branches on, which `Collection` does not yet expose; every other case exercises a specification rule or a defect found during implementation (the invented-baseline case). None added: the defects I looked for were not there.

**Considered and declined:** none of the touched sites carries an accepted-tradeoff `NOTE:` relevant to these findings.

**Size:** `packages/db-core/src/collection/collection.ts` measures 2740 lines (`wc -l`), up from 2556. Backlog `debt-collection-write-retry-logic-outgrew-its-file` already claims the file and the third ticket plans the shared-machinery extraction, so no second ticket.
