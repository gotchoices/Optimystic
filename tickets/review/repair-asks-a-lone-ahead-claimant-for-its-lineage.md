# Repair asks a lone ahead claimant for its lineage — implemented

## What landed

Both block-repair paths now run a second, narrow round for the one shape the quorum selector cannot settle on the claim data alone: a single-signer certified claim (one machine's receipt for its own solo commit) strictly ahead of a pair backed by more than one machine. The claimants of the lone pair are asked whether their own history contains the group's pair, through an injected callback over `IBlockStorage.lineageOf`; an `excludes` answer marks every claim at the lone pair `forkedFromCohort`, the selector sets marked claims aside, and the group's pair wins by the existing rules. `contains` and no usable answer leave the lone pair winning as before, which keeps the ordinary lagging-cohort repair working. The callback is optional at both call sites and absent in production until `the-sync-consult-answers-a-lineage-question` supplies it; without it every selection is byte-identical to before (the existing suites pass unchanged).

Where it lives:

- `packages/db-p2p/src/cluster/quorum-restore.ts`: `RevClaim.forkedFromCohort`; `selectQuorumRev` and `certifiedEquivocation` filter marked claims first (`weighable`) — a marked claim is not a responder, not a corroborator and not certified; the pure shape detector `soloAheadOfCohort` and its result type `SoloAheadShape`. The equal-revision `NOTE:` is retired.
- `packages/db-p2p/src/cluster/solo-ahead.ts` (new): `BlockLineageAsk`, `SoloAheadVerdict`, `settleSoloAhead` — detect, ask each claimant in parallel, fold, mark in place; a throwing ask is recorded as a `silent` claimant and folded as `unknown`.
- `packages/db-p2p/src/cluster/reconcile-block.ts`: `ReconcileBlockDeps.askLineage?`; the round runs after `certifyCandidates` and before `selectQuorumRev`; logs `reconcile:fork-declined` / `reconcile:solo-ahead` with the whole verdict (both pairs, claimants, each answer).
- `packages/db-p2p/src/repo/coordinator-repo.ts`: `blockLineageCallback?` on the factory's components type and as the last constructor parameter (existing positional callers unaffected); `settleSoloAheadClaims` runs after the certify pass and before selection, each ask under `withDeadline(cohortQueryTimeoutMs)`, outside the consult seat; logs `cluster-fetch:fork-declined` / `cluster-fetch:solo-ahead`.
- Docs: the *One-peer proofs* paragraph and the first bullet of *The certified exception* in `docs/internals.md`, and the "A claim needs a second, independent peer" bullet in `docs/transactions.md`, now state the rule, the three shapes it leaves undecided, and the stalled-write cost the group pays instead of a silently lost write. The `debt-repair-cannot-tell-a-fork-from-a-lagging-cohort` citations are gone. ## Two decisions the reviewer should check

**The shape is defined through the selector, not restated beside it.** The ticket described the lone pair as "the top certified revision ... with no corroborated pair at or above it". Read literally that excludes the ticket's own pushed-replica edge case (the lone machine pushed its revision to a second peer: two supporters at the lone pair make it a corroborated group). `soloAheadOfCohort` instead asks the selector twice: the shape fires when `selectQuorumRev` currently picks a certified pair certified only by single-signer proofs, and, with every claim at that pair set aside, picks a pair backed by more than one machine (`CORROBORATION_FLOOR`-plus distinct supporters, or a multi-signer proof). The cohort pair named is therefore by construction the one that wins after a `fork` mark. Consequences worth verifying: a group's side that is itself an equivocation decline (two multi-signer pairs at the same revision below the solo) yields no shape, which is today's behaviour; a single-signer certified pair below the solo (two lone machines) is not a group's side; a quorum-meeting group of one supporter (capacity-one cohort) is not a group's side. Pinned in the `soloAheadOfCohort` describe.

**A forked claimant is handed to the read path's acquisition as "served nothing".** `queryClusterForLatest` records each forked claimant in `consulted` as `undefined`. Without that, `restoreCorroborated` hands the acquisition (the reconcile callback) the solo's archive with the group's pair as `committed`; the reconcile keeps archives at or above that revision as candidates, so it would re-run the lineage question (a second request to the same claimants), and on a reconcile wired without `askLineage` it would select and persist the fork the consult just declined. The forked claimant cannot serve the group's pair anyway (its archive is the fork's revision). Documented at `ClusterLatestQuery.consulted`. The reviewer may prefer threading the verdict through instead; the exclusion is the smaller change.

## Use cases to validate

- **A joiner (holds nothing)** consults, the shape fires, the claimant answers `excludes` → the group's pair is selected and acquired from its supporters; the joiner lands on the group's lineage. Covered at the reconcile tier (its claim set also has no local revision).
- **A group member at the group's pair** → `fork` → selection at the reader's revision → `cluster-fetch:local-current`, nothing acquired, no `unconfirmedAheadRev`. Pinned on the coordinator spec.
- **The lone machine itself** reading: its own claim is excluded from its consult, nothing certified sits above the group's pair, the shape does not fire. By inspection only.
- **The pushed replica**: both claimants asked, the replica's `unknown` beside the originator's `excludes` folds to `fork`, both claims marked, so the pair cannot win as a two-supporter corroborated group either. Pinned in `solo-ahead.spec.ts` and the detector describe.
- **`same-lineage`** → the lone pair wins, with its proof persisted on the reconcile path (the content gate needs the solo's proof to declare a digest, since a single carrier otherwise fails the content quorum; the spec mints one that does).
