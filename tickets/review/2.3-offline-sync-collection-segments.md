Third of three tickets for stage 2 of [docs/offline-sync.md §Implementation stages](../../docs/offline-sync.md#implementation-stages), which the doc now marks implemented. The design is [§The drain does not hold the collection latch across the network](../../docs/offline-sync.md#the-drain-does-not-hold-the-collection-latch-across-the-network); the rules as built are the new sixth bullet of [docs/internals.md §Staged actions are segments](../../docs/internals.md#staged-actions-are-segments). The reviewer should treat this as a starting point: the retry loop is the most delicate path in the package and it was restructured here.

## What landed

**The shared attempt machinery** is a new module, `packages/db-core/src/collection/write-attempts.ts`. `AttemptCycle` holds one write's retry accounting (the consecutive-failure budget, the stall check and its evidence, the three refusals stopped by name, the durability folded across commits) and its `refreshUntilSettled` loop (back off, refresh, repeat while finishing a half-landed write is refused for a cause that can clear); `stageAttempt` builds one attempt (a per-attempt tracker over a copy of the target's transforms sharing its pins, the log append under a fixed `WriteIdentity`, the digests, the size check); `retainedAttempt` is what a refused attempt hands to `retainInFlightAttempt`. `Collection.syncAttempts` is now about forty lines over that machinery and `Collection.commitHead`'s cycle about thirty; each decides only what an attempt is built from and what to do with a commit. `retryBackoffMs` and the four default constants moved with it. Two citations in `docs/correctness.md` and `docs/transactions.md` were retargeted to the new file.

**`Collection.commitHead(entryId, options)`** with `HeadCommitOptions` (`SyncOptions` plus the queue's `actionId` and `timestamp`) in `struct.ts`, and `SealedSegmentsError` extended with `reason` (`whole-collection` for the existing throw, `not-head`, `refused`, `in-flight`) and an optional `entryId`. The cycle: preconditions; mark the action in flight and the head entry (`headCommitEntry`) for the whole cycle; a refresh first, through the shared loop, which with the mark set recognises an earlier attempt's own entry (the queue's re-drive of an in-doubt entry) and finishes or consumes it; then attempts until the head is committed. Each attempt runs on a head tracker (`headTrackerFor` / `buildHeadTracker`): the head segment's actions staged atomically over the committed state, from the invented baseline while there is no committed revision, into a `Tracker` over the handle's cache with pins of its own, built once per requested revision, re-judged before each attempt the way `restageIfBasesMoved` re-judges the live queue (`rereadUnretainedBases`, now shared), and rebuilt on a moved base or a new revision. The one `await` on `source.transact` is made with the latch released (`sendUnlatched`), `headInFlight` set for exactly that window and cleared right after the retake. The head's recorded refusal is checked before every attempt and after every retake (`committableHead`) and thrown in place of a retry. On success: the head is dropped (asserted still first), `foldOwnCommit` folds the attempt's transforms and adopts the revision through `recordCommitted`, then `replayActions`.

**The three hooks the released latch needs.** `updateInternal` returns before `forgetAndAdopt`, changing nothing, when `headInFlight` is set and the walk holds that action's entry (`collection:refresh-deferred-to-head-commit`); the check sits ahead of `ownEntry` and is gated on the window field, so the head commit's own refreshes take the own-entry path. `consumeOwnEntry` has a head arm (`consumeHead`, keyed on `headCommitEntry`): assert the head is that entry and the action counts agree, then drop it, leaving the open segment alone. `dropSealed` of the in-flight head throws `in-flight`.

**Test double.** `RefusesPendsAsShortfall` in `src/testing/test-transactor.ts` answers every pend with a promise shortfall and delegates nothing.

**Docs.** `docs/internals.md`: the segments subsection's fourth bullet names the head arm, a sixth bullet describes the head commit in full, and the three Key Invariants bullets that named `syncAttempts` alone (the fixed-function log append, the moved-base re-stage, the in-flight id) now name `commitHead` and `write-attempts.ts`. `docs/offline-sync.md`: status line and stage 2 marked implemented, `commitHead`'s signature and return corrected in §The segment model, and §The drain does not hold the collection latch rewritten for the head tracker, the deferred refresh, the rival case, `dropSealed` and the shared module. `yarn lint:docs` is clean.

## Decisions the ticket left open, and how they went

- **`commitHead` refreshes before its first attempt**, like `updateAndSync`, not like bare `sync`. The ticket's "head refused on rebase before send" case requires it: with the live replay finding the rival on that refresh, the segment is refused and reported once, the recorded `TreeKeyTakenError` is thrown, and nothing is sent. It is also what makes the queue's in-doubt re-drive work without a second entry point: the refresh runs with the supplied action id already marked. The cost is one request per entry when nothing changed.
- **The fold is of the attempt's transforms, not the head tracker's alone.** The ticket's text says `transformCache(headTracker.transforms, newRev)`. That would leave the cache's log tail block without the entry just appended (the append lives only in the per-attempt tracker), and the next head commit's `Log.open` through the cache would append over a tail that lacks it. `foldOwnCommit` folds `staged.tracker.reset()`, exactly what `syncAttempts` folds. The tests over two consecutive head commits pin this.
- **`refreshUntilSettled` sleeps 0 ms when no attempt has failed yet** (the refresh that opens a head commit cycle). `jitteredBackoffMs` throws on a negative attempt index, so the pre-existing sync path, which only enters the loop after a refusal, is unchanged.
- **The success path does not re-check the refusal.** A reader's refresh in the window can refuse the head only by adopting a rival at the revision our attempt requested, which makes the attempt stale, not successful; so on success the head is dropped without looking at `refused`. On failure it is checked before the refusal is accounted for.
- **Precondition order** is `not-head`, then `refused`, then `in-flight`. A second `commitHead` of the in-flight entry gets `in-flight`; of a later entry it gets `not-head`.

## Tests added, each beside what it verifies

In `packages/db-core/test/collection-segments.spec.ts`, `describe('commitHead …')`, all over `Tree` and the real doubles:

- *releases the latch while the commit is on the network…* (`GatedCommitTransactor`): `stage` resolves during the window (raced against a 2 s timer); a second `commitHead` and a `dropSealed` of the head throw `in-flight`; after the gate the committed log holds the head alone, the open action is still staged, and a read sees both.
- *refuses a segment that is not the head, and commits each head in turn…*: the `not-head` precondition changes nothing; after E1, E2 is the head and is re-staged readable (its actions never consumed); after E2, the log holds both in order.
- *a reader's refresh that walks into the in-flight entry defers…* (`GatedCommitTransactor`): the log line, held revision unchanged, head still sealed, no refusal; after the commit the next refresh adopts nothing more and the head is logged once.
- *a rival that lands while the attempt is in flight…* (`GatedPendTransactor`): a reader's `update()` in the window proceeds and adopts the rival; the attempt is refused; the retry commits the head rebuilt at the adopted revision; two pends; the log is rival then head; a fresh reader sees both.
- *a head attempt whose tail landed but was refused…* (`TailLandsButReportsStale`, with `stage` queued on the latch so it runs in the window): one tear, a defined durability from the refresh's finish, the HEAD consumed and the open action still staged, the segment behind the head intact and never consumed, the head logged once.
- *a head refused on rebase before anything is sent…* (`RecordsAttemptsRefusesFirstCommit` armed to refuse nothing): `TreeKeyTakenError` thrown, reported once, zero pends recorded; a second call meets the `refused` precondition without reporting again.
- *a head refused by a rival that landed while the attempt was in flight…* (`GatedPendTransactor`): one pend, the rival adopted, the recorded refusal thrown with no retry.
- *a snapshot restored across a head commit takes the replay arm…*: nothing staged afterwards, the committed segment reads through, the open action is gone, a following `sync` commits nothing more.
- *an invented collection commits its header, log and root with the head…*: `Tree.open` over the transactor finds the collection with E1 and not E2; E2 is re-staged readable; `committedRevision()` is 1.
- *a promise shortfall out of a head attempt is thrown by name…* (`RefusesPendsAsShortfall`): one pend, every segment and the open action still staged, no refusal recorded. Expiry and validity rejection share `AttemptCycle.recordRefusal` with this case and with the existing sync specs, so they carry no head-commit case of their own.

In `packages/db-core/test/retry-resends-identical-transforms.spec.ts`: *head commit path: a retry re-sends the same entry, under the supplied action id and timestamp* (`RecordsAttemptsRefusesFirstCommit`, the head inserting a fresh block): the two attempts' transforms are deep-equal, the action id is the supplied one, the single log entry carries the supplied timestamp. The overflow variant was not added: `commitHead` reaches `logAppendBlockIds` through the same `stageAttempt` the collection path does.

By inspection only, as the ticket allowed: abort and deadline through the shared loop; `dropSealed` of a segment behind the in-flight head; `sync` over a collection whose segments were all committed (the existing suites).

## Known gaps and things worth a reviewer's eye

- **A throw out of the head commit's own refresh after it finished the entry** (an invalidation read failing, or the OPEN segment's replay refusing) leaves the head consumed, the revision adopted and the live tracker half re-staged — the same shape backlog `debt-a-failed-refresh-can-leave-a-collection-half-restaged` records for a refresh; `commitHead` then throws that error, not a durability. Not new, not fixed.
- **`commitHead` answers `undefined` for a write the status-read fallback recognised as saved** (the queue's re-drive of an entry whose earlier attempt fully landed, with no retained attempt). That is the documented return, but the queue (stage 3) must not read `undefined` as "nothing written".
- **The deferred refresh is gated on the in-flight action id being in the walk.** A reader's refresh that reads the log tail before our commit lands and walks nothing is unaffected; one that lands between our tail commit and our data sweep on a real network sees our entry and defers, which is the intended shape. I did not build a case where a reader's refresh and a rival's entry both sit in one deferred walk; the argument that our entry is always first above the held revision is in the comment at the check.
- **`GatedPendTransactor` needs `collection` assigned** for its snapshots; the two cases that use it do so. A future case that forgets gets a `TypeError` from inside `pend`, which reads as a refused attempt.
- **The quadratic replay tripwire** is the `NOTE:` at the `replayActions` call in `headCommitAttempts`, as the ticket asked.
- **What remains of `debt-collection-write-retry-logic-outgrew-its-file`** is appended to that ticket: `TransactionCoordinator.commit` still has its own copy of the refresh-until-settled loop, the own-entry machinery and the in-flight state (two fields larger now) stay in `collection.ts`, and the file is a net fourteen lines shorter only because the head commit it gained is about the size of what left.
- `Tree` and `Diary` expose no `commitHead` wrapper; the tests call `tree.getCollection().commitHead(...)`. The queue in stage 3 is the first production caller and will decide where it wants the surface.
