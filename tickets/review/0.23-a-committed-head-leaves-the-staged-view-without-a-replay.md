GitHub #53, the remedy the `NOTE:` at the head commit's `replay` step and docs/offline-sync.md §Failure modes and accepted tradeoffs both named. The implement ticket's design was built as specified with one structural deviation, described under *What differs from the ticket's design*.

## What was built

**Each staged action records what it staged.** `Collection.stageInto` returns the atomic's transforms beside the tally (`Tracker.absorb` and `Atomic.commit` now return the child's transforms, which the parent copied what it kept of, so nothing else references them). `Collection.actInternal` records them on the open segment as one `StagedUnit` per action: the action, its delta and its tally. `SegmentList.seal` folds the units' deltas into one (`foldTransforms` in `packages/db-core/src/transform/helpers.ts`, under the tracker's own semantics: an update of a block the delta inserts is applied into the block, a delete removes the insert and the updates) and sums the tallies, so a sealed segment carries `SealedSegment.delta` and `SealedSegment.applied` from staging, with no rebuild. `Collection.replayActions` records every delta again under the ids it mints, and bumps the collection's replay epoch first.

**A head attempt is built from the delta.** `Collection.headTrackerFor` first tries `Collection.headTrackerFromDelta`: a tracker over the read cache with a copy of the delta and the live tracker's pins for the blocks the delta updates, re-judged like any head tracker (`rereadUnretainedBases`, then `movedBases`). It falls back to running the handlers (`Collection.buildHeadTracker`, unchanged) when the delta is not on record, when a pin under it has moved, or while the collection holds no committed revision (an invented collection's first commit carries the baseline). A resumed attempt keeps its branch. The handle's `stage` step records whether the attempt came from the delta and in which epoch.

**A committed head is subtracted.** The handle's `replay` step takes `Collection.subtractCommittedHead` when the attempt was built from the delta and the epoch has not moved since: the remaining segments' and open units' deltas are collected (`SegmentList.stagedDeltas`), each touched block a remaining delta updates and the cache no longer retains is read through the cache first, then in one synchronous step `viewChanged` and `Tracker.refold`, which forgets the touched ids in the live tracker and stages them again from the remaining deltas in order, pinning an update-only block's base from the cache as it is now. Otherwise, and when any remaining delta is not on record, the old full replay runs, logged `collection:committed-head-replayed` with the reason; the fast path logs `collection:committed-head-subtracted`.

**Snapshots carry the units and the epoch.** `CollectionSnapshot.pending` is now the open units (shared; a unit is immutable) and `replayEpoch` is recorded beside `sealedVersion`. `Collection.restorePending` takes the replay arm when a replay ran while a segment is sealed, since the captured transforms would otherwise put back block ids the segments' recorded deltas no longer name, and the next head commit would take the wrong blocks out of the tracker. The verbatim arm restores the units with their deltas.

## What differs from the ticket's design

The ticket proposed one `openDelta` accumulator plus `openApplied` on `SegmentList`, folded into on every `act` and shared by the snapshot. Folding into a shared accumulator would have needed the tracker's copy-before-change discipline a second time (an update of an inserted block mutates the block; an update list grows), so the open segment keeps one immutable unit per action instead, and the fold happens once, at the seal. A snapshot copies the unit array (references), a restore puts it back, and nothing is ever changed in place. A group staged by one `act` call over several actions records its delta on the group's last action, the earlier ones recording an empty delta; nothing slices inside a group (every caller stages one action per call today). `unseal` puts the actions back with no delta on record, because the segment holds the folded delta only; the enqueue that unseals is failing and the rollback that follows replays, which records them again.

Everything that set `segments.open` directly now goes through `SegmentList.setOpen` or `pushOpen`, and `Collection.openActions` / `getPendingActions` return a new array per call rather than the list's own. `Collection.filterAgainstEntry` keeps a kept action's unit and gives a replacement a unit with no delta, which the replay it forces records.

### For GitHub #53

The queue drain no longer re-applies every queued transaction after each one is sent. Each queued transaction keeps the changes it staged, a sent one is taken out of the staged view in place, and the per-entry drain cost on the reporter's shape is flat at about 6 ms whether 100, 200 or 400 transactions are queued, where it was 65 to 106 ms and growing with the queue. Back-to-back offline commits with a file journal now cost 12 to 17 ms each throughout a run of 200 instead of climbing from 12 to 76, and a commit that waits for its own send costs about 9 ms against 8 ms online. What did not change: the journal still flushes to disk three times per entry and the commit waits for the first, which is the acknowledgement's promise (backlog `feat-a-committed-drain-entry-can-settle-without-waiting-for-disk`), and the report's baseline kept blocks in memory; with blocks on disk an online commit costs 40 to 80 ms here, more than an offline acknowledgement. Measured with `packages/quereus-plugin-optimystic/test/offline-commit-cost.bench.mjs` on a solo libp2p node, Node 24, Windows.

## What the reviewer should look at

- `Tracker.refold` and `foldTransforms` are two implementations of the same semantics, one into a tracker and one into a plain `Transforms`; both mirror `Tracker.absorb`'s order (deletes, inserts, updates). `foldTransforms` matches `Tracker.insert` in not removing a stale update list when a block is inserted over one (the malformed state the `tryGet` note describes); worth a second opinion on whether that should differ.
- The pin rebase in `Tracker.refold` is the correctness argument of the ticket: an update-only block the head touched gets the cache's revision after the fold. The property spec checks the declared bases against storage on every attempt, and the committed tree against the model, but the `TestTransactor` does not enforce declared bases, so a wrong base would show only through those two checks.
- `subtractCommittedHead` visits the head's delta's ids, not the attempt's. A tail roll-over that rewrote the header while a later segment holds a header update leaves that segment's pin judged moved, and the next refresh or head commit replays; the `NOTE:` at the method says so. Unmeasured; at most once per 32 entries.
- `foldTransforms` copies an inserted block once per later unit that updates it, at the seal (`NOTE:` at the function). `SealedSegment.delta` holds each segment's staged content a second time while queued (`NOTE:` at the field).
- `Collection.replayActions` clears every open unit's delta before re-staging and records them one by one, so a throw mid-way leaves the units behind it with none on record (`NOTE:` at the method), on top of the half-restaged tracker the callers' note already accepts.
- `Collection.openActions` returns a copy per call; `OfflineMode` calls it once per outlook judgement and once at commit.
