Stage 5 of [docs/offline-sync.md §Implementation stages](../../docs/offline-sync.md#implementation-stages), part two of three: the key-value journal behind db-core's `IOfflineJournal` port (part one, `offline-sync-journal-port`, landed). Nothing reads the journal back yet; that is part three (`offline-sync-journal-restore`, in `implement/`, which now carries a "Notes from part two" section with what it needs from this code).

## What was built

**Registration** (`parseOfflineConfig` in `offline-config.ts`). Two new keys under `offline`:

- `journal`: an `IKVStore` or a zero-argument function returning one, called once inside `register` (a throw propagates out of `register`). Checked by shape — `get`, `set`, `delete`, `list` must be functions, `flush` a function when present — with an error naming what is missing. A `MemoryKVStore` (`instanceof`) is refused with a message saying to leave `journal` off. A store without `flush` is accepted.
- `journalLabel`: `/^[A-Za-z0-9_-]+$/` (`JOURNAL_KEY_SEGMENT`, shared with the journal), default `'default'`, refused without `journal`.
- The host-facing `OfflineConfig` type allows the factory; `parseOfflineConfig` returns `ParsedOfflineConfig` (journal resolved), which `OfflineMode` takes.

**The journal** (`OfflineJournal` in `offline-journal.ts`). Records under `optimystic-offline/<label>/`:

| Key | Written | Value |
|---|---|---|
| `entries/<seq12>` | at enqueue, flushed | `EntryRecordV1`: `v: 1`, `transactionId`, `seq`, `queuedAt`, `expiresAt?`, `segments: [{ collectionId, table: { schemaName, tableName }, actions }]` |
| `drives/<seq12>` | before each send, after a partial commit / identity renewal, flushed | `DriveRecordV1`: `v: 1`, `transactionId`, `actionId`, `attempts` as pairs, `committed` as pairs (`null` for unknown holders) |
| `outcomes/<transactionId>` | at a terminal outcome, flushed, then the other two deleted (not flushed) | `OutcomeRecordV1`: `v: 1`, `at`, `status: PersistedOutcome` |

`encodeOutcome` / `decodeOutcome` sit beside each other; a decoded `committed` has `followed: false`, a decoded `failed` carries a plain `Error` (name + message, no `cause`) — stated on `TransactionOutcome` in `offline-status.ts`.

**`OfflineMode`** builds the journal and passes it as `OfflineQueueOptions.journal`. `persisted: boolean` is on the plugin's `queued` status and on `QueuedTransaction` (`true` exactly when a journal is configured). `acknowledge` and `pruneOutcomes` delete outcome records, fire-and-forget and logged (`offline:journal-outcome-delete-failed`).

**One translation per settled entry.** The journal's `settled` calls back `OfflineMode.journalOutcome`, which translates the db-core status (`translateOutcome`, split out of `translate`; its `committed` arm calls `TransactionBridge.reportCommitDurability`) and caches it in `journaledOutcomes`; `onQueueStatus` takes the cached translation through `outcomeOf` instead of translating again. So `onCommitDurability` fires once per drained commit, one call earlier than without a journal and still before the `committed` status is delivered. The retained map and the outcome record share the same `at`.

**db-p2p.** `OFFLINE_JOURNAL_KEY_PREFIX = 'optimystic-offline/'` in `node-kv-namespaces.ts`, added to `NODE_KV_NAMESPACES` (the fs README's list and the `FileKVStore` comment updated). `PowerLossKVStore` in `src/testing/power-loss-kv-store.ts`, exported from `@optimystic/db-p2p/testing`.

**Unchanged and why.** db-core is untouched. `TransactionBridge.sealIntoQueue` already awaited `OfflineMode.enqueue`, and `afterFailedCommit` already treated a refused enqueue as a clean rollback — both landed with part one; the store-throws spec case confirms the path end to end.

## Decisions a reviewer should weigh

- **`PowerLossKVStore` has no `deferSync`.** `IKVStore.flush` takes no window, so every flush is strict and a flushed batch is durable when the flush resolves; there is no committed-but-unsynced state to model. The crash window is between a write and the flush after it, held in the store's `WriteGroup` overlay, which `powerLoss()` + `reopen()` discard. The double also never idle-flushes (a no-op `setTimer`), so a test, not a 1 s timer, decides where the crash lands, and after `powerLoss()` the old instance throws `StoreLostPowerError` on every call (and a commit queued behind another refuses to land), so a still-running drain cannot write into the reopened rows. `reopen()` refuses before `powerLoss()`.
- **`settled` deletes the entry and drive records even when the outcome write throws** (a `finally`), then rethrows for the queue to log. The ticket's ordering is "outcome, then deletes"; that order is kept on success. On a failed outcome write, keeping the entry record would let part three restore and re-drive it — a `discarded` transaction would then be sent after all — while deleting it only loses the outcome across a restart. Documented in §Write ordering rule 3.
- **`queued` takes back a failed write** (delete + flush, best effort, logged `offline:journal-take-back-failed`), since a record left by a set that succeeded before a failing flush would bring a rolled-back transaction back on restore. Residual recorded as a `NOTE:` at the site: a store whose flush throws after persisting and then also refuses the take-back.
- **A collection that backs no table fails the commit** (the journal throws from `queued`): nothing could restore it. Unreachable through SQL (every collection is registered with its table); a bare-bridge test with a journal would hit it.
- **The transaction id must be key-safe** (`JOURNAL_KEY_SEGMENT`), checked at `queued` so a bad id fails the commit rather than the later outcome write. Always true for `StampId()` (base64url).
- **`table` is stored structured** (`{ schemaName, tableName }`) rather than as the `schema.table` string, so the restore needs no parser.
- **Record types and the codec are not exported** from either entry; the spec imports `../src/optimystic-adapter/offline-journal.js` directly, as other specs do for internals.
- **`acknowledge` stays synchronous**; its delete is fire-and-forget and not flushed, so an acknowledged outcome can reappear after a crash (stated in the README and §Outcomes after a restart).
- Log namespace for the journal's own lines: `optimystic:quereus-plugin:offline-journal` (`offline:journal-ambiguous-table`, `offline:journal-take-back-failed`).

## Tests

`packages/quereus-plugin-optimystic/test/offline-journal.spec.ts` (two-node mesh, node A's journal a `PowerLossKVStore`, node B severed through `mesh.failures.failingPeers` where a case needs an entry to stay queued):

- **Record layout and lifecycle** — two offline commits over a table and its index: two entry records in `seq` order, each with two segments (main tree, index tree) naming `main.Notes`, the transaction ids, `persisted: true` on the status and `entries()`; after the drain, no entry or drive records, one `committed` outcome record each, and `onCommitDurability` called exactly once per drained commit (pins the single-translation rule).
- **Store throws at enqueue** — `set` made to throw once: the commit rejects with that error, no status, nothing in the store, nothing sealed, the row absent.
- **Crash after the drain committed** — after `committed`, the deletes are visible, then `powerLoss()`/`reopen()`: entry, drive and outcome records all present, and the drive record's attempt names the action id node B's log holds exactly once (pins "deletes are not flushed" and the drive record's write-ahead).
- **Crash after one refused send** — B severed, wait for `attempts >= 1`, crash: entry and drive records present, no outcome; the drive record's attempt is under its `actionId`.
- **Two labels over one store** — two `Database`s, labels `one` and `two`: each label lists only its own entry record, both at `seq` 1.
- **Registration refusals** — `MemoryKVStore` (direct and from a factory), a store missing `list`, `journalLabel` without `journal`, a label with `/`; a factory is called once.
- **Outcome codec round trip** — a `committed` and a `failed` outcome through `encodeOutcome` → JSON → `decodeOutcome`: equal except `followed: false` and the error's class/cause.

`offline-mode.spec.ts`: the unknown-key assertion now uses `allowSoloCommit` (`journal` is a real key now).

Not tested, by inspection: `offline:journal-ambiguous-table`; a factory that throws; outcome deletes on `acknowledge` / prune; a store without `flush`; the take-back.
