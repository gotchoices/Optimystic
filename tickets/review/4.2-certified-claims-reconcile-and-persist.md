# Review: certified claims — selection, read repair, reconcile, persistence

This is the handoff for the WHOLE three-ticket chain (`certified-claim-selection`,
`certified-claims-read-repair`, `certified-claims-reconcile-and-persist`). The first two are
already through review individually; this pass should treat the chain end-to-end, with emphasis
on the reconcile + persistence work (the third ticket), which has not been reviewed.

## What the chain does

Before: a block-repair claim needed two distinct peers to corroborate it, so a sole holder in a
large cohort was a permanent repair deadlock (`sole-holder`), and the `BlockCommitProof` artifact
traveled on both repair wires but nothing verified or weighed it.

After: both repair paths run peer-attached proofs through one shared certification layer
(`src/cluster/certified-claims.ts`), and the selectors (`src/cluster/quorum-restore.ts`,
`selectQuorumRev` / `selectQuorumBlock`) weigh the verdicts — a claim whose proof verified, and
content whose bytes match the proof's declared digest, is accepted with NO second peer at any
cohort size. The reconcile path additionally hands the verified proof to
`saveReplicatedBlock`, which persists it (`BlockStorage.saveReplica` → `saveRestored` →
`saveBlockProof`), so a repaired machine serves the proof onward and certification no longer
decays across repair hops.

## What was implemented in the third ticket (this one)

- `src/cluster/reconcile-block.ts`: certification pass over proof-carrying cohort answers
  before selection (`certifyCandidates`), mirroring the read path — same 0.5 hardcoded simple
  majority (matches `ClusterMember.hasMajority`, NOT the 0.51 config value), same penalty
  discipline (only attributable failures penalize; `digest-mismatch` drops the candidate from
  the content quorum while its rev claim still counts). New logs:
  `reconcile:proof-uncertified`, `reconcile:content-rejected`, `reconcile:certified-selected`,
  `reconcile:certified-equivocation`, `reconcile:certified-content-equivocation`.
  Contradicting-content penalties are SKIPPED when a certified carrier won (deliberate — an
  unanchored proof must not be a reputation lever against honest dissenters; mirrors the read
  path).
- `ReconcileBlockDeps` grew required `superMajorityThreshold`, optional `anchoring`, and a
  4-param `saveReplicatedBlock(blockId, block, source, verifiedProof?)`. All construction
  sites updated (`libp2p-node-base.ts`, `src/testing/mesh-harness.ts`, three mesh specs).
- Persistence: `StorageRepo.saveReplicatedBlock` / `BlockStorage.saveReplica` accept and
  persist a verified proof; `BlockStorage.restoreBlock` STRIPS any proof a remote archive
  attached (the RestorationCoordinator wire verifies nothing); `saveRestored`'s doc states the
  callers-must-strip contract.
- Docs: `internals.md` (repair-wires section rewritten — proofs now decide and now survive
  certified repair; `saveReplicatedBlock` proof sentence corrected), `transactions.md` (:636
  certified exemption from the two-peer floor; :637 content-gate certified short-circuit),
  `correctness.md` (Theorem 14 :397 rewritten — availability win complete,
  manufactured-agreement resistance improved-but-unanchored; Theorem 15 :409 consistency fix —
  the old "commit certificates are not persisted or served" claim was flatly false).

## Known gaps and accepted residuals (be honest with these)

- **Anchoring is unwired.** Layer 1 proves the listed signers signed — NOT that they are the
  block's legitimate cohort. An attacker who mints an entire cohort's keys still forges a
  passing proof. Accepted, logged posture (`ProofAnchoring.onUnanchored` observes, never
  gates) until `feat-cluster-membership-threshold-cert-anchoring` lands. The docs now say this
  in all three places.
- **Monotonic-skip proof gap.** A revision that first landed proof-less keeps no proof even if
  a later certified heal of the same rev carries one (`saveForwardRevision`'s guard returns
  before persisting). Deliberate; pinned by a test whose comment says it is the assertion to
  flip if backfill is ever added. NOTE at the guard site.
- **Promotion path asymmetry (pre-existing, unchanged).** `CoordinatorRepo`'s read-driven
  promotion lands a revision without a proof. Same class as the monotonic-skip gap.
- **Churn/push path persists no proof** — `BlockTransferService` passes none; tracked as
  `require-proof-on-block-push`.
- **Certified-win penalty skip.** When a certified carrier wins the content quorum, peers that
  served different bytes are NOT penalized (deliberate, see above) — a reviewer may reasonably
  probe whether that is exploitable for free lying; the rationale is that penalizing against
  an unanchored proof is the worse failure mode.
- Reviewer suggestions: the equivocation declines leave the block behind forever by design
  (incident, not shortage) — check that operators have enough in the two log lines to act;
  and `certifyCandidates` verifies every proof-carrying answer per reconcile (bounded by
  MAX_PROOF_SIGNERS × cohort width — NOTE at the site says fine at ~10 cohorts).
