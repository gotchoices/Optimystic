# What was wrong, in one paragraph

A write's log entry lands before its data blocks. When the writer is told the commit failed, its retry finds its own entry and re-sends the write at its own revision to finish it. Storage refuses that re-send as soon as ANY block has moved past the revision — and every later commit to the collection moves the log tail past it — so the refusal only says a rival came after, not whether the rival built ON the write (the row is saved; the rival read it and added to it) or OVER it (the row is lost). The writer took the refusal as loss and threw `TornActionError` either way.

# Phase 1 — the routes, pinned

**Deterministic case (repro: verified).** Rebuilt as the first case of `packages/db-p2p/test/superseded-own-write-is-saved.spec.ts`: two-member mesh, writer B's commit really lands, A's `replace()` builds the next revision on top, then B hears an injected `commit-not-durable`. Failed before the fix with the ticket's exact text (`rival-holds-revision`, the row present through A); passes now.

**"Reported torn, appeared one rep later" — the route is NOT a pending-record promotion.** Measured with a throwaway latency spec (deleted before handoff) on a two-member mesh, 2–17 ms delivery latency, 10 pairs per run, with the prerequisite's cancel escape temporarily gated off to provoke tears (restored byte-identical; `git diff` clean on `race-resolution.ts`):

- With the failed writer's `Tree` instance KEPT (as the ticket's run and the concurrent spec do): 17 torn writes over 4 runs, of which 15 were absent right after and present at the end. The counts match exactly: pair k adds the torn row from pair k-1 to the next successful write from the same `Tree` (2 → 6 = a-0, a-1, a-2, b-2).
- With the failed writer's `Tree` instance DISCARDED after each failure (`Tree.createOrOpen` afresh): 17 torn writes over 5 runs, **0 of 17** absent-then-present. 15 stayed absent (truly torn), 2 were present immediately (the deterministic case above, under real contention).

So the row "appeared later" because the torn write's actions stayed staged on the `Collection` (the documented contract: the caller decides) and `Tree.replace`, which never gave the caller that choice, sent them along with the next `replace()`. Statically, the promotion route needs a pending record that survived the cancel AND a block still below the write's revision AND a read carrying the entry as committed; with the cancel confirmed (which the fix now does before declaring final) there is no record left to promote. Fixed by `Collection.actAndSync`: `Tree.replace` and `Diary.append` stage and flush under one latch hold and unstage exactly what they staged when the flush throws (verbatim restore of the earlier transforms while the revision has not moved, so an invented collection keeps its header/root; replay of the rest once it has). Pinned by "a failed replace leaves nothing staged, so nothing rides along with the next one" in `superseded-own-entry.spec.ts`.

# Phase 2 — the design

**Evidence: a per-member "lineage floor", asked by the writer, folded per block at the commit's own acknowledgement bar.**

- `BlockMetadata.lineageFloor` (metadata is one JSON blob on every driver, so the optional field is compatible with stored data): the lowest revision from which every later revision record was produced on this node by applying an update-only transform to the content before it. Maintained in `BlockStorage.setLatest` (new required `builtOnPrior` argument — `internalCommit` passes `transform.insert === undefined`), `recover` (redoes what each lost `setLatest` would have recorded), and `saveForwardRevision` (replica / tombstone: floor = that revision). Pre-existing metadata without the field: the next commit starts the floor at the revision it was built on, claiming nothing below.
- `IBlockStorage.lineageOf(target)` → `contains | excludes | behind | unknown` from the index + floor. This is what closes the ticket's two holes at the member level: a member that held the write and then took a later revision as a replica (the **fork**) answers `unknown`, never `contains`; a member that was behind and took the later revision as a replica (**restored past the revision**) answers `unknown`, never `excludes`. `packages/db-p2p/test/block-lineage.spec.ts` pins every real path (local chain, replica of the write's own revision, replica later, insert later, legacy metadata, recover).
- `StorageRepo.get` answers it when `BlockGets.lineageOf` is present (`GetBlockResult.lineage`); `CoordinatorRepo.get` forwards it on the re-read it does after a cluster consult. On the wire, unlike `floors`; an older peer leaves the field absent, read as `unknown`.
- `NetworkTransactor.getLineage` asks EVERY cohort member of each block directly (no coordinator round: no single member can answer for the cohort), with no context so it promotes nothing, and folds with `judgeCohortLineage` (`db-core/src/network/lineage.ts`): `contains` needs a strict majority of holders, where an `unknown` member counts when it holds the same `(rev, actionId)` latest as a member that vouched (same agreed content — this is what lets the restored-past member not veto); `excludes` needs one prover and every member accounted for (excluding, same-latest-as-an-excluder, or behind); `behind` needs every member; contradictions (a content fork) and everything else are `unknown`. Pinned in `cohort-lineage.spec.ts`; the fan-out in `network-transactor.spec.ts` ("getLineage").
- `Collection.completeOwnEntry` re-sends as before. On a refusal with a confirmed rival (or on the round that spends the last of the budget — `lastChance`, threaded from both write paths), `settleUnfinished` runs: confirm the cancel of the write's pending records FIRST (`ITransactor.cancel` returns only when discharged), then ask `getLineage`, then answer exactly one of: saved (every non-tail block `contains`; returns the folded durability), torn and `final: true` (every unsaved block `excludes`/`behind`, cancel confirmed), torn and `final: false` (anything else). The log block is never asked about — the refresh just read the entry out of it.
- `TornActionError.final` is the field a caller acts on; `reason` stays the cause. The message ends with "safe to submit again" or "could not be established". `TornActionReason`'s `rival-holds-revision` wording corrected (it is no longer "nothing can land this write").

**Rejected alternatives**, briefly: extending the pend-tier `isOwnRevision` carve-out to the revision index was the ticket's "smallest change", and the fork test shows exactly why it is unsound (node B's index names the write; the content came from node A). A client-side pinned read (`materialized`) has the same hole in the other direction (a member restored past the revision serves its older copy for a pin at the write's revision). A pend-tier carve-out that treats a superseded-but-contained own TAIL as satisfied — so the re-send could still land a data block that never landed while only the tail moved on — would let more writes survive contention rather than be reported torn; not done here (it is not a truthfulness fix, and it has to decide what a member holding the tail only as a replica votes). Recorded as an arm on `backlog/bug-a-refused-write-can-leave-its-log-entry-behind`, whose "another writer commits in between" case this ticket splits in two.

# What must be true afterwards — and how each is met

- **Saved means saved.** Case 1 (landed everywhere, rival on top) and case 2 (one member missed the write's data-block commit, the rival read the write through the other member and declared it as its base, the missing member's fork guard refused and reconciled the rival's revision as a replica) both resolve, with the row readable through both members, and with per-member assertions that node B answers `contains` and node A `unknown` — so the case cannot pass vacuously.
- **Torn means final.** Case 3 (one member holds the write, the rival read the OTHER member's older copy and built on it, the holder's fork guard refused and took the rival's content as a replica): `TornActionError` `rival-holds-revision` with `final: true`, node B's revision index still names the write (asserted), node B answers `unknown`, node A `excludes`; the row is absent on both, the failed `replace` left nothing staged, and resubmitting stores it once. Cancel-before-ask is what makes `behind` mean "never will be"; a refused confirmation (`LaterCancelsRefused` in the db-core spec) yields `final: false`, as does a transactor or wrapper without `getLineage` (`cannotSay`).
- **Regression specs.** Deterministic case: `superseded-own-write-is-saved.spec.ts` case 1 (mesh) and `superseded-own-entry.spec.ts` "is SAVED when the rival built…" (in-memory). "Appeared later" route: "a failed replace leaves nothing staged…". Both holes: cases 2 and 3 on the mesh, built from ONE fault (a member's `storageRepo.commit` answering the tolerated "already past that revision" refusal for one data-block commit) and real traffic from there — no other injection. Determinism: 8 of 8 runs green after routing the fork rival's reads to node A's own storage (the mesh picks a read coordinator by ring distance to the random block id, so before that the rival sometimes legitimately read the write and the fix correctly reported *saved* — the fix was never wrong, the case was under-specified).
- **Mutation checks.** (a) `lineageOf` answering from the index alone → 2 mesh cases and 4 storage cases fail. (b) `judgeCohortLineage` not counting same-latest `unknown` members → the restored-past mesh case fails. Both restored by reverse edit; sources verified.

# Behaviour changes a reviewer should weigh

- `Tree.replace` / `Diary.append` no longer leave a failed call's action staged. Every failure kind, not only torn ones: a transient error used to leave the action for a later `sync()` to carry; now the caller re-calls `replace`. The plugin's own commit paths use `stage` + `sync` + `restore` and are unaffected; its catalog writes use `replace` (2 sites) and its full suite is green. `Collection.act`/`sync` keep the old contract (the "gives up as a TORN write … keeps the action staged" case still passes).
- One cancel consensus round is added on the torn path (the confirmation), and one `get` per cohort member per block. Only reached when a re-send is refused with a confirmed rival or on a budget's last round.
- `IBlockStorage.setLatest` gained a required `builtOnPrior` argument (one production caller, 15 spec setup calls updated; a test double in `storage-repo.spec.ts` updated).
- `TornActionError`'s message text changed ("are not known to hold it" + the final/not-final suffix). The sereus text coupling in `debt-a-downstream-repo-classifies-retries-by-parsing-our-error-text` is on the transactor's per-batch detail renderers, not on this error; nothing in-tree matches the old phrase.

# Known gaps (honest)

- **`final: false` is the conservative answer in more situations than the ticket may have wanted.** A write given up on a DEADLINE (not the attempt count) escapes unsettled; a cohort member that cannot be reached, a cohort where fewer than a majority can vouch, and the content-fork residual (`backlog/bug-a-pended-transform-does-not-carry-its-base`: a rival with no declared base applied on two different bases — one member `contains`, one `excludes`) all yield `unknown` → not final. Provability also decays: if every member has since taken a later revision as a replica, nobody can vouch. All are documented at `judgeCohortLineage` and in internals.md; none are wrong answers, and I did not measure how often they occur under contention.
- **Durability of a lineage-recognised save** comes from the folded per-block reports of the DATA blocks; when the log block's own cohort cannot vouch for it, the sync answers `undefined` for a saved write (the pre-existing wart of the status-read branch, NOTE at `settleUnfinished`).
- **The pend-tier carve-out is not done** (see the arm on `bug-a-refused-write-can-leave-its-log-entry-behind`): a write whose tail moved on while its data block never landed is reported torn-final rather than finished.
- **Single-node transactor wrappers** (`quereus-plugin-optimystic` local mode, `reference-peer`'s `LocalTransactor`) do not offer `getLineage`; a torn write there would read `final: false`. Not wired because a write cannot tear on them — their commit is one atomic `StorageRepo.commit`, never tail-then-sweep.
- **Sibling ticket** `implement/a-member-that-missed-a-commit-refuses-every-later-write` asked to reuse "how a member decides an action is already committed"; a note appended there explains that what landed answers a different question (was MY content built from it, member-local) than theirs (did the COHORT commit it, for a member that is behind), and what shape to reuse.
- The prerequisite ticket's latency spec (`concurrent-two-member-writes-do-not-tear.spec.ts`) was not changed and still passes in the full run.
