# What landed

**The drain.** `UnderReplicationDrain` is a `Startable` constructed in `createLibp2pNodeBase` right after the spread-on-churn block and before the arachnode/FRET gate, on every node unless `underReplicationDrain: { enabled: false }`. It has its own stop wrapper. A pass runs on start, on `connection:open` and `peer:identify` (debounced 5 s, throttled to one pass per 15 s with a trigger inside the window deferred to the window's end rather than dropped), and on a 30 s re-check timer armed while `ledger.size() > 0`. A pass: skips under a partition; computes the pushable peers (connected AND identified as serving this network's block-transfer protocol, read from the peer store) and skips at that O(connections) step when there are none; lists the ledger; rotates through at most 64 entries oldest-first (a cursor, so a backlog wider than the budget is swept in full); and per entry works out who is owed (a named set as recorded, never re-resolved; an empty set re-resolved via `findCluster`, a self-only cohort counted as `solo` with no attempt), pushes to the reachable non-abandoned owed peers through the shared helper, takes confirmed peers off the entry (naming an unnamed entry first), counts a round via `noteAttempt` when a reachable peer did not confirm, abandons a peer per block after 5 consecutive unconfirmed rounds until it reconnects, deletes the entry for a block gone from local storage, and fires the event after the entry is deleted. `getDiagnostics()` and `checkNow()` exist for tests and operators.

**Give-up bookkeeping.** The in-memory per-(block, peer) failure counter and abandoned set are the give-up mechanism, cleared by a fresh `connection:open` for that peer (a second parallel connection to a still-connected peer does not clear it) and by the peer being absent at pass time. The ledger's `attempts` field is bumped once per unsuccessful round as the durable, visible counter; it is not what the abandonment decision reads, because a same-revision re-record keeps it and reconnect could then never grant a fresh budget.

**Revision guard.** `IUnderReplicationLedger.satisfy` gained an optional `heldRev`: an entry recording a higher revision than the copy the peer confirmed is left alone. The drain passes the pushed `state.latest.rev` (falling back to the entry's own rev). A copy of a newer revision satisfies an older entry, as the ledger's supersession rule says. This closes a window the ticket did not name: a rev-6 shortfall recorded on the coordinator while the drain's rev-5 push was in flight would otherwise have had its missing member wrongly removed.

**The event.** `BlockDurabilityReachedEvent` / `IBlockDurabilityNotifier` in db-core, implemented by `StorageRepo` beside `IBlockChangeNotifier`, with the block-keyed rationale in the type's doc comment. The drain receives `(event) => storageRepo.emitBlockDurabilityReached(event)` — one method wide. Emitted per block (one id per event); the array shape is kept for a future producer that batches.

**The push loop.** `pushBlockToPeers` in `block-transfer-service.ts` is the read-certify-push-interpret core, with `stopAfterConfirmed`, the two per-peer deadlines, reason and prefix as parameters, returning `{ 'no-local-data' | 'unavailable' | 'pushed' }` with confirmed / refusals (`rejected` vs `unreachable`) / skipped, plus the pushed `latest`, the header's `collectionId` and whether a proof travelled. **All three** existing copies were converted, not two: `executePush` (`stopAfterConfirmed: 1`), `executeConfirm` (`stopAfterConfirmed: floor`) and the spread loop. The helper logs nothing per peer; each caller keeps its own tags and namespace. `backlog/debt-three-sites-repeat-the-certified-push-loop` is rewritten to say nothing of the core remains and recommends closing it at the next gardening pass.

**Ledger warm-up.** `KvUnderReplicationLedger.list` seeds the eviction index when it is the first scan, so the drain's start pass loads the index for free on a node that starts connected. `size()` reads the index. The backlog ticket on the solo first-commit stall is updated with exactly what this does and does not cover (a node that starts alone still pays the load on its first commit).

# Deviations from the ticket, stated plainly

- **Reachable means identified, not merely connected.** The ticket says "skip any peer that is not currently connected"; the drain additionally requires the peer store to show the peer serving this network's block-transfer protocol (what identify fills). Reason: it makes the "nobody to push to" short-circuit precise and O(connections), so a solo node with only a foreign-network or relay connection never scans its ledger on the re-check timer. Consequence: a peer that just connected is pushable only after identify completes, which is why `peer:identify` is a trigger. With no `protocolPrefix` configured (unit tests), any connected peer counts.
- **`executePush` / `executeConfirm` timeout semantics changed slightly.** They passed `transferTimeoutMs` through a `withTimeout` race that resolved `undefined` and left the dial running; they now pass it as the client's dial and reply deadlines, which abort the dial and tear down a silent stream. Worst-case per-peer wait is two deadlines rather than one. Documented on `BlockTransferConfig.transferTimeoutMs`; the existing "clears the timeout timer" spec still passes because the client clears both timers on settle.
- **Two interface additions on the prerequisite's ledger** (`heldRev` on `satisfy`, `size()`), both justified above; the one fake ledger in the tree (`coordinator-repo-write-durability.spec.ts`) gained `size`.
- **The event is per block** rather than per round. Simpler ordering ("after the deletion") and a host removing ids one at a time does not care.
- **Naming an unnamed entry happens only when at least one peer confirmed.** An entry that could not name its members stays unnamed while nobody confirms, so the next pass re-resolves the cohort against the then-current membership instead of pinning names from a failed round. The interface's own guidance ("name it first, then satisfy") is followed at the moment a confirmation is taken.
- **`node.underReplicationLedger` and `node.underReplicationDrain` are exposed untyped** (`(node as any)`), matching `rebalanceMonitor` / `spreadOnChurnMonitor` rather than `OptimysticNodeAttachments`; the integration util reaches the ledger through a cast helper (`ledgerOf`).

# Known gaps and things a reviewer should push on

- **Entries recorded while the owed member is already connected** (a `majority` write where a connected member failed to apply) are not pushed until the next connection/identify event, the re-check timer (armed only while `size() > 0`, so it does fire — within 30 s), or a restart. The in-memory commit retry usually lands the copy first; the drain then only confirms it. There is no hook from `noteReplicationShortfall` to the drain; I judged the re-check timer sufficient and cheap. If a reviewer disagrees, the seam is a `record` listener on the ledger.
- **A large network.** The drain never dials a cohort member it is not connected to (the ticket's rule). In a network wider than one cohort, an entry whose owed members never connect directly waits indefinitely, costing one `list()` per re-check tick while any pushable peer is connected. The rebalance growth arm still dials such members. Parked as the scan `NOTE:` on the class.
- **`peer:identify` and FRET admission are not the same moment.** An unnamed entry's cohort is re-resolved through `findCluster`, which needs FRET to have admitted the peer; over loopback that is well inside the 5 s debounce (the integration runs confirm it), and the re-check timer covers a slow link. The `RebalanceMonitor` carries the same note.
- **Solo-node cost on the re-check timer** is one `getConnections()` scan plus one `size()` per 30 s while any entry exists — a solo node keeps entries forever, so this ticks forever at O(1). Not measured in wall clock.
- **Only the drain's own timers are `unref`'d**; the stop wrapper is idempotent; an in-flight pass is not awaited by `stop()` (it checks `running` between entries and bails) so a stop never waits out a push deadline. A push already on the wire at stop time completes or fails on its own.
- **The lone-survivor write in the integration spec takes 19–27 s** to be acknowledged at `local`. That is the write path's timing (pend/commit deadlines against the departed member), not the drain's, but it dominates the phase and is worth knowing when reading the log.
- **`transferTimeoutMs` change** (above) is the one behavioural change to existing paths beyond the refactor; `block-transfer.spec.ts` and `rebalance-reaction.spec.ts` still pass, but no spec pins "worst case is two deadlines".
- Coverage floor: the unit spec drives passes through `checkNow()`; the debounce/throttle/timer paths have one case each with short intervals.
