# Review: a node measures its use against one allotment

## What landed

`StorageMonitor` is gone. In its place `StorageAccount` (`packages/db-p2p/src/storage/storage-account.ts`) is the node's one opinion about how full it is, built once per node on either role from `NodeOptions.storage` and the new top-level `NodeOptions.storageAllotment`, and exposed as `node.storageAccount` on `OptimysticNodeAttachmentsBase`.

- **One option, validated at construction.** `storageAllotment: { bytes?, usedBytesCacheTtlMs?, now? }`. `bytes` must be an integer above 0 or `createLibp2pNodeBase` throws a `RangeError` (`assertStorageAllotmentBytes`, directly below the `resolveBlobCacheBudgetBytes` whose rule it copies). `arachnode.storage` and `StorageMonitorConfig` are deleted; `arachnode` keeps only `enableRingZulu`. The two test-only overrides (`usedBytes`, `availableBytes`) and the "short-circuit the backend when an override is supplied" branch are gone with them.
- **The effective total** is `effectiveTotalBytes(allotmentBytes, locationBytes)` — the smaller of the two over whichever are present, `undefined` when neither is. `locationBytes` is the measured use plus `getApproximateBytesFree()`, read **inside the same memoized scan** so the two figures cannot come from different moments. A rejected free read is caught, logged once for that scan, and leaves the location unknown — never 0.
- **`usedBytes` is unclamped** (it still floors negatives at 0, which is a different rule — a backend reporting a negative figure would otherwise inflate the location total). A node past its allotment reports more used than its total; `availableBytes` floors at 0.
- **Ring selection declines on an unknown capacity rather than inventing one.** `determineRing` answers ring 0 (not `-1`); `shouldTransition` folds nothing into the EWMA, answers no move, and logs once; `createArachnodeInfo` omits `ArachnodeInfo.capacity` entirely, which is now optional. `ArachnodeFretAdapter.getRingStats` skips a capacity-less peer out of both the peer count and the capacity total, so a ring where nobody said yields no stat at all and falls through to `estimateNetworkData`'s bootstrap estimate.
- **Start-up line**, on both roles, beside `logStorageDurability`: `logStorageAllotment` names the allotment (or that none was configured), the location figure (or that the backend reports no free space) and the effective total; `WARN:` when neither is known, naming what ring selection will and will not do and how to set an allotment; plus an ordinary-level line when the allotment is below `DEFAULT_MIN_RING_CAPACITY_BYTES` (100 MiB, now exported from `ring-selector.ts` and read by both the wiring and this line, rather than written twice).
- **reference-peer**: `--storage-capacity` → `--storage-allotment`, feeding `storageAllotment: { bytes }`; `logRingInfo` is now async and logs `node.storageAccount.capacity()` rather than the raw flag it was handed.

## What to test, validate and look at

**The effective-total rule on a real backend.** The four arms were exercised against `fileNodeStorage` during implementation (throwaway spec, since deleted): no allotment → total = use + free (≈2.3 TiB on the dev machine); 10 GiB allotment → total 10 GiB; 1 PiB allotment → total bounded back to the disk's figure. The committed coverage of this is the pure-function table in `storage-account.spec.ts`, which does not touch a backend — worth a look at whether that is the right trade.

**The memory-storage case, which is every mesh spec in the repository.** No allotment and no free-space figure (memory, SQLite, LevelDB, a web store under Node) means no total. Checked against today's behaviour: a memory node previously computed `coverage = 10 GiB / estimateNetworkData(...)`, which with no ring stats is far above 1 and already answered ring 0; move-in is unreachable from ring 0 and move-out needed 85 % of a total it did not have. **The one real change** is that a memory node whose peers DO report ring stats no longer specializes off them at bootstrap — it used to divide the invented 10 GiB by the observed aggregate and could land on ring 2 or 3; it now answers ring 0. A second, narrower consequence: such a node, if it somehow were advertising a ring above 0 (stale metadata read at restart), now never moves in, where before it would have.

**Mixed versions.** An older peer reading an `ArachnodeInfo` with no `capacity` dereferences `arachnode.capacity.available` in its own `getRingStats` and throws. Accepted under this repository's stated position on backwards compatibility, and the only node advertising none is one that cannot measure its storage — in practice a memory-storage node, i.e. a test mesh. Not guarded, by the ticket's instruction.

**The client node's account is built and exposed but unread.** Nothing on a client consumes it yet; the blob cache budget still has its own unrelated figure. That is `a-node-stays-within-its-allotment`'s work, and the account exists here so that ticket has one measurement to spend against.

**Unclamped `usedBytes` — each consumer was checked.** `shouldTransition`'s ratio (fine: compared against a threshold below 1, so an over-full node trips the move-out arm, which is the existing full-node behaviour), `availableBytes` (floored), `ArachnodeInfo.capacity.used` on the wire (peers read only `available`).

## Tests

Added or changed, each beside what it pins:

- `storage-account.spec.ts` (renamed from `storage-monitor.spec.ts`)
  - **`effectiveTotalBytes` over its arms** (new) — allotment only, location only, both (smaller wins), an over-promise bounded by the disk, neither (undefined, not 0).
  - **`reads the location figure as the measured use plus the free space`** (new) — the `usedBytes + free` rule and that the location is the whole total when no allotment was configured.
  - **`leaves the total unknown on a failed free-space read, and recovers on the next scan`** (new) — the arm that would otherwise shed responsibility on a transient failure; also that the use is still measured on such a scan.
  - **`reports a node past its allotment as over-used with nothing available`** (new) — replaces the dropped `clamps used bytes to the configured total`, whose rule this ticket reverses.
  - Kept, unchanged in subject: the backend-reported figure, no `getApproximateBytesUsed` → 0, the negative floor, the two piece-identity branches, and the five cache cases (within TTL, past TTL, TTL 0, single-flight, no-cache-or-wedge on error).
  - Dropped: `honors usedBytes override`, `honors availableBytes override`, `never scans when usedBytes override is supplied` (the overrides are gone), `clamps used bytes to the configured total`.
- `ring-selector.spec.ts` — **`answers ring 0, not -1, when the node cannot measure its storage`** and **`declines to move when the node cannot measure its storage`** (both new; two cases because they are two rules a future edit could break independently). `MockStorageMonitor` → `MockStorageAccount` with `capacity()` and a `setUnknownCapacity()`; `advertise()` now builds an info with no capacity, which exercises the optional field.
- `arachnode-fret-adapter.spec.ts` (new file, two cases) — **a capacity-less peer is out of both the count and the average**, and **a ring where nobody advertised capacity yields no stat** (not a stat with `avgCapacity: 0`, which `estimateNetworkData` would read as real demand).
- `ring-shift-coordinator.spec.ts` — mock renamed with the class; no behavioural change.

**No test** for: the start-up line's words, the option validation (one `RangeError` under a rule stated and untested at `resolveBlobCacheBudgetBytes`), the `storageAccount` attachment (`tsc` checks the wiring), or the reference-peer flag rename.
