# A consensus round stops waiting once its outcome is decided

GitHub #64 (VoteTorrent). Before this, `ClusterCoordinator` awaited every member in each of its three rounds (promise, commit, consensus broadcast), so one connected-but-silent member cost every write its RPC deadline per round (reporter: median control write 0.47 s healthy against 75 s with one silent phone).

## What changed

- **`round-decision.ts`** holds the mechanism and the three rules. `awaitDecision(requests, decided, grace)` resolves once the rule says the answers in hand settle the round — reached, or out of reach — and, when members are still unanswered, after a grace (below). The rules: promise round decided at `ceil(n × superMajority)` approvals in the merged map or when approvals + outstanding can't reach it; commit round the same over the commits the record would keep (`commitsSignedOverRecordPromises`, the equal-map rule) against `floor(n × simpleMajority) + 1`; broadcast when holders reach `isDurableMajority` (moved here from `coordinator-repo.ts`, which now imports it, so the broadcast and the durability gate count with one function) or possible holders can't. "Holds" (`holdsRecord`): commit record → `commit.success === true`; pend/cancel record → `executed === true` and no pend refusal. This node's member counts by its retained verdicts (`localApplyOutcome`), and as a possible holder when it applied without holding.
- **Rounds.** The promise round switched from `Pending` + catch to `deliver`. Only answered promises are merged; per-peer penalties only for failures before the decision. Every round sends a snapshot of the record (a straggler's request is still on its way when the record is next written). The commit round never targets a member a promise request is still riding to (`targetsNow`); this node's member, when in the commit round (pre-sign threw), is delivered first and awaited, so it is never a straggler.
- **Stragglers** (`adoptStragglers`, called inside `awaitRound` before the caller resumes): marked `inFlight`, added to `state.stragglers` (or to the retry's pending set when a retry run's own broadcast left them). Their answers (`onStragglerAnswer`) are logged `cluster-tx:straggler-answer`, never penalised, their promises never merged; buffered in `state.lateAnswers` until the next round boundary (`foldBufferedAnswers`, called by `targetsNow` and `owedAfterRounds`) while the rounds run, folded directly after the retry is armed (`foldStragglerAnswer` → `settleOwedDelivery`). Every round end (`deliverConsensus`, the commit round's no-majority branch, the pend deferral) arms the retry with failures + unsettled stragglers.
- **`runRetry`**: when every pending member is in flight it re-arms at the same attempt and interval (keeping `deferred`) instead of spending an attempt; the old `NOTE:` is replaced by the rule.
- **`releaseTransaction`** is idempotent (`state.released`): a straggler can now finish a retry before `executeClusterTransaction`'s `finally` runs, which would otherwise arm a second release.
- Docs: `docs/internals.md` (new paragraphs under §Both consensus rounds…, and the "A behind member actively reconciles" bullet), `packages/db-p2p/docs/cluster.md` (Phase 1, Phase 2 with the size table, Consensus Achievement, Commit Retry Loop, Latency), `docs/correctness.md` (§2 durability residual names round stragglers; Theorem 1 notes late votes are not counted and why that is safe). `PromiseShortfallError.silentPeers` doc says it can name merely-unanswered members.

## Deviation from the plan: a bounded grace after the decision — please weigh it

The plan said each round resolves "as soon as the answers in hand decide it", and also that `transaction-node-count-sweep.spec.ts` must stay green with its table unchanged for healthy cohorts. Those conflict. Implemented strictly, a healthy 4- or 5-member cohort's last member (a few ms behind the others) became a straggler on every write: the sweep failed (`isFullyDurable` false, `quorum: 'majority'`, `unconfirmed: [last member]`), and in production every such write would also have put that member in the under-replication ledger and drawn a redundant drain push per block.

So `awaitDecision` waits, after the decision, for the unanswered members up to **as long again as the decision took, at least 50 ms** (`STRAGGLER_GRACE_FLOOR_MS`, on the coordinator's injected clock/timer). Healthy cohorts are unchanged (everyone lands in the grace — sweep, plugin and integration suites pass untouched); a silent member costs a round about twice its decision time instead of its RPC deadline (≈1–2 s per write on the reporter's relay instead of 75 s). A two-member cohort never arms a grace (every rule needs both). The `NOTE:` on `DecisionGrace` names the tuning direction. If the reviewer prefers the strict rule, the change is to drop the `grace` argument in `ClusterCoordinator.awaitRound` and then accept the healthy-cohort durability-class change (update the sweep's `isFullyDurable` assertion at sizes 4–5, and consider settling ledger entries from a straggler's late "holds" answer).

With the grace, the existing unit specs pass unchanged; an earlier strict-rule run needed edits to five of them (order-dependent), all reverted.

## Known gaps and things to look at

- **Classification on votes in hand.** A round decided out of reach reports merely-unanswered members in `PromiseShortfallError.silentPeers`; a late minority `reject` no longer marks the record `disputed` (dispute origination is dormant; noted in correctness.md Theorem 1). The grace makes both rare in practice.
- **Escorted pend vs. its own stale promise request** (`NOTE:` at `takeOwedRecords`): an action's commit still escorts an owed pend to a member whose pend promise request is in flight; if the stale promise request lands second it plants a reservation until the member's staleness sweep. The commit round avoids this ordering; the escort path does not. Not observed; tripwire recorded.
- **Behind coordinator + outstanding remote.** When this node's member applied but does not yet hold (behind), the broadcast counts it as a possible holder and waits for outstanding remotes rather than running the second reconcile first. Same wait as before the change, not a regression.
- **Deferred pend retry while carried.** A deferral timer that finds the member in flight now re-arms at `pendDeliveryDeferralMs` (2 s) rather than becoming counted attempt 1 at 250 ms; only matters when the carried request then comes back not delivered.
- `runRetry`'s own first delivery loop is still `Promise.all` (background only; a hanging member holds that run, as before).
- `cluster-coordinator.ts` grew to 2230 lines; the straggler path is owed-delivery bookkeeping, recorded as an arm of backlog `debt-cluster-coordinator-owed-delivery-outgrew-its-file`.
