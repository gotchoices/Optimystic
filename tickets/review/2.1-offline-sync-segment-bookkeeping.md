## What was built

**The segment list.** `SegmentList` in `packages/db-core/src/collection/segments.ts` holds `sealed` (one `SealedSegment` per entry, oldest first, each with its `entryId`, its `actions` and an optional `refused`), `open`, and `version`. The pure operations live on it: `seal` (returns `false` and changes nothing, version included, for an empty open segment; throws for a duplicate id), `drop` (anywhere in the list; throws for an unknown id), `refuse`, `find`, `head`, `entryIds`, `replayable` (non-refused sealed segments) and `replayableCount` (their actions plus the open segment's). Every sealed-list change bumps `version`. The collection's `pending` field is gone; every former site reads `segments.open` or a replayable count, and each non-obvious site says which in a comment.

**The refusal class.** `ActionRefusedError` in `packages/db-core/src/collection/struct.ts` (collection id and message). `TreeGuardRefusedError` extends it, keeping its `key` field and name; its `collectionId` is now inherited rather than redeclared, which is the same public type. `classifyCommitFailure` and the Quereus bridge are untouched and still match `TreeGuardRefusedError`.

**The collection's new members.** `sealPending(entryId): boolean` (synchronous, latch-free, caller brackets it), `sealedEntries()`, `sealedRefusal(entryId)`, `dropSealed(entryId): Promise<void>` (takes the latch, drops, then `replayActions`), and the constructor-time `onSealedRefused` in `CollectionInitOptions`, threaded through the constructor and both open paths. The callback is called once per discovery, synchronously under the latch; a throw out of it is caught and logged as `collection:refusal-callback-threw`.

**Segment-aware replay.** `replayActions` resets the tracker to `replayBaseline()` (a copy of the new private `inventedBaseline` while `source.actionContext` is undefined, empty otherwise), stages each replayable sealed segment through ONE `internalTransact(...segment.actions)` call, catches only `ActionRefusedError` (records it via `recordSealedRefusal`: `SegmentList.refuse`, the `collection:sealed-segment-refused` log line, the callback), and then stages the open segment one action at a time exactly as before, with a refusal propagating. The baseline is captured in `createOrOpen`'s create branch with `copyTransforms` after `Log.open`, never cleared; `Collection.open` leaves it undefined.

**Counts.** `mustReplay` and `restageIfBasesMoved` gate on `replayableCount()`. The invalidation conjunct in `updateInternal` does too. `hasUnsyncedChanges` keeps its formula and its doc now says `sync` refuses while segments are sealed.

**The success-branch reorder in `syncAttempts`.** Fold (`sourceCache.transformCache`) and context adoption now run before `replayActions`; the dormant NOTE is replaced by one that says why the order is load-bearing (the invented baseline, and the head commit of the third ticket).

**Whole-collection commits refuse over sealed segments.** `SealedSegmentsError` (collection id, sealed entry ids) is thrown by `assertNoSealedSegments` from `syncInternal` (before the action id is minted, so `sync` and `updateAndSync` both refuse), from `actAndSync` (before staging, so nothing is unstaged) and from `getPendingActions`. `clearPendingActions`, `filterAgainstEntry`, `consumeOwnEntry` and `unstage` touch the open segment only. `consumeOwnEntry` now returns a boolean and does its slice through `consumedFromOpen`, so the third ticket's head arm is a branch beside it.

**Tree.** `Tree.open` and `Tree.createOrOpen` take a sixth optional parameter, `options?: TreeCollectionOptions` (a `Pick` of `CollectionInitOptions` for `onSealedRefused`), spread into the init that `buildInit` builds. All existing callers pass five or fewer arguments and are unchanged.

**Docs.** `docs/internals.md` has a new subsection *Staged actions are segments* under the read path, right after *Conflict replay must read at the revision it is adopting*. `docs/offline-sync.md` §The segment model lists the settled members (`sealedRefusal`, `onSealedRefused`, the `SealedSegmentsError` rule) and §Replay with a per-segment refusal names `ActionRefusedError` with `TreeGuardRefusedError` as the tree's subclass. The stage status line and the stage-2 item are left for the third ticket.

## Tests added (`packages/db-core/test/collection-segments.spec.ts`, all over `TestTransactor` and `Tree`)

- **`SegmentList` seals only a non-empty open segment and bumps the version on every change** — pins the version rule the next ticket's restore branches on, including that a refused seal does not bump it; tested on the list directly because `Collection` does not expose the version yet.
- **`sealPending` with an empty open segment returns false and leaves an invented collection untouched** — the header is not sealed, the tree still reads, and a duplicate id throws.
- **A refresh records the refusal, reports it once, keeps the segment listed, and the read sees the rival** — the ticket's `absent`-guard case over `commitRivalTreeWrite`, asserting the callback's `(entryId, error)`, `sealedRefusal`, `sealedEntries`, the read result and the log line.
- **A refused segment is not replayed or reported again on a later refresh** — a second rival commit and a second `update()`; the callback count stays one.
- **A sealed segment stages atomically** — three actions whose third refuses on replay leave none of the three staged.
- **A refusal of the open segment propagates out of `update()` and reports nothing.**
- **A throwing callback is logged and the replay still records the refusal and finishes** — asserts `collection:refusal-callback-threw` and that the segment after the refused one was re-staged.
- **`dropSealed` removes a non-head segment, reports a later dependent segment's refusal, and clears a refused segment when dropped** — four segments; the dropped one is second, the `unchanged`-guarded third refuses with `actual === undefined`, dropping it leaves the rest replaying cleanly with no new report, and an unknown id throws without changing anything.
- **An invented collection replays from its baseline** — two sealed segments, the first dropped: still invented, readable, holding the remaining segment, nothing in storage. Then both dropped and `sync()`: `hasUnsyncedChanges()` turns false, which is the direct test of the fold-before-replay order over a baseline.
- **`sync` refuses with `SealedSegmentsError` and leaves everything staged; `replace` refuses before staging; the coordinator's commit throws it by name with every participant restored** — the sibling collection keeps its staged action and nothing of it lands.

No test was added for the no-sealed-segment paths: the whole db-core suite is the safety net the ticket named, and it passes unchanged.

## Gaps and judgement calls for the reviewer

- **`restorePending` is not yet segment-aware.** `CollectionSnapshot.pending` is the open segment, but a restore still reinstates `transforms` verbatim, and its moved-boundary arm with an empty captured open segment resets the tracker EMPTY, which with sealed segments present would drop their effects. Both are the next ticket's table; a NOTE at `CollectionSnapshot.pending` in `collection.ts` says so. No caller seals yet, so nothing reaches it.
- **`hasUnsyncedChanges` over a sealed segment that wrote nothing.** The formula is kept as the ticket asked, so a sealed segment whose actions produced no transforms (a blind delete of an unseen key) over an otherwise empty tracker reads `false` while `sealedEntries()` is non-empty. The stage-3 queue should drive off `sealedEntries()`, not this predicate.
- **How the callback reaches a `Tree`** was not specified; the trailing `options` parameter on `Tree.open` and `Tree.createOrOpen` is my choice. The stage-3 queue may prefer to install the callback after opening (a setter), in which case that parameter should go rather than grow.
- **A duplicate entry id throws from `sealPending` even when the open segment is empty** (the duplicate check runs first). Deliberate: a duplicate is a caller bug whatever is staged.
- **A non-refusal error thrown while replaying a sealed segment still leaves the tracker half-rebuilt**, the shape backlog `debt-a-failed-refresh-can-leave-a-collection-half-restaged` describes; the NOTE at `updateInternal`'s replay call now says which half is atomic (sealed) and which is not (open). Unchanged by design.
- **`collection.ts` grew from 2556 to 2735 lines.** The third ticket already plans the shared-machinery extraction and a re-measurement of backlog `debt-collection-write-retry-logic-outgrew-its-file`, so this ticket did not append one.
- `tickets/.garden-report.md` shows as modified in the working tree; that change is not this ticket's.
