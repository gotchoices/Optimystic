## What was built

- `packages/db-core/src/offline/queue.ts` — `OfflineQueue` and `OfflineQueueOptions`, as the implement ticket specified: `enqueue` (seals each collection's open segment under one entry id, emits `queued` synchronously, starts the drain lazily), `onSealedRefused` (records and wakes only; never calls a latched method), `entries`, `status`, `discard`, `onStatus`, `onQueueState`, `queueState`, `wake`, `whenDrained`, `dispose`. One drain loop per queue, serialised, with the iteration order of the ticket's *The drain* section (pending refusals, expiry, empty-queue wait, head refusal check, reachability gate, submit, outcome by class). The gate and the transient failures share one re-check timer (`RecheckBackoff`, `jitteredBackoffMs`, unref'd via `armUnrefTimer`, reset by a wake or a committed entry). Every removal, the loop's and `discard`'s, runs on one promise chain so two never interleave their drops.
- `packages/db-core/src/offline/status.ts` — `EntryStatus`, `OfflineQueueState`, `QueuedEntry`, `QueuedFailureReason`, `EntryWaitingFor`, and the errors `EntryNotDiscardableError`, `UnknownEntryError`, `QueueDisposedError`, plus `EntryDiscardedError` and `EntryExpiredError` (the `error` a discarded or expired entry fails with — the ticket's status shape requires an `Error` there and named none).

- `refusalReason` in `packages/db-core/src/transaction/commit-failure-class.ts` — the tree-specific `constraint` / `concurrent-modification` mapping extracted from the classifier's `guard-refused` arm, now used there and by the queue for refusals that never pass through a commit (the callback's, and an `ActionRefusedError` thrown directly out of a submission). ## Judgment calls the reviewer should weigh

- **`causedBy` names the nearest earlier failing entry, not the entry whose drop ran the replay.** The ticket's *Removing an entry* section says a refusal reported during entry X's drop gets `causedBy = X`; its *Cascade three deep* case expects E3 `causedBy: E2`. The two disagree, because one replay refuses every later segment that no longer applies at once: dropping E1 re-stages E2 (refused) and then E3 over state without E2, so E3's refusal is reported during E1's drop. `OfflineQueue.causeOf` names the nearest earlier entry (in queue order) that is being removed or already has a refusal pending, which gives the chain E3 ← E2 ← E1 the test expects, and the same rule attributes a reader's refresh that refuses E1 and its dependent E2 in one replay as E2 ← E1. The comment at `causeOf` records why. If the literal rule is wanted, the fix is one line; the test's E3 assertion changes with it.
- **A `partial` outcome is kept queued and re-driven under the same id, with its own log line (`offline:partial-commit-deferred`), and is NOT counted in `unclassifiedFailures`.** The ticket said "treated as `unknown`"; the transition is the same, but the count is meant to name failures the drain cannot classify, and a partial commit is classified. The re-drive submits only the collections that still hold the entry's segment (`OfflineQueue.submit` filters on `sealedEntries`), which is what makes a re-drive after a partial landing commit what is left instead of meeting `SealedSegmentsError` (`not-head`) on a consumed participant. A re-drive that leaves one collection goes through `commitHead`, and its `committed` durability names only that collection; the next ticket owns reporting the whole entry.
- **In doubt is set before the write-ahead hook is awaited, so a hook that throws leaves the entry in doubt though nothing was sent.** Literal to the ticket ("set the first time the queue's `recordAttempt` wrapper runs"); conservative, costs only discardability for that entry.
- **A wake that arrives while a submission is in flight is kept and consumed by the next wait**, so a transient failure right after such a wake is re-driven at once rather than after the backoff. Needed so a connection event during a submission is not lost; the cost is one extra attempt in that interleaving.
- **The head's promotion (`waitingFor: 'earlier-entry'` → `undefined`) is not re-emitted as a `queued` event**; `status()` and `entries()` reflect it live, and the next emission for the head is normally `submitting`. A `queued` event is re-emitted only when something visible changed (waitingFor, attempts, inDoubt, lastError).
- **`discard` removes the entry from the queue synchronously and drops its segments on the removal chain concurrently with a submission in flight** (the ticket's test requires the segment gone while the head's commit is parked). The loop re-reads the head right before submitting, so a `discard` that lands while the gate is being asked cannot have the removed entry submitted. A removal that throws off the loop's path is recorded as a defect the loop meets on its next iteration.
- **After a defect** (`SealedSegmentsError` out of a submission, a removal that threw): the drain stops, `whenDrained` rejects with it, and `enqueue` throws an `Error` with the defect as `cause` — nothing queued then would ever drain, so refusing seemed better than silently accepting. The ticket specified only the first two.
- **Expiry is checked once per iteration**, so it can be applied up to one re-check delay late (tripwire `NOTE:` at `OfflineQueue.expireDue`).

## Tests added — `packages/db-core/test/offline-queue.spec.ts`

Each over `Tree` on `TestTransactor`; the reachability gate is a switch; the doubles only decide when an answer comes back or how much lands.

- **Lifecycle, happy path** — two entries over one tree: `queued` → `submitting` → `committed` once each, E1 settled before E2 sent; each log entry under its own action id (read through the write-ahead hook) with `timestamp = queuedAt` from the injected clock; a fresh handle reads both rows; `enqueue` with nothing staged returns `undefined` and seals nothing; a sibling with an empty open segment is left out of the entry; final `queueState` and an emptied `entries()`.
- **Multi-collection entry** — one entry over two trees through `commitEntry`: one action id in both logs, both collections in the durability.
- **Gate closed** — nothing sent, head `waitingFor: 'cohort'`, state `waitingFor: 'cohort'`, the gate asked with `[header id, known log tail id]`; flipping it plus `wake()` drains; flipping it with no wake drains through the re-check timer.
- **Cascade three deep** — E1 `absent` insert, E2 `unchanged` on E1's image, E3 `unchanged` on E2's; a rival takes the key before the gate opens: E1 `constraint` (`TreeKeyTakenError`, `changedCollections` naming the tree, no `causedBy`), E2 `concurrent-modification` caused by E1, E3 caused by E2, in queue order; nothing sent (the opening refresh found the refusal); the local read shows the rival's row; the queue drains empty.
- **Refusal found by a reader before the drain** — gate closed, rival commits, `tree.update()` reports the refusal: the entry fails `constraint` with nothing sent and the gate never opened.
- **Transient classes** — `RefusesPendsAsShortfall`: `queued`, `waitingFor: 'cohort'`, `inDoubt: true`, re-driven after the switch and committed, `unclassifiedFailures` 0; a double throwing a plain `Error` from `commit`: `unclassifiedFailures` 1, both pends under the same action id, then committed.
- **Crash-free in-doubt resolution** — `TailLandsButReportsStale` with `maxAttempts: 1`: the first drive leaves the entry `queued`, `inDoubt`, `waitingFor: 'backoff'`, `lastError` a `SyncRetryExhaustedError`; `discard` throws `EntryNotDiscardableError`; a passed `expireAfterMs` logs `offline:expiry-deferred` and fails nothing (gate closed to observe it); the next drive resumes the one recorded attempt, builds no new one, lands whole once; the log holds the entry once and a fresh handle reads the row.
- **`discard` racing the drain** — commit parked: `discard` of the head throws; `discard` of the second entry succeeds, its `failed` carries `reason: 'discarded'` and `changedCollections`, its segment is gone while the head's is still in flight; the head then commits.
- **A local commit during the drain** — second entry sealed while the first's commit is parked: its `queued` status says `waitingFor: 'earlier-entry'`, state shows `depth: 2, draining: true`; both commit in order.
- **Disposal while an attempt is in flight** — `dispose()` does not resolve while the commit is parked; after the gate opens, `committed` was emitted, `whenDrained` rejected with `QueueDisposedError`, the second entry was never submitted and is still sealed, `enqueue` throws `QueueDisposedError`, a later `whenDrained` rejects, `dispose` is idempotent.

Verified by inspection only, per the ticket: an unknown entry's refusal is ignored and logged; the callback never calls a latched method; listeners that throw are logged (`offline:listener-threw`); timers are unref'd and cleared on dispose.
