# `node.blobs` reads, fetches and probes across machines — review handoff

`node.blobs` is now a `NodeBlobService` (`packages/db-p2p/src/blob/node-blob-service.ts`) over the single-machine `LocalBlobService` and the node's `BlobFetcher`. The design is docs/blobs.md §The node's blob service, now rewritten as built.

## What was built

- **`LocalBlobService.readThrough(ref, options, missing)`**: `read` as before, except a piece not in the store is asked of `missing: MissingPiece` instead of failing. The source stays `verified: true` (a fetched piece was checked by the fetcher before storing, so it is hashed once). `read` is now `readThrough` with a `missing` that throws `BlobUnavailableError` (`not-here` for `from: 'here'`, else `cohort-unreachable`) — unchanged standalone behaviour. `read` is no longer an `async *` method itself; it returns `readThrough`'s generator, so the body still runs lazily on first `next()`.
- **`NodeBlobService`**:
  - `put`, `pin`, `unpin`, `announce`, `onDurability` delegate to `local`.
  - `read`: `from: 'here'` → `local.read`. Otherwise `local.readThrough` whose `missing` starts ONE foreground session at the first miss (synchronously, so later misses reuse it) with the read's `offset`/`length`/`waitMs` and a `readController` signal chained to the caller's; awaits `session.stored(id)` then reads the piece from the store; a piece absent after `stored` resolved throws a plain `Error` (invariant violation).
  - `fetch`: `startSession(...).done`, whole blob, foreground.
  - `stat`: without `probe`, `local.stat`. With `probe`, only from a local `unknown`: `blobHolderCandidates(holders, ref)`, every peer asked `client.stat` in parallel, each under its own `AbortController` aborted after `probeTimeoutMs` (`armUnrefTimer`, cancelled on settle) or when the probe settles; first `whole`/`partial` → `{ ...here, locality: 'elsewhere' }`. Logs `stat:probe` once per probe that asked anyone.
  - `onProgress`: per-reference `ProgressCount` (sessions, pieces, bytes, size) kept only for sessions started through the private `startSession`; deleted when the reference's last session settles. Counting runs on a per-reference promise chain so a size read never reorders events.
  - `wireBytes()` → `client.wireBytes()`; `readonly local`.
- **Wiring**: `wireBlobFetcher` builds one `BlobHolderDeps` and returns `{ client, fetcher, holders }`. `blobs.start()` + its stop wrapper and `wireBlobFetcher` moved ahead of the `shared` attachments (same relative order: the fetcher's stop still runs before the local service's). `_blobTransfer` deleted. The blob protocol service still answers from the `LocalBlobService`, so a peer's `stat` never starts a probe of its own.

## Deviations from the implement ticket, with reasons

- **A read that consumed its whole range does NOT abort its session.** The ticket said the read's `finally` always aborts. Measured with `DEBUG`: the read routinely finishes before its session's confirming walk, so an unconditional abort logged `fetch:failed err=blob read ended` after every successful read and skipped `markWhole` (the record stayed `whole: false` until the next `stat` corrected it). Only a read that throws or whose consumer stops early aborts now. A completed read has, by construction, every piece of the range here, so the session has nothing left to fetch. Comment at the site.
- **Progress `size` is looked up at the first counted event, not only at the root's event.** `readBlobSize` returns `undefined` while the root is absent and the root is always the first piece a session stores when it lacks it, so this costs at most one extra root `get`; in exchange a range read of a blob whose root was already here reports `size` too (the ticket's rule would never have reported it there). A lookup that throws sets `sizeFailed` and is not retried for that entry (`progress:size-failed`).
- **Extra log line `stat:probe-peer-failed`** (`ref`, `peer`, `err`) for a peer that failed or timed out before the probe settled — the project rule against swallowing exceptions without logging. Peers aborted because the probe settled are not logged (they show as `client:aborted err=blob stat probe settled` under `blob:protocol` when their stream was open).
- **docs/blobs.md "Open: replacing a corrupt piece"** said replacement was "for the transfer work"; it now says it is not built (the fetcher brings only pieces this node lacks). No ticket filed; the doc's "Open:" paragraph is its record.

## Tests added

- `packages/db-p2p/test/blob-network-read.spec.ts` (two real nodes, loopback TCP, `clusterSize: 2`):
  - *reads a blob held by another machine, finding it by probe and reporting progress* — pins: `stat` `unknown` → probe `elsewhere`; a read across machines yields the input bytes; `onProgress` reports 4 pieces, final `bytesFetched` = sum of the four pieces' sizes, `size` = 600 KiB; afterwards `stat` is `here`.
  - *keeps a read with waitMs alive until a holder has the blob* — pins: no `waitMs` → `BlobUnavailableError` `not-held`; with `waitMs: 10_000` the read completes once A puts the content 300 ms later.
- `test/support/blob-mesh.ts`: `streamOf`, `concat`, `localTcpAddr`, `until`, moved out of `blob-fetcher.spec.ts` (now imported by both).

## Known gaps / things to look at

- **Probe timeout path, early-stop abort, node stop mid-read, and listener-throw isolation are inspection only** (per the ticket's test plan). A reviewer may want a unit test of `askAll`'s settle logic with a stub client if it looks fragile.
- **Progress emission is one microtask behind the store** (the per-reference chain). The spec waits with `until(events.length === 4)` for that reason. A consumer that needs the event before the read's last chunk is yielded would not get that guarantee.
- **`storedPiece`'s invariant error has a theoretical window** when the session found nothing missing (another session or a put stored the piece between the read's miss and the session's plan) and so took no hold: an orphan piece (no record) could be swept between `stored` resolving and the `get`. Pieces from another session sit under its `cached` hold and a put's under its pin, so this needs a crashed put's orphan read at that instant. Not parked as a `NOTE:`; judge whether it deserves one.
- `NodeBlobService` subscribes to `fetcher.onPieceStored` in its constructor and never unsubscribes (the fetcher and the service live as long as the node); comment at the site.
- Measuring transfer speed and wire size is the follow-on `blob-transfer-is-measured`; `wireBytes()` is its hook.
