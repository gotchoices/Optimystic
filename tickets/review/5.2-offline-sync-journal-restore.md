Stage 5 of [docs/offline-sync.md §Implementation stages](../../docs/offline-sync.md#implementation-stages), part three of three: restoring journaled offline transactions after a restart. The design as built is in [docs/offline-sync.md §Restore after a restart](../../docs/offline-sync.md#restore-after-a-restart); this handoff says what was built, where it departs from the implement ticket, what each test pins, and what the reviewer should look at hardest.

## What was built

**The pass** (`OfflineRestore` in `packages/quereus-plugin-optimystic/src/optimystic-adapter/offline-restore.ts`, owned by `OfflineMode`). One pass lists the label's entry, drive and outcome records once (`OfflineJournal.scan`), loads the outcomes into the retained map (a record past the retention window is deleted), reserves every listed sequence number on the queue, and walks the entry records in queue order: a record whose transaction has an outcome record is settled (records deleted, nothing emitted); one the queue already holds is skipped; one of an unknown version throws `OfflineJournalError` out of the pass with every record from it on untouched; otherwise each table it names is initialized through `OptimysticModule.collectionsOfTable` and the entry is put back through `OfflineQueue.restore`. The `stage` callback answers `already-committed` when the collection holds the drive record's attempt (`Collection.holdsCommitted`) or the drive record lists the collection as committed, else inserts the segment ahead of the first later queued entry still sealed in that collection (`Collection.restoreSealed`). A table missing from the engine's catalog, a `BlockUnavailableError` anywhere along the cause chain of initialization or of the replay (the queue rolls back), or a record naming a collection the table no longer backs **defers** the record; the pass stops there and lists every later record as deferred too (reason `behind-deferred`). Passes run one at a time; a pass requested while one runs starts after it, and later requests join that pending one. A pass refuses to run while a transaction is open.

**Retry triggers**: every `plugin.offline.restore()`; `OfflineMode.tableReadable`, called by the vtab after a full initialization (`doInitialize`, after `publishOfflinePolicy`) and after a live refresh that reached the cohort (`reachedCohort`), which schedules a pass once `TransactionBridge.whenTransactionIdle` resolves when a deferred record other than the one being attempted names the table; and the controller's reachability gate, which while anything is deferred starts a pass and answers `false`.

**The controller** (`OfflineMode`): `restore()`, `tableReadable`, `attachTableResolver` (the module, attached by `register`); `entries()` lists deferred records after the queue's as `queued` with `waitingFor: 'restore'`, `status()` answers them, `discard()` of a deferred record not in doubt writes its outcome through the journal's `settled` and emits `failed`/`discarded` with empty `changedTables`, one in doubt is refused; `whenDrained()` waits for an empty queue and no deferred record and no pass or discard in progress; `hasQueuedEntries()` counts deferred and discarding records; `assertNoQueuedEntries` refuses DDL on a table a deferred record names; `degradesLiveReads` answers `false` for such a table; `dispose` awaits a running pass (the pass finishes its current record and stops). `plugin.hydrate` calls `offline.restore()` after `hydrateCatalog`.

**Smaller pieces**: `OfflineJournal.adopt`, `scan`, `deleteRecords` and the three record decoders; `OfflineJournalError` exported from both entries; `waitingFor: EntryWaitingFor | 'restore' | undefined` on the plugin's `queued` status; `TransactionBridge.whenTransactionIdle`; `OptimysticModule.collectionsOfTable`; db-core `OfflineQueue.reserveSeqThrough`; `Mesh.restart(node, { rawStorage })`, `MeshOptions.durabilityWindowFor`, and the mesh transactors resolving a node's coordinator per call so they follow a restart.

## Departures from the implement ticket, each deliberate

- **The gate starts a pass instead of awaiting it.** The queue asks the gate inside a drain iteration, and `OfflineQueue.restore` waits for the loop to park between iterations, so a gate that awaited the pass would deadlock. The gate answers `false` while anything is deferred; the restored entry wakes the loop and the next gate ask sees nothing deferred.
- **Sequence numbers of deferred records are reserved on the queue** (`OfflineQueue.reserveSeqThrough`, new in db-core). Without it a fresh queue hands a transaction queued after the restart the number a deferred record holds, and `OfflineQueue.restore` then refuses the record for good. Reserved from the record keys, so a record of an unknown version reserves its number too.
- **A fourth deferral reason, `behind-deferred`**, for the records after the one the pass stopped at; they are listed and counted like the stopping one.
- **A discarded deferred record gets an outcome record**, through `OfflineJournal.settled` after `adopt`, so `status()` answers it afterwards and across a restart, as for a queued entry's discard. The ticket said only "deletes its records and emits failed"; `settled` does both.
- **`whenDrained` also waits out a running pass or discard**, not only the deferred set, since a pass about to register an entry would otherwise let it resolve early.
- **`TransactionBridge.whenTransactionIdle`** was added for the vtab trigger: a pass scheduled from inside a statement waits for the statement's transaction to end rather than skipping.
- **The mesh harness gained `durabilityWindowFor`** (threaded to every member and coordinator) because every apply on the harness was strict, so a node store over `MemoryBatchBackend({ deferSync: true })` could never lose a commit; and **`repoAsReachedBy` resolves `node.coordinatorRepo` per call**, since every mesh transactor built before a `Mesh.restart` otherwise kept the inert old coordinator.

## Tests (`packages/quereus-plugin-optimystic/test/offline-restore.spec.ts`, 12 cases)

- Crash after the network commit, before the journal delete, node store synced: `committed` emitted once inside `hydrate`, holders unknown, `followed: false`, no pend sent after the restart, row on both nodes, one log entry under the recorded action id on B, index agrees with scan, records replaced by the outcome.
- Same crash, node store lost the commit with the power (`MemoryBatchBackend.powerLoss`, `Mesh.restart` over a fresh driver): `committed` once, one log entry, row on both nodes.
- Crash after the local acknowledgement, before any send: `queued` emitted before `hydrate` resolved, listed `persisted`, not in doubt, the row served locally on A with the gate closed and B severed, drains on reconnect.
- Crash with the send held at B's cluster round (`MeshFailureConfig.onClusterDelivery`): the drive record is on disk, the restored entry is in doubt with one attempt, `discard` refuses it, it drains once under the recorded action id.
- An entry record of version 2 behind a valid one: `hydrate` rejects with `OfflineJournalError` (`unknown-version`, key, version), the valid record is restored, both records stay in the store.
- Two `Database`s over one store under two labels restore only their own.
- Table dropped by B while A was down: deferred (`waitingFor: 'restore'`, `tables`, counts as queued, no status emitted); `discard` fails it with `discarded` and empty `changedTables`; `whenDrained` resolves; records replaced by the outcome.
- Table unreadable at restore (`Isolation` armed for the table's blocks): deferred; a read fails with `cohort-unreachable` in offline mode; after disarming, the first read reaches the cohort and puts the entry back; it drains to B.
- A new offline commit made while a record is deferred waits (`waitingFor: 'cohort'`, nothing submitted); after the record restores, both drain in sequence order and the restored entry's `seq` is lower.
- Restored entry refused by a guard (B took the key): fails with `constraint` as a `ConstraintError`, A reads B's row, records replaced by the outcome, `status` answers the retained outcome.
- Entry record left beside its outcome record: skipped, records deleted, outcome listed once, no status emitted.
- `restore()` inside an open transaction throws and changes nothing.

No new test for `reserveSeqThrough` on its own (pinned through the "new offline commit waits behind a deferred record" case, which needs it), nor for the harness changes (pinned by the power-loss case and by db-p2p's existing restart specs).

## Known gaps and what to look at

- **The "node store lost the commit" variant most likely takes the already-committed path**, not the "stale replica re-staged in doubt" path the ticket describes: on the two-node mesh A's rebuilt coordinator read-repairs from B on the first read, so `holdsCommitted` answers `true`. The assertions are on the log and the row, per the ticket, so both paths pass, but the re-stage-then-consume path (`completeOwnEntry` from the drain's opening refresh, or the verbatim re-send of `resumedAttempt`) is exercised only by the "send held at the cohort" case, which re-sends the recorded attempt. A deterministic way to serve A its stale replica would need `selfFirstReads` plus a suppressed consult; not attempted.
- **`Isolation` intercepts at the transactor**, so "a node that never held the table's blocks" is simulated, not real; the deferral decision is the same `BlockUnavailableError` either way.
- **Not tested, by inspection as the ticket allowed**: a record naming a collection its table no longer backs (`collection-mismatch`); `dispose` during a pass; the `offline:restore-stuck` line; a pass that throws out of a table's initialization for a reason other than unavailability (it propagates out of `restore()` with the deferred map left as the previous pass left it, see `OfflineRestore.pass`).
- **A transaction that begins during a pass** is host misuse (`hydrate` runs before statements), but a gate-triggered pass can coincide with one; `restoreSealed` then throws a plain error out of the pass, logged `offline:restore-pass-failed`, and the next trigger retries. Worth a reviewer's eye on whether that should defer instead.
- **The deferred-discard race**: `discard` removes the record synchronously and settles on the restore chain, and a pass skips ids in `discarding`; a pass already walking a scan taken before the discard still skips them, since the check is per record. Check `OfflineRestore.walk` and `discard` together.
- **Tripwires recorded as `NOTE:`**: one pass per queue re-check while anything is deferred (`OfflineMode.reachability`); the in-doubt dropped-table record that can neither be put back nor discarded (`OfflineRestore.discard`, accepted tradeoff with its revisit condition).
- **Hydrated-table initialization through `collectionsOfTable`** mirrors `resolveConnectedTable`'s live path (initialize, then `ensureConnectionRegistered` for a fresh instance). Confirm nothing else that path does is skipped.
