# A wide-mesh watch test sometimes sees no notification wake — review handoff

Test and docs only; no product code changed. The diagnosis (the watcher can store the collection's leaf, so its local feed wakes it at once; the first commit after a registration is often unannounced) is in the git history of the fix/implement ticket.

## What changed

- **Attempt loop waits for wake and frame together**: `wakes > wakesBeforeCommit && delivered > deliveredBeforeCommit`, then counts when `reads.started === readsBeforeCommit` — same shape as the sibling "every machine in every cohort" describe. A lost notification now costs its attempt the 10 s timeout instead of ending it instantly on the local-storage wake.
- **Replication wait uses the forwarder's lookup**: `writerHost.registry.findByCoord(reactivityRootCoord(reactivityTailBytes(tail!.tailId)))` with `engine.rootPlaced`, matching `directSubscribers` in `packages/db-p2p/src/libp2p-node-base.ts` (which passes the tail bytes to `reactivityRootCoord` the same way). Replaces `findServing(topicId, 0)`, which could disagree.
- **Attempts 4 → 6**: a lost notification spends two attempts (its timeout, then the next one disqualified by the gap-recovery tail read). Worst case adds ~20 s inside the 480 s describe timeout.
- **Comments**: describe header reworded (the old "passes only on a wake that a delivered notification accounts for" sentence is gone); loop comments rewritten to say why both wake and frame are awaited and why a loss costs two attempts.
- **Docs**: new paragraph after "A commit right after a registration can reach nobody" in `docs/reactivity.md`: the announcing member drops its own notification as `untrusted` until the newly formed root group publishes its membership certificate on the next gossip-cadence pump (`publishMembership`); recovery by gap recovery or tick; measured 8/16 first-commit losses, next commit 3–13 s later announced, no tighter bound; tripwire: publish the certificate when a root-placed engine forms. `yarn lint:docs` passes.

## Validation

- Whole file (`OPTIMYSTIC_INTEGRATION=1 node --import ./register.mjs node_modules/mocha/bin/mocha.js test/substrate-real-libp2p.integration.spec.ts --reporter dot`), 5 runs: **5/5 — 14 passing, 2 pending** each (the 2 pending are the pre-existing unimplemented/mock-tier placeholders, not the cases touched). No tests added; the change is to an existing test's synchronisation.
