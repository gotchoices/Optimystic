# A relay reservation the relay dropped is never renewed — review

Source: GitHub sereus#64. The supervisor judged "held" only from the node's own advertised addresses, so a relay that forgot the reservation while the connection stayed open left the node unreachable (every relayed dial answered `NO_RESERVATION`) until the connection happened to drop.

## What was built

**Seam.** `RelayReservationStoreLike` gains `getReservation` (a public method on libp2p's store class); `isReservationStore` requires it, so an upgrade that drops it makes `findCircuitRelayTransport` return `null` loudly. `forceReservationRenewal(store, relayPeerId)` reads the held reservation, sets its `expire` to `0n` so libp2p's refresh-window check sends a fresh RESERVE over the open connection, and calls `addRelay(relay, 'discovered')`. It never throws: `null` on success, otherwise a reason, including "the store holds no reservation" (in which case nothing is requested). The `NOTE:` at `findCircuitRelayTransport` records why a plain `addRelay` cannot reach the relay, what zeroing `expire` does and does not touch, and why renewing as `'discovered'` is right while every supervised reservation is `discovered`.

**Supervisor.** New option `renewMs` (default `DEFAULT_RELAY_RENEW_MS` = 5 min, clamped ≥ 1, each interval jittered ±`RELAY_RENEW_JITTER` = 10%). The renewal clock (`nextRenewAtMs`) is cleared whenever a drive starts, armed in `onHeld` when unset (so it starts when a drive lands or a tick first finds the reservation held), and re-armed after every renewal attempt, success or not. In `tick`, held and due → `renewOnce` (serialized through `inFlight`, raced against `driveTimeoutMs` via the new shared `raceDeadline`, which `requestReservation` now uses too, and discarded on `stop()`). After it: held → `onHeld`; not held (relay refused, libp2p withdrew the address) → drive at once, then the usual tail. `onHeld` schedules `min(checkMs, nextRenewAtMs − now)`. Logs: `relay-reservation:renewed` (debug), `relay-reservation:renew-failed reason=…` (error). A trigger that lands between the renewal finishing and the tick resuming owns the loop (`if (this.stopped || this.inFlight) return`), which prevents two concurrent drives.

**Node option.** `NodeOptions.relayReservation?: RelayReservationSupervisorOptions`, passed to `superviseRelayReservations` in `createLibp2pNodeBase`. Readme, `docs/optimystic.md`, `docs/architecture.md` and the `relay-reservation` row of `docs/debugging.md` mention the renewal and its interval.

**Backlog arm.** `debt-relay-supervisor-can-keep-the-configured-listen-address` gained one bullet: if the supervisor ever switches to `'configured'` reservations, the renewal must renew as `'configured'` too (a `'discovered'` refresh over a `configured` entry takes a pending slot id, or throws `HadEnoughRelaysError` and removes the reservation).

## Tests added

- `relay-reservation-seam.spec.ts`, first case: also asserts `getReservation` exists on the store.
- `relay-reservation-seam.spec.ts`, "a relay that forgot a held reservation is reached by forceReservationRenewal, never by a plain addRelay": pins the libp2p fact the fix rests on. The relay deletes the client from `relay.services.relay.reservations`; a plain `addRelay` leaves the relay without it; `forceReservationRenewal` returns `null` and the relay holds it again; no `self:peer:update` without the circuit address fires during or 250 ms after the renewal. If a libp2p upgrade changes the refresh rule, this fails.
- `relay-reservation-supervisor.spec.ts`, group 2, "renews the reservation after the relay forgets it while the connection stays open, without withdrawing the address": the regression. Supervisor at `renewMs` 2 s; the relay forgets right after the first drive; a dial-only third node dials the client's circuit address every ~200 ms (hanging up after a success). Asserts the first dial fails with `NO_RESERVATION`, a later one succeeds within `renewMs × 1.1 + 3 s`, and the client advertised its circuit address at every sample. Observed: reachable again ~1.9 s after the relay forgot. Verified to fail with `renewMs` pushed to 10 min (25 dials, all `NO_RESERVATION`).

`relay-reservation-refresh.integration.spec.ts` lost its local `StoreWithReservations` cast now that the interface carries `getReservation`; no behavior change. `relayServiceOf` in `test/util/relay-topology.ts` is the cast to the relay's `CircuitRelayService`.

## Known gaps and things to look at

- The refresh integration spec runs at the default 5 min `renewMs`, so no run overlaps a forced renewal with libp2p's own timer-driven refresh. By reading the store: a second `addRelay` for the same relay joins the queued job (`reserveQueue.find(peerId)` → `join`), and a refresh replaces the entry and clears the old timer, so the overlap should be benign. Not exercised.
- A renewal that hits its deadline leaves libp2p's `addRelay` running; the address stays advertised meanwhile, and if that refresh later fails libp2p withdraws the address, whose `self:peer:update` wakes a drive. Reasoned, not tested.
- The jitter uses `Math.random()`; tests rely only on the ±10% bound.
- Why a relay forgets a reservation is still unknown (one candidate is `handleReserve`'s `catch` on the relay); out of scope here. Deleting from the server's map also leaves the old reservation's expiry signal armed on the relay, which at its original expiry would delete the renewed reservation; that is the relay's behavior in the test setup only, and the next renewal repairs it anyway.
- Cost: one RESERVE stream per supervised relay every ~5 min per node. Default chosen to match Sereus's interim patch, which fixed their stuck "Joining" case.
