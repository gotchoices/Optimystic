# Escrow for reservation-required invariants

Item 3 of [docs/partition-healing.md](../../docs/partition-healing.md) §Subset That Lands on Today's Strict-CP Optimystic, and the one piece of the partition-healing design that uses today's strong-consensus machinery rather than replacing it.

## The idea

Some invariants cannot be compensated after a merge: a hard capacity, a balance that must not go negative where an overdraft reversal is unacceptable, a payment a contract forbids reversing. For these the honest answer is that local progress is not permitted, and the primitive is a **reservation**: at BEGIN the transaction acquires a reservation on the protected resource through the log-tail cohort's quorum (the strong lane), the reservation is itself a logged entry, reservations aggregate as a bounded counter the cohort verifies, and the actual change is a later transaction that consumes the reservation by id. A partitioned minority cannot reserve and so cannot spend; everything else in the collection keeps merging. This is the per-invariant stance the design recommends, and the I-confluence table in the doc says which invariant shapes need it.

## Questions this pass must settle

- The shape of a reservation entry in the log and of the counter it aggregates into; whether it is a guard kind (`TreeEntryGuard` already asserts a condition at commit and is re-decided on replay) or a new entry type.
- The doc's open question: a reservation is acquired under strong consensus, the consuming transaction is in the replay lane; if the consumer is preempted by a lower-HLC transaction, the reservation names the intended transaction id and only that transaction may consume it. Design the expiry of an unconsumed reservation.
- How SQL declares it: a tag on a column or constraint, compiled to a reservation at BEGIN and a consumption in the statement.
- Whether a reservation can span collections (the doc's cross-collection reconcile question).

## Edge cases & interactions

- A reservation held by a machine that never returns: expiry by the cohort, with the released amount visible as a logged entry.
- A reservation acquired on a `strong` collection for a resource in a `replay-permitted` one: the counter lives with the resource; decide which cohort verifies it.
- Two reservations racing at the quorum: today's race resolution decides, and the loser is refused at pend as today.

## Tests that pay for themselves

One test that two concurrent reservations against one budget cannot both be granted, on the in-process mesh. One that a consumption naming a reservation another transaction holds is refused.
