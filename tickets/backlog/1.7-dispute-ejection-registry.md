# Durable, term-limited ejection

## Why

Ejection is what makes a dispute pay for itself (`docs/right-is-right.md` §Economic Model): after a ruling the losing side stops voting on and storing that ring segment, so the next transaction there is fast. Today a ruling only adds reputation penalties, which are in-memory, decay, are bounded to 1024 records, and are not consulted by cohort assembly (`findCluster`) at all. The design of record specifies a durable, ruling-backed, term-limited ejection that cohort assembly honors. This ticket builds the registry and the places that honor it; `dispute-ejection-propagation` makes sure the nodes that need a ruling get it.

## Specification

- **`EjectionRegistry`** (db-p2p): records `{ peerId, rulingDigest, disputeId, termStart, termEnd }` per ejected peer, plus the ruling itself (so this node can prove the ejection to others). Persisted through the node's `IKVStore` under its own key prefix (`ejection/…`), loaded on node start, so ejections survive restart. One active record per peer: a later ejection of an ejected peer extends to the later `termEnd`.
- **Source**: the dispute service's `onRuling` sink. A ruling is recorded only after the ruling verifier accepts it (never on a local computation path that skipped verification). Losers = every participant whose signed vote opposed the ruling — the record's promise voters on the losing side, the coordinator when approvers lose (its record endorsed the transaction), and every arbitrator in any round whose vote opposed. Inconclusive and silent participants are not ejected.
- **Term policy** (configurable; these defaults are the design's): first term 24 h; a peer ejected again within 7 days of its previous term's end gets twice its previous term, capped at 30 days. The repeat history is kept in the same persisted record after a term ends (a lapsed record keeps `lastTermMs` and `lastTermEnd`, and is pruned once the repeat window has passed).
- **Honored** while a term is active:
  - cohort assembly — `findCluster` leaves the peer out of every cohort it assembles; the cohort is filled by the next eligible peer as for any absent peer;
  - coordinator selection — `isSelectable` treats the peer as excluded;
  - consensus — a coordinator never includes it in a record's peers (follows from `findCluster`); a member's admission gate derives its view through the same `findCluster`, so it agrees (by inspection).
- **Rejoin**: when `termEnd` passes the peer is eligible again with no further action; its reputation penalties keep decaying on their own clock.
- **Self**: a node that verifies a ruling naming itself records its own ejection too, and its key network leaves itself out of the cohorts it assembles for the term — otherwise this node's view disagrees with every other node's, and its admission gate refuses records the rest of the network considers correct. Log it at error level once per ruling: it means this node's engine judged a transaction wrongly.

## Edge cases & interactions

- **Forged rulings** never reach the registry (verification at the sink) — test this; ejection of honest peers is the attack the anchoring gate exists for.
- **Cohort shrink below quorum**: excluding ejected peers can leave fewer peers than `clusterSize` for a block; that is the ordinary short-cohort path (admission floor, `allowClusterDownsize`). Do not special-case it, but log `ejection:cohort-short` when an assembly drops an ejected peer and ends below `clusterSize`, so an operator can tell ejection from churn.
- **Data the ejected peer held**: rebalance's `grown` arm (`RebalanceMonitor`) copies blocks to the peers that replace it (by inspection — the arm keys on `findCluster`). The ejected peer's own copies are not deleted; it simply stops counting.
- **Partition / disagreement between nodes**: two nodes with different registries assemble different cohorts and an admission gate refuses; resolving that is `dispute-ejection-propagation`. Here: the registry exposes `rulingsFor(peerIds)` for that ticket to serve.
- **Clock**: terms are judged by the local clock; skew shifts term ends by the skew, which is harmless at hour granularity (by inspection; mention at the term check).
- **Self-report guard**: the reputation service refuses reports about this node (`refuseSelfReport`); the registry is deliberately different (see *Self* above). Note the contrast in a comment so nobody "fixes" one to match the other.
- **Growth bound**: the registry grows by losers per ruling, bounded by the round cap (at most 15 × K arbitrators plus K cohort members per dispute) and pruned after the repeat window. If ruling volume ever makes it large, cap it like the reputation table — add a `NOTE:` tripwire at the store.

## Tests

- Term policy: first term, doubled repeat inside the window, cap, fresh term after the window (pure function, real branching).
- A ruling that fails verification records nothing.
- An ejected peer is absent from `findCluster`'s cohort until its term ends (key-network spec with a registry stub is acceptable only if the registry is injected through a port the key network owns; otherwise use the real registry over the in-memory `IKVStore`).
